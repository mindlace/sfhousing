## Per check

1. **Coverage.** I traced each unit's line in `sec-subsystems`, the rules of G1 to G5, S1 to S3 and X1, `sec-model-loop`, `-level1`, `-locks`, `-bar`, `-saves` and `-accounts`, and every `tbl-traceability` row that names these units, to a spec section and its tests. Every rule has an owner. There are two gaps: the level 5 "returns the clock to today" trigger (F4) and the `rows.ts` item that `funding-measures` hands to `game-sim` (F13). No rule is claimed by two specs. `dateMeasure`'s calls belong to `game-sim` and its body to `funding-measures`, and both specs say so. The `preset` set, clear and parse rules are `game-sim`'s, and both `game-core` and `funding-measures` defer to it.
2. **Contradictions.** These are F2, F3, F7, F9, F10, F11, F12, F15 and F22. Checked and clean: the storage keys (`sfhousing:saves:v1`, `sfhousing:save:<id>`, `sfhousing:save:corrupt:*`, `sfhousing:saves:revs`, `sfhousing:e2e:*`); HTTP statuses (S1 and the `game-save` routes agree); level numbers and `unlocksAt`; the win titles; the outcome messages; "Unlocks when you level up", "Unlocks at level N" and "Unlocks in a later round"; "Unlock the simulation to remix", "Level 4 runs all of it" and "Parts unlock in a later round"; and the default names. The test ids `closing` binds to (`win-card`, `paint-win`, `header-level`, `header-level-panel`, `header-level-up`, `level-panel`, `sim-locked`, `tl-win`, `tl-to-beat`) each exist in the owning spec.
3. **Signatures.** Every use of G2 in the four specs' own sections matches the design: `allowed`, `open`, `offer`, `setOffer`, `levelUp()` with no arguments, `touched()`, `sync`, `openGame()`, `remix({ kind, record, title })`, `read`, `adopt`. None of the remnants listed in the brief appears: no `canLevelUp`, `HeaderLevel`, `opts.level`, `seedDefault`, `syncState`, `remix({ text })` or sync context, and no `allowed === ALL_ALLOWED` test used to mean the open game. `contracts` declares only G-row shapes, with no `Bound`. `WinCardProps` and the prop-free `LevelPanel`/`AccountOffer` match G5 and S3.
4. **Placeholders.** None found: no TBD, TODO, "as appropriate", question callouts or "awaiting". "Cast as needed" in a contracts test is harmless.
5. **Readability.** Every code block carries `data-lang` on `<code>`. The step references in `game-core` construction and the `game-save` PUT steps point at real `<ol>` items. Past-tense wording: F20.
6. **Fidelity.** I checked R0 to R7 and D1 to D14 against the ledger's words. All are carried as said, apart from the dropped word in D12 (F25). D5 is carried exactly: `/s/<id>` runs with `game={null}`, a remix runs `playable`, and Publish sends `run`. D2 holds: the offer is set only while `won`, cleared on any edit and on unmount, and kept in memory only. D7 holds: `changed` sends nothing before `firstWinAt`, and the PUT answers 422 `no_win`. D8 and D14 hold, including the name rules and the 12. R5 holds through the "Keep playing" and kept-month rules. One narrowing is F24.
7. **Delegation.** These are F4, F16 and F17. Everything else visible outside a module is decided: the panel's keyboard behaviour, the remix record's shape, the sync's state order, the account offer's texts and its failure texts, the locked page's text and test id, and the merged flag's 30-month rule.
8. **Seams.** The until-mounted rule reads the same in `game-core`, `game-sim` and `game-shell`. The server provides `openGame()`. `SiteHeader` renders today's steps with no level cell, `SimRoute` in edit mode renders an empty `#map-region`, and Paint and the banner render no game part until mounted. `/s/<id>` renders at once with `game={null}`. The open game: no level cell, card, flag, lane or offer anywhere, except for Remix (F9). Parallel write sets: `game-sim` and `game-shell` share no file. `game-sim` with `funding-measures` and `pool-closing` share no file (`copy.ts` and `pool-level.ts` are carved out, and `pool-closing`'s "ran out" line is in `src/lib/fzp`). `game-save` touches only its own two `src/lib/game` files. Test dependencies across units: F1, F2, F5, F6 and F8. The level-up flow end to end: Paint and `/sim` set the offer, and the card, the panel and the header button call `levelUp()`. `/sim` reacts to the level rising and Paint navigates. The one weak step is level 5 (F4).
