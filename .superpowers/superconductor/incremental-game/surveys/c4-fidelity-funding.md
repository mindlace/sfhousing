## Per check

**1. Coverage.** I compared each of the four units' lines in `sec-subsystems`, their interface rows (L1, F1, F2, F3, M1) and the `tbl-traceability` rows that name them against the specs' own design and test sections. L1's rules are carried, every one of them: the manifest parse, the byte-copy basemap, the catalog through `FREEWAY_LAND_JSON`, the `parcels.bin` proof, the commands, `regimes` and `reservedFrom`, and the `PATHS` walk test. F1 and F2 are carried except for V25 (M1) and the old-dataset case (B1). F3 is fully determined against the engine's real fields. The check runs in system 5 after `holdingReturns`. "Spent" uses `lastArrivalOf`, the money rows' `INSTR_LOAN`/`INSTR_GUARANTEE`, and `asksOf` against `availableOf`. Reopening goes through `beforeStep`. Closings are world columns, so rewind, restore and `keep` are covered, and the referee runs with `poolClosing: false` in `tolerance.referee.spec.ts` and `score/money.spec.ts`. Those two are the only funded cross-engine comparisons; `series.referee`, `referee` and `map-stream.equality` run with no money. M1's rules are split between `funding-measures` (the body) and `game-sim` (the calls); both specs say so consistently. One behaviour has no owner (M4).

**2. Contradictions.** I compared money texts across specs: `labelScaleApplied`, `summaryScaleText`, `scaleLines`, the range error, `COVERAGE_OFF_SHORT_SENTENCE4` and "ran out". They agree between `finite-funding` and `funding-measures`. The remaining text mismatches are m6 and m11, and F3 contradicts itself (m4). The dollar amounts and dates in `finite-funding` match the research note and the live file. I checked $260.31M = 107.54 + 152.77, $21.8M + $14.9M = $36.7M, 1,274 × $443,108 = $564,519,592, the City total of $234M, and the fixture's sums of $6.0M and 30 × $443,108. The fixture's anniversaries (2042-10-10 for the HTF, with `asOf` 2026-10-10) are right.

**3. Signatures.** `dateMeasure(policy, cal)`, `FundPools.closedDay`, `WorldOptions.poolClosing`, `MoneyView.closed`, `FREEWAY_LAND_JSON`, `WorkerMode`, the `Pool` and `Inflow` shapes and `levelSet` all match the design's interface sections. `PolicyTimeline` has exactly the four members that `closedTimeline` reproduces (`src/lib/sim/world/policy/frame.ts:46-54`). The one gap is `closedLines`'s `Money` typing (m7). None of the four specs carries a remnant of a removed interface.

**4. Placeholders.** None found. The impact-fee bound is decided by a rule plus a reported handback. "The helper's name and file are the operator's" (`remove-lesscars`) concerns an internal test helper only.

**5. Readability.** The code blocks carry `data-lang` on `code`. One paragraph is dense and rests on a false premise: `finite-funding` `sec-design-pool-types` on `parseCalibration` (B1). A few "in place of" sentences describe replaced wording (the `fundingWarning` text, the provenance method). They are tolerable.

**6. Fidelity.** I read R2, R4, D3, D4, D11 and D13 in the ledger and in the design. Their wording and their yes-to-a-proposal form match. Every bound traces to `funding-bounds.md` with a tier, and no bound has tier 4. The spec tells the builder to read each primary and to take the primary's value when it differs. Where the spec departs from or narrows the design: the state source's research instruction is dropped (M3), the impact-fee fallback is missing from the design (m2), the Charter for the HTF end is not read (m3), and the design counts the preset terms wrong (m1). D1: no spec conflicts with the reach note. `finite-funding` says live misses between its merge and `pool-closing`'s and asserts no live goal month. `closing` owns `scripts/level2-check.ts` and runs it before upload, after both units. `game-core` and `game-sim` both point to `closing`. The two invented ten-year terms are marked tier 4, "custom, not sourced". Every offered preset has an end and a tier in `tbl-presets`. The `dateMeasure` rules match M1, and the extra "content differs, return itself" guard is consistent with G3.

**7. Delegation.** Behaviour left to the builder that another unit can observe: the V25 rule (M1), the old-dataset behaviour (B1) and `closedLines`'s typing (m7). Everything else observable is decided.

**8. Seams.** `pool-closing` and `funding-measures` share no file. Their lists (`world/funding/**`, `world/{types,world}.ts`, `world/score/**`, `runner/ensemble.spec.ts`, `fzp/{money-view,MoneyResults}`, docs `world-funding` and `finance-score`, against `sim-page/funding-*`, `Funding*.svelte`, `ProgrammeEditor`, `PolicySummary`, `sim-page/test/**`, `pool-level.ts`, `copy.ts`, `e2e/sim-{funding,pools}`, `sim-funding.md`) are disjoint and agree with `tbl-write-sets`. `pool-closing` and `game-sim` share nothing either: Details' new lines are in `MoneyResults`, `chunk-log.ts` needs no edit, and `game-sim` writes no `sim/world` file. The test-file overlap with `game-sim` is m12. `remove-lesscars` shares no file with `remove-multiuser`, which runs in parallel; I checked `pages-a11y`, `targets`, `sim` and `sim-citywide` against its e2e list.
