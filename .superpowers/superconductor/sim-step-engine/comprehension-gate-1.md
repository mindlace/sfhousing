## Part 1. The system as built (from the code only)

### 1.1 The world, what is fixed per run, and one step

**State.** A `World` is `{ month, frame, lots, projects, line, pools }` (`types.ts:133-140`). `month` is the last model month stepped (0 at creation, `columns.ts:123-132`); `frame` is the id of the frame that month ran on (`world.ts:658-659`).

- `lots` (`types.ts:43-62`) has a row only for a lot that has left "free since the dataset date, attempt 0, never touched": a row is made by `ensureLot` (`columns.ts:240-263`) when a lot gets a holder, is blocked, is demolished or starts. Columns: state (HELD, FREE, LIVE, DONE; `types.ts:13-16`), attempt `k`, day it became free `r`, holder count, latest holder exit, `blocked`, `demolished`, live simulated row. `rowOf` maps lot to row + 1. No start day or running total is stored for any lot.
- `projects` (`types.ts:65-130`): one append-only row per pipeline project, master, phase or simulated start. It holds identity (`origin`, `src`, `sub`, `stream`), route, filed day, `certain`, revision rule, the stage clock (`stage`, `outcome`, `length`, `used`, `anchor`, `speed`, `leave`), the end, 7 entered days per row, size and caps, the size tables of a start (`level`, `env`, `top`, `yields`), the next phase filing of a master, funding columns (`kind`, `funded`, `applied`, `granted`, `lent`, `guaranteed`, `programmes` joined with ','), and two side tables: revisions (a linked list per row, `columns.ts:163-196`) and displacements (`columns.ts:199-214`).
- `line` and `pools` are `{ n: 0 }` until the funding system's `initFunding` replaces them (`columns.ts:129-130`; `funding/columns.ts:194-200`). The funding line holds one row per waiting application (`funding/columns.ts:12-59`), plus a spell table (a kind's respelled start day per lot) and a shadow table. Pools hold `received`, `committed`, `spentGrant`, `outLoan`, `lostLoan`, `lostGuarantee`, the day and month of the latest money, and one money row per (awarded project, paying programme) (`funding/columns.ts:61-85`). Pools and programmes are named by id strings in the world (`mPool`, `mProg`, `bundle`).

**Fixed per run.** `Statics` = dataset date, parcel ids (row i = row i of every view), parcel rank, pipeline projects, district max stories (`statics.ts:8-27`). The seed enters only as `seedHash(seed)` (`world.ts:219`; `src/lib/sim/scenario/rng.ts:45-47`). The `PolicyTimeline` gives one frame per month (`policy/frame.ts:46-54`). Pipeline rows, masters and phases always take their parcel rows, caps and phase rows from month 1's view (`world.ts:228`, `world.ts:131`; `pipeline.ts:176-177`).

**The due list** is outside the world (`due.ts:29-126`): per entity (lot ≥ 0, project = -1 - row) one month, bucketed by month; `take` returns ascending, stale entries filtered (`due.ts:78-94`); `put` past `through` forgets the entity (`due.ts:100-104`). `planDue` rebuilds it from the world: every live row at the month of its `leave`, every master at its next phase filing, every free lot at its ordinary start month (or the earlier funded application month), reading frames up to `world.month + 1` (`due.ts:152-171`, `due.ts:263-298`). A rowless lot whose `INIT` draw is above `skipBound` (its start falls after `through`) is not planned (`due.ts:284`; `rates.ts:146-190`). A rowless lot whose rate is 0 in every frame so far is skipped unless a funding system is in force (`due.ts:229-249`).

**`createWorld`** (`world.ts:115-139`): `createHolds` (`holds.ts:128-146`: a skipped project blocks its lots, a master holds its `all` rows, an unphased project holds its rows, demolition from history), then `createProjects` (`pipeline.ts:211-219`): each non-skipped project becomes a pipeline row (`pipeline.ts:232-261`, its current stage drawn given the days already spent, `survived = asOf + 1 - e`) or a master (`pipeline.ts:263-309`; one already entitled ends `entitled` at once and points at its first phase filing). Events for everything already true are sent. A lot freed at the dataset date throws (`world.ts:136`). With a funding system and month 1 finance, `f.create` gives the world its funding columns (`world.ts:137`).

**`step`** (`world.ts:545-660`) steps month `m = world.month + 1`, which must be `<= readyThrough()` (`world.ts:555-556`). The systems, in order:

1. **Policy** (`world.ts:561-564`), only when `frame.id !== world.frame`. Core part `policyChanged` (`world.ts:491-533`): free lots are replanned (dropped from the due list, ordinary start recomputed through this month, put at `plannedMonth(ordinary, planLot)`); which lots: the `touched(m)` rows, or every free lot when `touched` is null, the market shift changed, or a funding system is plugged in and this or last month's frame has `finance` (`world.ts:509-515`). Then every live row whose speed under the new frame differs is re-anchored on the month's first day and re-put (`world.ts:524-532`; `stage.ts:95-101`). Then `fund.policyChanged` (`funding/policy.ts:54-77`): every pool id of the frame gets a pool row; unless last month's frame is the one the world stepped and view and money identity are unchanged (`funding/policy.ts:58-60`), each waiting line row on a lot no longer free leaves, and each other is re-made under this frame or lapses (`funding/apply.ts:318-335`); lapsed lots are planned again (`funding/policy.ts:26-37`).
2. **Money in** (`world.ts:566`; `funding/pools.ts:44-56`): each `frame.finance.arrivals` entry adds to `received`, notes the money day, writes an `'in'` ledger event; 0 dollars skipped.
3. **Stages** (`world.ts:567-576`): the month is taken from the due list; project entities are followed in ascending row order (`world.ts:569-574`) by `followProject` (`pipeline.ts:419-432`): while alive and `leave <= lastDay`, `leaveStage` (`pipeline.ts:443-486`) exits (outcome ≠ 0), enters the next stage (a `revise` may follow, `stage.ts:119-156`; entering construction demolishes held lots, `pipeline.ts:467`), completes, or stops at `stopAt` (a master ends `entitled` and points at its first phase). A master files each phase whose day has come (`pipeline.ts:386-412`) and the phase row is followed in turn. Ends release holds (`pipeline.ts:311-325`; `holds.ts:63-121`) and push freed lots onto `x.freed`. While a funding system is in force each event is also passed to `onProjectEvent` (`world.ts:572`, tee at `world.ts:536-543`); `startProject` throws in this system (`world.ts:430-432`).
4. **Starts** (`world.ts:577-631`): the lots taken this month plus the lots system 3 freed, sorted by rank (`world.ts:595-596`). For each, the ordinary start is recomputed; one falling this month is set aside; any other is "later" (unfunded, that means the due list was out of step: throws, `world.ts:619`). Lots whose `planLot` answer is this month are passed to `fund.apply` in rank order (`world.ts:623-625`; `funding/apply.ts:231-260`). The "later" lots still free are planned again after `apply` (`world.ts:627-631`).
5. **The line** (`world.ts:635-637`): `fund.serve` (`funding/serve.ts:50-112`), events teed to the hook.
6. **Ordinary starts** (`world.ts:642-654`): each set-aside lot with no start made on it this month starts (`world.ts:282-404`); a start follows its chain to the month's end and plans lots that chain frees (`world.ts:262-276`, `world.ts:400-402`).
7. **Shadows** (`world.ts:657`; `funding/shadow.ts:50-84`), then the line is compacted (`funding/line-index.ts:123-131`).
Then `world.month` and `world.frame` are set (`world.ts:658-659`).

**A start** (`startRow`, `world.ts:282-404`): ordinary starts read route shares, Local Program column and size from the frame of the model month holding the lot-month's first day (`world.ts:312`; `OrdinaryStart.frameMonth`, `rates.ts:70-77`); a funded start reads its `FundedStart.view` and this month's `newCalibration` (`world.ts:312-313`, `world.ts:343`). Size: `SIZE` draw against the lot's size cdf, against 0 existing homes if demolished (`world.ts:314-337`); no level gains a home → the lot becomes `LOT_DONE` for good and no row is made (`world.ts:338-341`). Route: funded `pathway`, else streamlined on a Local Program lot, else drawn (`world.ts:344-346`). First stage: pre-application with the `PPA` draw, else `permitFiled` or `filed` (`world.ts:347-352`). Stage draws and speed always come from the step's frame (`world.ts:399`).

**A stage** (`stage.ts:54-88`): `cal = isNew ? newCalibration : calibration`; a `certain` row always advances on `sampleAdvance` with no exit; else `sampleTransition` on `STAGE`/`STAGE_TIME` given the days already spent; `anchor = entered + survived`, `leave = anchor + floor(speed × (length - survived))`. Speed = `frame.speed[route][stage]` × every `speedNew` entry with `filed === NO_DAY || filed >= from`, clamped to `[MIN_SCALE, MAX_SCALE]` (`stage.ts:36-42`). Re-anchor on a speed change: `used += (c - anchor)/s_old; leave = max(c, c + floor(s_new × (length - used)))` (`stage.ts:95-101`).

### 1.2 Chance

Every draw is `drawOn(streamOf(seedHash(seed), entity, k), purpose, stageIdx)` (`src/lib/sim/scenario/rng.ts:49-72`), a pure counter hash; purposes at `rng.ts:19-32`. Entities: a lot is `fnv1a32('p:' + parcelId)` (`rates.ts:55-67`); a pipeline project or master is `jobEntity(projectId)`, a phase `jobEntity('<master>:phase:<i>')` (`pipeline.ts:146-160`). `k`: a lot's attempt (0 at first; a sim start's exit frees it on `sub + 1`, `holds.ts:99-102`; the last pipeline holder's exit frees it on attempt 0 again, `holds.ts:75-79`); a phase gap draws on the master's entity with `k = i` (`pipeline.ts:349`).

Draws in use: `INIT` (ordinary and market threshold, `rates.ts:126`; `funding/clocks.ts:241`), `INIT_DAY` (ordinary and market day, `rates.ts:129`; `clocks.ts:251`), `INIT_AFFORDABLE` / `INIT_DAY_AFFORDABLE` (`clocks.ts:250-251`), `SIZE` (`world.ts:335`; `clocks.ts:238`; `shadow.ts:76`), `PATHWAY` (inside `pathwayFrom`, `src/lib/sim/scenario/engine.ts:913`; also the affordable route of a certain award, `funding/serve.ts:270-273`), `PPA` (`world.ts:348`), `STAGE` and `STAGE_TIME` per stage index (`stage.ts:70-77`), `REV` and `REV_RATIO` per stage (`stage.ts:133-134`), `PHASE_GAP` (`pipeline.ts:349`). Nothing is drawn per month.

The ordinary clock (`rates.ts:91-131`): lot-month T of a spell free since `r` starts on day `r + 1 + floor((T-1) × 30.436875)` and runs on the frame of the model month holding that day; the segments are the frame of `monthOfDay(r+1)` (capped at the month being read) then each change `c` up to that month, from lot-month `lastInitiationMonth(r, firstDay(c) - 1) + 1`; equal neighbouring rates merge, empty segments are replaced (`rates.ts:105-124`). The rate is `log(1 - logistic(logit + market))` (`rates.ts:27-45`). `piecewiseMonth(log(1 - u_INIT), bounds, rates)` gives T (0 = never). The step only ever reads frames up to the month being stepped.

Comparability: two worlds on one seed draw the same uniform for the same (entity, attempt, purpose, stage) whatever the policy, and draws are order-free. The past frames a lot has lived through are fixed, so a later policy change moves only lot-months that begin on or after the change, never an earlier pass.

### 1.3 Dated policy

**Document** (`src/lib/timeline/policy4.ts:17-61`): three kinds (zoning: transit/painted/category; permitting: routes; money: baseline/fund/terms), each policy `{ id, label, preset, on, where, spans[{from, to, what}] }`. Parsing (`src/lib/timeline/parse4.ts:353-383`) checks each `what` with today's schema 3 parsers, spans start on a model month's first day after the dataset date and end on one's last day, ascending, non-overlapping (`parse4.ts:215-238`), and fund-policy id consistency (`parse4.ts:304-347`). `reconcilePolicy4` re-fits presets, drops unhonourable scales/programmes, drops programmes with an unsourced term, and snaps spans saved on an earlier dataset (`src/lib/timeline/reconcile4.ts:51-93`, `254-299`). A schema 3 policy opens as schema 4 with every policy from month 1 (`src/lib/timeline/upgrade.ts:12-136`).

**Compile** (`policy/compile.ts:153-406`): per month m, in order, the policies in force on its first day (`combine.ts:48-59`) form a state; zoning combines as: rules → each lot the highest storey count naming it, paint → lower policy in the list wins a lot, protections → any turns one on, `newerThan` the largest years (`combine.ts:100-155`); permitting: `all` timers multiplied, `new` timers one `speedNew` entry each with `from` = the span's first day, route shares from the lowest policy setting them, Local Program off if any policy says so (`combine.ts:184-204`); money: baseline scale entries merged with the lower policy winning, added pools and programmes in document order, terms with the lower winning (`combine.ts:211-232`). The zoning view is built by a sliced job (`policy/zoning-view.ts:86-178`) and interned by content (`compile.ts:252-268`). The Local Program switch is a zoning-view input (`compile.ts:203`), not part of `newCalibration`. `frame.calibration` is always the dataset's (`compile.ts:313`); `newCalibration` differs only by route shares (`compile.ts:221-229`); `market` is the dataset's own market step in force on the month's first day (`compile.ts:270-274`), not a policy.

**Frame** (`policy/frame.ts:14-43`): view, calibration, newCalibration, speed, speedNew, market, finance. Finance per month (`policy/money.ts:136-188`): pools and programmes in a fixed id order (baseline then added, off policies included; `money.ts:36-48`); a programme whose policy is not in force carries a placeholder definition and is not offered (`money.ts:88-92`); `offered[g]` from `isOffered` on the first day; arrivals over the month's days (month 1 from the dataset date), capped pools only, off days skipped (`money.ts:157-168`). `finance` is null in every month when the dataset has no finance and non-null in every month otherwise (`money.ts:118`). Frame id = digest of (input and layers identity, view digest, permitting key, market, money key) interned for the worker's life (`compile.ts:295-297`; `intern.ts:124-135`); the money key covers offered programmes, the pools they draw (without inflows), terms and slope (`money.ts:177-182`).

**At a changed month.** Zoning-touched free lots (or all, see 1.1 system 1) are replanned; live rows keep route, size, outcome, length and money; only their speed changes (re-anchor). Waiting applications are re-made under the new frame (size from the attempt's SIZE draw in the new view, bundle of offered programmes it meets) keeping their place, month, applied day and key, or lapse when the size or chance is gone, the bundle empty, or its coverage level lower (`funding/apply.ts:285-335`); a lapse starts the kind's new spell on the month's first day (`apply.ts:325`). An application made this month whose lot-month began under an earlier, different frame is re-made the same way as it joins, or lapses and may re-apply at once (`apply.ts:231-260`).

### 1.4 Public money

**Clocks** (`funding/clocks.ts:217-324`): per free lot and kind a funded clock over the same segments as the ordinary clock. Per segment (`clocks.ts:170-203`): size from `SIZE` in that frame's view; market rate = `min(ordinary, shifted)` where the shift is `oddsShift(grantEquivalent)` of the offered bundle the size meets; affordable rate = the affordable logit (`z + market - slope × (uncovered gap)/cost`) shifted the same way; a certain bundle gives rate -Infinity. Market threshold uses `INIT` (so never later than the ordinary pass; if no lot-month up to T was actually shifted, T is the ordinary one, `clocks.ts:262-273`); affordable uses `INIT_AFFORDABLE`. If the bundle of lot-month T is empty, the kind gets no day: the spell is spent (`clocks.ts:305-308`). Day: certain → first day of lot-month T; else with `INIT_DAY` / `INIT_DAY_AFFORDABLE` (`clocks.ts:307-308`). Key day: the first day of the ordinary pass (market) or of the unshifted affordable pass (affordable), `NEVER_KEY_DAY` when none (`clocks.ts:275-284`). A cheap per-frame eligibility test skips clocks no frame could fund (`apply.ts:97-150`).

**When a lot applies.** `planLot` (`apply.ts:162-171`) = earliest application month of a kind not already waiting, ignoring a month whose `apply` already ran (`line.appliedThrough`). The core puts a free lot at `min(ordinary month, planLot)` (`due.ts:180-185`). System 4 calls `apply` with the taken lots whose `planLot` is this month (`world.ts:623-625`).

**The line and its order.** `applyLots` appends the month's applications after every waiting row, sorted by `lineKey(keyDay, rank, kind)` (`apply.ts:177-198`; key at `src/lib/sim/scenario/funded.ts:1057-1061`): the line is in (month applied, key) order; `seq` numbers rows (`funding/columns.ts:31-34`).

**Serving** (`funding/serve.ts:50-112`), once in system 5: one pass in line order. Grouped form: rows with one award rule (kind, bundle ids, market prototype) form a group; a group's threshold H (largest units that pass against the pools as they stand) is found by bisection (`serve.ts:138-152`); the next row awarded is the earliest member at or below H after the last decided; after an award every H is recomputed (`serve.ts:110`). A row whose lot is no longer free takes its lot's rows off the line (lazy guard, `serve.ts:158-175`). `availableOf` = `leverage × received - committed - spentGrant - outLoan - lostLoan - lostGuarantee`, Infinity uncapped (`funding/pools.ts:59-69`).

**The award** (`serve.ts:183-216`): `awardSet` gives the set; the award day is the later of (`applied` if it applied this month, else the month's first day) and the latest day money reached, this month, any capped pool it asks of (`serve.ts:223-235`); `commitAward` adds money rows and `'commit'` ledger events (`pools.ts:72-112`); the lot's rows leave the line; a shadow is recorded (`shadow.ts:18-25`); `startProject` makes the funded row (its size, view, programmes, dollars; `certain` = coverage level 1; a certain affordable award draws its route from `affordableRoutes`; `serve.ts:242-289`); then `'award'` is sent, after the start's chain (`serve.ts:210-215`).

**Money events** (`pools.ts:129-204`): on entering construction grants and loans `spend`; on completion loans `repay`, guarantees `release`; on exit before construction everything is `release`d; on exit after construction a grant loses `round(λ × G)`, a loan repays `P - L` and loses `L`, a guarantee releases then loses `min(round(λ × guaranteed), reserve)`, λ the `lossOnExit` of the award's frame (`pools.ts:85`). A release or repayment of a capped pool notes the money day (`pools.ts:237-240`). Money rows are dropped at the end (`pools.ts:190`).

**Lapse and re-make**: 1.3. **Ordinary start vs line**: an ordinary start's `'start'` takes both of the lot's applications off the line (`funding/system.ts:49-53`).

**Shadows** (`shadow.ts:50-84`): each shadow (attempt k and `lots.r` at the award, existing homes then) re-evaluates its ordinary start the first time and in every change month; in the month holding that day it calls `displace(row, lot, day, size.units - existing)` with the size from the `SIZE` draw in the view `startRow` would read, and ends; no size → ends silently. Nothing else ends a shadow (`shadow.ts:46-48`).

**Ledger**: `in` (system 2), `commit` (award), `spend`/`repay`/`loss`/`release` (project events), each with day, pool, programme, dollars, via `StepSink.ledger` (`pools.ts:54`, `98`, `195-202`).

### 1.5 Results

**Per world** (`score/series.ts:218-606`): `WorldSeries.add` files each event's deltas by calendar month of its day (only days up to the horizon): stage spans (in-progress per live stage), completions (pipeline vs new), `entitledNew` (simulated rows, `units - existing` on entering entitled after the dataset date), exits after the dataset date, minus existing homes on entering construction, funded homes at a funded completion (`units - existing`), displaced homes on the event's day and, separately, displaced homes paired with a funded completion (`series.ts:403-414`, `495-501`), and tier homes at completion by the programme definitions of the award month (`series.ts:294-317`). A calendar month is final once the stepped model month holds its last day (`series.ts:509-513`; `score/statics.ts:223-227`). Prefix sums give `MonthRow`s (`series.ts:528-542`).

**Ensemble** (`score/chunk.ts:56-107`): per final month, mean and sample standard deviation (divisor W − 1) across plan worlds of every count; `shown` = world 1; plan effect per world = plan − twin of `entitledNew` and `completedNetNew`, then mean and sd. **Band** (`score/view.ts:117-120`): `mean.completedNet ± 1.2816 × sd`; the plan effect band likewise (`view.ts:16-27`). Targets from the mean (`view.ts:141`).

**Money and tiers** are world 1's only (`chunk.ts:74-78`): pool series rebuilt from all of world 1's ledger by today's `poolSeries` (`score/money.ts:16-57`); `owed` from each debt pool's `received` at the last filled month (`view.ts:66-79`); `perAddedHome` and `displacedShare` over funded minus displaced-of-completed homes (`view.ts:88-97`); limit statements computed once, seed-free, from the plan's arrivals over the whole window with month W's pool shapes (`score/statics.ts:115-169`, `202`); tier targets from world 1's tiers (`view.ts:142-143`).

**Card** (`score/card.ts:97-156`): world 1's schedules on the lot read on `day`, today's `chooseProject` and `projectCard`, envelope and lot-limits line from the frame passed in (`policy/limits.ts:143-158`), stage tables from `lookupsAt(frame)`.

### 1.6 The map stream (`map-stream.ts`)

World 1 only. `onEvent` places the row (anchor cell = cell of its smallest T11 parcel; masters nowhere) and marks the anchor cell dirty; an entry into construction records the earliest construction day per T11 lot (`map-stream.ts:289-300`). `flush(world, statics, budget)` packs dirty cells oldest first, each whole from `scheduleAt` on the last day of the stepped month, only once every cell of every project in it is chunk-ready; massings memoised by revision content in an LRU of 60,000; the first piece of work is unbudgeted, a cell's warmed massings carry over when the budget stops mid-cell (`map-stream.ts:302-343`). A dirty cell left with no project is removed if it had been sent. Demolitions: revision 0 carries all, later updates only new or earlier days (`map-stream.ts:354-368`). `complete` once month `windowMonths` is stepped and no ready dirty cell remains, sent once (`map-stream.ts:267-272`, `344-352`). `rewind` (`map-stream.ts:375-409`): rows past the restored world's end are forgotten and their cells dirtied; rows whose schedule key differs dirty their cells; demolitions recomputed and diffed into `restore` and `demolish`. `snapshot` (`map-stream.ts:411-459`): revision 0 with every ready cell packed unbudgeted and the whole demolish list; cells waiting on chunks stay dirty; the next revision is 1.

### 1.7 Rules and special cases a model reader would not expect

- A start where no size level gains a home ends the lot for good (`LOT_DONE`), no row (`world.ts:338-341`).
- The last pipeline holder's exit frees a lot on attempt 0 from the latest exit day of any holder (`holds.ts:73-79`); a completion, a skipped project or a master's `rest` lots block a lot for good (`holds.ts:68-71`, `111`).
- Pipeline rows and phases take caps and existing homes from month 1's view, even a phase filed decades later (`world.ts:228`; `pipeline.ts:395-404`).
- In-flight pipeline rows: time before the dataset date counts at speed 1; the scale applies only to the remainder (`stage.ts:84-87` with `pipeline.ts:260`).
- Re-anchor never puts the leave before the change day (`stage.ts:100`).
- Speed clamp `[MIN_SCALE, MAX_SCALE]` on the product (`stage.ts:41`); each `new` timer's own table is also built per timer (`combine.ts:196`).
- With a funding system plugged in and a dataset that has finance, every frame change (zoning, permitting or money) replans every free lot of the city (`world.ts:510-515`), and finance is never null in such a timeline (`money.ts:118`).
- An application day in a month whose `apply` already ran is lost for that spell, without hiding the other kind (`apply.ts:155-171`); in practice lots freed by chains of systems 5 and 6.
- A pass in a lot-month with an empty bundle spends the kind's spell (`clocks.ts:305-308`).
- A certain application is dated the first day of its lot-month, no day draw (`clocks.ts:307-308`).
- Award day: never before the latest money of the month in a pool it asks of; an application from an earlier month is dated no earlier than the month's first day (`serve.ts:223-235`).
- Money moved by an awarded project's own same-month chain (a release or a repayment) is visible to later rows of the same scan, because thresholds are recomputed after every award and `availableOf` reads live counters (`serve.ts:56`, `110`; `world.ts:635`; `pools.ts:233-235`).
- `'award'` is sent after the funded start's whole chain (`serve.ts:210-215`); the series checks a funded completion against an earlier `'award'` and throws otherwise (`score/series.ts:298-304`).
- A shadow survives the funded project's exit and the lot restarting (`shadow.ts:46-48`).
- Uncapped pools get no arrivals (`money.ts:160`); 0-dollar arrivals skipped (`pools.ts:49`).
- Line compaction only once 1/8 of a line of ≥ 1,024 rows is gone (`line-index.ts:115-131`).
- Key day capped at 2^28, rank below 2^19 (`src/lib/sim/scenario/funded.ts:64-66`, `1057-1061`).
- skip bound widened by 1e-6 relative + 1e-12 (`src/lib/sim/scenario/input.ts:296`).
- Masters with more than 31 phases throw in the series (`series.ts:346`).
- Map: massing LRU 60,000 (`map-stream.ts:59`); an item that will not pack is dropped from its cell with a warning (`map-stream.ts:197-213`).
- Many due-list consistency throws ("out of step", `world.ts:588`, `607`, `612`, `619`, `647`, `652`; `shadow.ts:67-71`).
