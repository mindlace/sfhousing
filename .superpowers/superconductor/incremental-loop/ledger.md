## 2026-11-24 — tension audit (C2)

Source model against the code and against the rulings of the five-level design (`docs/superpowers/design/2026-11-22-1216-incremental-game.html`, R0 to R7, D1 to D14). Items marked PARTNER go to the partner; the rest are composer calls, to be written in the design doc as calls.

- T1 (call). "Only a played run pays" needs a run to be distinct from moving the playhead: today a drag reaches the same result as playing. A run is started by Run, hides the ruler, cannot be dragged, and is the only time drops exist. Between runs the timeline works as it does today and pays nothing. The goal is counted met when a run reaches the deadline.
- T2 (call). A run's speed is the span from today to the deadline over a fixed wall time; the two existing speeds stay for the timeline between runs.
- T3 (call, after D9). A deadline once met stays met after a data update.
- T4 (call). The header's unit count opens the panel that the level cell opens today (saved games, New game, the sync line).
- T5 (PARTNER). D5 and D6: a remixed published policy makes a saved game that "runs only what its level has unlocked", with the rest shown locked. With a tree, "unlocked" is per node. Options: keep D5 with a per-node rule (a policy runs only when everything it sets is within what is bought); offer Remix of a policy only once the tree is complete; or a remix is a free-play game with everything open and no units.
- T6 (PARTNER, proposal). The five-level design says "Paint stays open at every level" and a plan remix "takes the published plan". P11 makes Paint the opening only. Proposal: past the opening the plan is kept in the save and not editable; `/p` links still open; Remix of a plan starts a new game at the opening with that plan; Publish sends the plan during the opening and the policy after it.
- T7 (call, after D7). The first win is still the room goal in the opening; the account is minted there.
- T8 (PARTNER). Saved games made under the five levels (`v: 1`, `level`, `banked`). Options: keep each save's plan and policy, mark the opening done for level 2 and up, grant no nodes, and show its policies as locked until their nodes are bought; or grant the nodes of the kinds its level had unlocked; or set old saves aside and start afresh.
