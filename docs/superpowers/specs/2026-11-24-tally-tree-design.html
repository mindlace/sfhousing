Tally and tree

The game's screens and header parts: the tally card, the tree, the goal headline with side goals, the opening card, the header's unit count and the game panel.

Purpose

This unit draws what the player sees of the game between and around runs. It owns row U1 of the incremental loop design. It decides no rule of the game: every number it shows comes in as a prop or from useGame().

  • Tally is the card after a run: homes by the deadline, units collected, the lump and side rewards, where unbuilt homes stand, Continue.
  • Tree is the screen between runs (P7): the nodes by branch, one open node with Buy, Edit and the switch on a bought node, Run, "Take the next goal", and the ending.
  • GoalHeadline is the header's pinned item (P2, P6): the goal, its bar, what it unlocks, and the side goals behind a press.
  • OpeningCard closes the opening: the room goal met, what comes next, Start, the account offer (D7).
  • GameCell is the header's unit count (call 7). It opens GamePanel: saved games (D8, D14), New game, the sync line, the sound switch (call 18) and "Collect for me" (call 17).

Rulings carried: P2, P6, P7 and P9 (units are what was collected), P12 (the switch on a bought node), P15 (no five-level words), D7, D8, D14, calls 7, 9, 17 and 18. The global constraints on keyboard, targets, reduced motion and flashing bind every part.

The numbers the balance script tunes: the lump for a rung first met, and each side goal's target and reward by its id.

  • The first ladder year is the largest multiple of five that is at least five years before the window's end year. The last is the first multiple of five at or after the measured year. When more than four five-year steps fit, the first and last are kept with two evenly between.
  • The cheapest node shown at rung 0 costs no more than RUNG_LUMP.
  • A side goal's reward and target are read from SIDE_TUNING by its id.
  • goalAt is defaultGoal(asOf) with the rung's deadline, and with amount when given.
  • readRun returns null while the view does not cover the deadline's month.
  • resultOf lists a side goal when it is shown, not yet met, and its value is at least its target.
  • settle credits collected, lump and each side reward; records the rung in met when reachedOn is set and the rung is not there; records each side goal; adds 1 to runs; sets last.
  • settle never removes a met rung (call 8).
  • settle pays a lump or a side reward only when it also records it, so a result settled twice pays its lump and its side rewards once. The store refuses a second settle of one result (G7).
  • takeNext moves to the next rung when the rung in play is met and a next one exists. Otherwise it returns the same state.
  • finished is true once the last rung is in met.
  • fitGame drops a bought entry for a node the catalogue does not have and lowers a level above the node's last to it.
  • fitGame drops a side entry for an id SIDE_GOALS does not have.
  • fitGame lowers a rung at RUNGS or above to RUNGS - 1 and drops met rungs at RUNGS or above.
  • fitGame returns the same object when nothing is dropped or lowered.
  • sideShown returns the first SIDE_SHOWN goals whose ring is open and which are not met.
  • A side goal's read returns null when the view lacks what it reads.

Design

Two parts

The design's write-set rules place GameCell and GamePanel in the store's lane. The unit is built in two parts.

  • Part A: Tally, Tree, GoalHeadline, OpeningCard, words.ts, layout.ts, motion.ts, their hosts and specs. They read the store only through AccountOffer (open, sync), which both the store of today and G7 have. Part A merges on its own, before the lane.
  • Part B: GameCell, GamePanel, reload.ts, their hosts and specs. They read state, limits and saves of G7, src/lib/game/words.ts of game-core, and src/lib/game/run/prefs.ts of run-play. Part B is built in the lane of game-save, tally-tree part B, game-sim and game-shell (one unit, its tasks ordered across the parts by its plan), on the store game-save builds and once run-play has merged, and merges with the lane.

Rules every part follows

  • No file under src/lib/game/ui/ imports from $lib/sim, $lib/map, $lib/timeline or $lib/sim-page. A spec file may import $lib/map/key/rows to check labels.
  • Every word on screen comes from src/lib/game/ui/words.ts (Words), except the names the props carry (node labels, side goal labels, save names, deadline, unlocks) and the four store and sync words GamePanel imports from src/lib/game/words.ts: syncText, playedText, FULL_TEXT, STORAGE_TEXT.
  • Counts are written with formatCount ($lib/scoreboard/format) inside the words functions. A month is formatShortMonthYear ("Mar 2049", $lib/fzp/format). Money per added home is formatDollarsExact.
  • A deadline shown (deadline, next.label) is a rung's label, formatShortMonthYear of its deadline: "Dec 2055", "Sep 2066". The parts print it as given.
  • A unit amount shown is total(units) (G2). Every text that shows the mark ◆ is drawn in an aria-hidden span, and an sr-only span beside it holds the same words with the amount followed by "units", from the paired …Name word of Words: priceText and priceName, nextPriceText and nextPriceName, buyText and buyName, balanceText and balanceName; the mark with collectedText and collectedName, with lumpText and lumpName, with sideRewardText and sideRewardName; and a bare count with unitsName. A screen reader hears the …Name words and never the mark.
  • Buttons are BUTTON, BUTTON_PRIMARY or BUTTON_QUIET. A control that is none of them writes min-h-6 min-w-6 and the focus ring classes focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-1 focus-visible:outline-accent dark:focus-visible:outline-accent-dark beside its look.
  • Cards and pop-overs are SURFACE_POP with position and size beside it.
  • A switch is a BUTTON with role="switch", aria-checked, and aria-checked:bg-accent-tint dark:aria-checked:bg-accent-dark/30 beside it, as the Inspector draws it.
  • An off button is aria-disabled="true", never disabled, and its handler returns early.
  • Bars are drawn as a track h-1 rounded-full bg-line dark:bg-line-dark holding a fill whose width is a percentage. Bars are aria-hidden; the numbers beside them are the text.
  • Every part except Tree and OpeningCard renders nothing while useGame().open is true. Tree and OpeningCard are mounted only in a saved game.

Decisions

  • The tree is branch columns of nested lists. Adjacency is the first earlier after in the same branch; it needs no graph library, keeps a reading order, and stacks on a phone.
  • No fogged "?" nodes. treeView returns shown nodes only and the tree reads nothing else.
  • The detail is a side column from 1024 px and inline below. It matches the wireframe on a laptop and keeps the detail by its node on a phone.
  • Run is always pressable. The busy text says why it waits; game-sim owns the wait.
  • Focus at mount goes to the one forward action: Continue, Run, Start.
  • The opening card takes no Escape. Its one action is Start.
  • The tally's title is the month met, or "By <deadline>" when missed, so the title never reads as a goal met when it was not.
  • Stage labels are STAGE_KEY's, held in ui/words.ts and checked by its spec, so the tally names stages as the map's key does without importing the map.
  • The stage and money bars share one scale, the largest row, so the bottleneck is the longest bar.
  • Line 2 of the headline says "Goal met" while met, "Last goal" at the last rung, and "Unlocks: …" otherwise.
  • Below sm the headline drops the word "homes" and line 2 and keeps the bar, to fit 375 px beside the status and the Menu.
  • The panel's heading is the game's name; a row reads "Painting" or "Goal N of M", since a save entry has no deadline year.
  • Prefs are read at mount and written on each press.
  • Durations: 400 ms for a roll, 800 ms for the tally, 1,200 ms for the goal met pulse, 300 ms for a reveal. They are final for this round.
  • The mark ◆ from the approved wireframe stands for units, hidden from screen readers, which hear "units".
