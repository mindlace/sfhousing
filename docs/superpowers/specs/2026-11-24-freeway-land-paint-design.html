Freeway land in Paint

Freeway land is unpaintable, like road-like and underwater lots.

Why

The owner saw Paint draw new buildings straight over the Central Freeway between Market and Duboce (lots 3502112, 3513071, 3513074 and their neighbours). These lots are on the freeway-land list (pipeline/enrichment/freeway-land.json), which is checked before the road-like rule and skips it.

The scenario step zeroes freeway land in parcels.bin, so /sim never builds there. The chunk records (pipeline/sql/60_attributes.sql) clear eligible only for underwater and road-like lots, so all 559 catalog lots are eligible=1, and Paint's applyOp (src/lib/plan/resolve.ts) checks only rec.eligible. Paint therefore counts and draws about 32.8k homes of room on land under freeways.

Goals and non-goals

  • Goal. No lot that is road, freeway land or otherwise, can be painted: the chunk record of every freeway-land catalog lot has eligible=0.
  • Non-goal. Freed freeway land in Paint. If freeway removal comes back it belongs to later, non-Paint levels, which read parcels.bin, not the chunks.
  • Non-goal. Changing the road-like rule, its thresholds, road-lots.json or the catalog itself. Freeway land keeps its own list.
  • Non-goal. A chunk format change. A catalog lot is an ordinary ineligible lot, with no new column.

Approaches

  • A. Chunk eligible=0 for catalog land. Pipeline only. Paint matches /sim. Picked by the owner.
  • B. A per-lot freeway field, gated in Paint by a removed-freeway set. Dropped: any future freeway removal happens in later levels, so Paint never needs to open the land.

Design

  • 60_attributes.sql sets eligible false for a parcel in rl_catalog (already in the SQL session), next to the underwater and road-like exclusions.
  • Every build check that relates chunk eligible to parcels.bin, scenarioEligible or the catalog (V17, V18 and any other) agrees with that; a check asserts no catalog lot is chunk-eligible.
  • Paint's coverage, "Room for N homes", the Level 1 bar and the drawn boxes follow from eligible with no client change. Older datasets resolve as before.
  • The dataset version changes. The branch regenerates the fixture, builds the live dataset, runs scripts/level2-check.ts on it, and uploads before signoff (docs/dev/pipeline.md, "Finishing a branch that changes the dataset").
  • Docs: pipeline.md "Freeway land" (replace "The chunk records (and so the Paint editor) are unchanged") and "Road-like lots" where it describes chunk eligible; sim-page.md and game.md if they state the difference or Level 1's room.
Stop and report if Level 1's goal can no longer be reached on the live dataset with the room removed; do not change the goal.
