Better building shapes, part 1: towers that taper, parts the LiDAR disputes, no Sutro pop

Three pipeline changes to the existing-buildings boxes, landed with one dataset rebuild shared with the scenario-engine removal.

Why

The LiDAR boxes (docs/dev/building-boxes.md) were a big step up from extruded footprints, but a few places are still clearly wrong, and the worst are landmarks people look for first:

  • Salesforce Tower renders as a fat block to 203.5 m with a thin core to 326 m. The real tower is full width to about 130 m and then narrows smoothly to about 1,040 m² at 320 m. Cause: Overture models the taper as 8 skillion parts (height 326, roof_height 245); partBoxes draws a non-tiered roof at mid-roof (pipeline/lib/lidar/parts.ts:91-92), and because the parts cover 99% of the footprint the LiDAR is never consulted (pipeline/steps/boxes.ts:296-319).
  • Sutro Tower pops between z13 and z14: z12–13 draw its 757 m² ring footprint at 298 m (a solid slab, about 225,600 m³), z14 draws its 34 parts (about 7,800 m³). Towers on podiums with parts pop the same way, less visibly (Transamerica is the next worst).
  • Tall tapering towers in general are limited to one split per component and 4 steps per building (derive.ts:824-852), so a taper can never be more than a couple of blocks.

The roofer spike (docs/superpowers/design/2026-11-26-roofer-spike/, especially fig/salesforce_profile.png) measured the LiDAR's plan area by height for Salesforce and showed that the LiDAR itself, sliced into levels, is the closest available description of the tower. Roofer meshes help houses but need a new renderer; that is part 2 and out of scope here.

Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

Goals

  • Salesforce reads as a tapering tower at z14+: plan-area error against the LiDAR above 130 m at most 350 m² on average (today 852 m²; roofer managed 262 m²), measured with the spike's profile script.
  • Parts that the LiDAR plainly contradicts stop overriding it, building by building, without breaking parts that are right (Sutro, 555 California, domes and spires).
  • Tall buildings whose roof rises a long way above the main roof render as a stack of terraces following the LiDAR, not at most 4 blocks.
  • Sutro and other parts buildings look the same shape at z13 and z14.

Non-goals

  • Sloped roofs or meshes of any kind (part 2, renderer spike).
  • The z12–13 over-height of towers on podiums that have no parts.
  • Re-fetching Overture parts for roof_direction: the spike's profile shows Overture's wedges put the taper's foot at 81 m, below the LiDAR's 130 m, so even a faithful skillion rendering would be wrong.
  • 345 California (tower parts without heights): noted, not fixed.

Anti-goals

  • Any change to the model: buildings, pieces, parcel_out, buildings_export and every scenario file stay byte-identical (their fingerprints in render-boxes.test.ts hold).
  • Sutro losing its lattice, or any landmark spire being replaced by LiDAR noise.
  • z14+ tile drops (V37) or a z12 budget failure (V11).

For the Sutro pop the choices were: draw parts buildings at z12–13 with a volume-preserving height (no bytes, but Sutro becomes a 10 m ring at low zoom), or draw the parts themselves at z12–13 (about +30 KB at z12, +40 KB at z13 measured by the diagnosis; continuity). Chosen: the parts themselves, with a measurement gate (below).

Design

1. Parts the LiDAR disputes

In buildingBoxes() (pipeline/steps/boxes.ts), before the parts are drawn, each counted part is checked against the LiDAR of its cluster window w, sampled at the part's shape moved by the cluster's registration shift (parts themselves stay unshifted when drawn, as today). Use the cluster window, not buildingWindow: parts may leave the footprint.

  • Checked parts: counted parts with area ≥ stepMinAreaM2 whose roof shape is not in partTierShapes. Tiered parts (domes, spires) and small parts (Sutro's legs and masts) are always trusted.
  • Raised cells: cells under the part that are finite, not vegetation, with hag ≥ raisedHagM. A part whose raised share is under coverageMin is trusted: the LiDAR sees through it (a lattice) or misses it.
  • Disputed: otherwise, when the part's drawn top (today's rule: height or height − rh/2, relative to the building's ground) differs from the median raised height by more than max(partTolM, partTolFrac × drawn top).
  • Rejection: when disputed parts cover at least partRejectShare of the counted parts' area, the building ignores all its parts and takes the plain LiDAR derivation over its footprint (the no-parts path). Otherwise parts are used as today.

Starting values (tier 4, tuned in the live round): partTolM 5, partTolFrac 0.1, partRejectShare 0.25. Recorded per building in roofs.parquet: parts_checked, parts_disputed (counts) and parts_rejected (boolean). The artifact schema moves to boxes/2.

Note hag is Int16 centimetres and caps at 327.67 m; every SF building is under it.

2. Terraces for tall rises

In deriveBoxes (pipeline/lib/lidar/derive.ts), after the main roof (baseTop) is found, a building whose raised cells reach at least taperMinRiseM above baseTop takes a sliced step stage instead of today's single median split. Every other building keeps today's step stage unchanged.

  • Levels: baseTop + k × band, with band = max(stepGapM, rise / taperTiers), up to the raised top's 99th percentile.
  • For each level, the region is the candidate cells (same masks as today: interior, non-vegetation, not rough) with height at least that level, opened and grown back as today, components under stepMinAreaM2 dropped.
  • Each region becomes a box from its level's base to the next level's base (the top level to the median of its cells), so the boxes stack: base_m is the level below, not baseTop. Regions keep today's flush-face growth and clip to the shape; a region drawn at a level must lie inside the region of the level below, enforced by intersection.
  • Rough cells (treeRoughM) stay excluded; fronts lose to terraces as they lose to steps today.

Starting values: taperMinRiseM 40, taperTiers 16. The terraces are src: 'step' boxes. The cap becomes per building: stepCap (4) for the median-split stage, taperTiers × 2 for sliced buildings (two components per level at most before the cap applies, largest by area first). V37's step check, check.ts:1000-1003, compares each building's n_step with its own cap (a new step_cap column, or sliced boolean, in roofs.parquet).

3. Parts at z12–13

For buildings that have part render boxes, z12–13 draw the building's render boxes (parts and whatever LiDAR boxes cover the rest) instead of the single box. Every other building's z12–13 feature is unchanged.

  • Built in pipeline/sql/76_render_boxes.sql as a new low_export table: buildings_export features of buildings with no part boxes, plus the render-export features of buildings with part boxes, with the four z12–13 properties only (parcelId, heightM, baseM, hs).
  • writeBuildingsTiles reads low_export only when the artifact is present (split); without it, the input stays buildings_export and the archive stays byte-identical to tiles-no-boxes/.
  • Gate in the live build: V11 passes, the z12 buildings bytes grow by at most 60 KB, and the count of features tippecanoe drops at z12 does not grow by more than 1% (new report count tiles.buildings.drops_z12_13). If the gate fails, fall back to the volume-preserving height for parts buildings (same table, one column) and say so in the PR.

Buildings whose parts are rejected (section 1) have no part boxes and keep today's z12–13 box.

Constraints

  • Every threshold lives in BOXES (pipeline/boxes-config.ts) with its tier, and is documented in docs/dev/building-boxes.md.
  • Changing the boxes code or config changes the dataset version: boxes → commit lock → build → reach check → upload → signoff (pipeline.md, "Finishing a branch that changes the dataset").
  • One rebuild: the remove-scenario-engine branch also needs a rebuild. Before running the live pipeline, confirm that branch is finished, merge it into this one, then run boxes and build once.
  • The mini fixture's artifact and golden report are regenerated (boxes --fixture, fixture --update-golden); the fixture should gain a synthetic disputed-parts building and a sliced tower so both paths are exercised end to end.
  • The LiDAR tiles for boxes come from fetch --restore --lidar; the 59 GB EPT cache in the building-steps worktree is only needed for the profile measurement.

Acceptance

  • Salesforce (bid 626a57f8-…): parts rejected, sliced, plan-area error above 130 m ≤ 350 m² (profile script from the spike, rerun on the new boxes); its top within 5 m of 326 m.
  • Sutro (bid c5cbfced-…): parts trusted, same 34 part boxes as today at z14+; at z13 its features are those parts (minus what tippecanoe drops), not the 298 m slab.
  • 555 California, Transamerica and the 10 domed or spired buildings with the most part area: parts not rejected (listed in the PR with their check numbers).
  • Rejected-parts buildings citywide are listed in the PR (count, the 20 largest by area), with before/after captures of the top 5 from scripts/building-review.ts.
  • V11 and V37 pass; z14+ drops 0; z12 gate (section 3) holds.
  • Model fingerprints unchanged; reach check exits 0.
