Paint as step one plan

Slice 4 of the information design review: on / and /p/<id> the header reads "Room for N homes" with its share of the goal, Paint and Simulate become two numbered steps on every page, the Paint tab is labelled and starts with one sentence, each stroke shows what it did in a chip, the painted-areas list goes, and undo and redo leave the desktop screen for the keyboard while touch keeps its buttons.

Overview

Goal
Paint reads as the first of two steps. The header shows the steps "1 Paint" and "2 Simulate" in place of the mode links, and on Paint a headline that cannot be read as a forecast: "Room for 47,335 homes", then "57% of the goal". The Paint tab says how to start, labels Tool, Height and Brush size, and ends with Keyboard shortcuts. A stroke, a refusal, an undo and a redo each show in a chip over the map that is also the polite status. The painted-areas list, its Remove and its Retry go from /; a shared plan shows one line in its place. Desktop has no undo or redo buttons (the keyboard does both); a touch screen keeps them, and the phone's palette strip gains Undo.
Architecture
One new pure module for the headline (src/lib/paint/headline.ts), one for the read-only line (src/lib/paint/summary.ts), and one component split out of HistoryButtons (src/lib/paint/ShortcutsButton.svelte). Paint's header reuses /sim's TimelineHeaderScore with a HeadlineText, so the two pages have one header score; the hashed HeaderScore is left alone. The steps are a change to SiteHeader's mode-link snippet. Everything else is in BrushEditor, PaintTab, PaletteStrip, RadiusPopover, HeightPalette and messages.ts. No engine, worker or /sim timeline change.
Tech Stack
SvelteKit 2, Svelte 5 runes, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, Vitest (server project for *.spec.ts, client project for *.svelte.spec.ts), Playwright, axe through expectNoA11yViolations.
