Overview

Goal
The app gets its shared parts: a spacing scale, three button kinds, two message shapes (a chip for a few words, a card for a paragraph) and two surfaces (a popover for menus, panels and explanations; a dialog), each in light and dark, in one module and one doc. Every dialog and menu on /, /p/<id>, /sim and /s/<id> draws on them, so none is white in dark mode; every notice is a chip or a card; a disabled Publish says why on hover and focus. The header centres the score from lg, shows the step numbers at every width, and keeps Undo and Redo for touch only; "Autosave off" is never covered; "Updating" shows the moment the numbers go stale and says why when Play waits for the forecast. Map messages never cover each other. Escape closes the innermost thing only; the key's toggles come before its panels in tab order; a bar's name starts with its visible words. On a phone the policy rows show their names and Redo is in the painting strip. Edge glows clear the header line, the notices and an open key panel, and the key's glow swatch is legible. Under forced colours the pool level and the column's fade are drawn, and the fade works without scroll timelines. At 1280 by 800 Activity shows at least four Latest items.
Architecture
Page and shell work. One new module, src/lib/shell/ui.ts, holds the class constants (the project's pattern: src/lib/shell/pill.ts today, which it replaces) and messageShape; one new component, src/lib/shell/Message.svelte, renders a message in its shape; one new attachment, src/lib/shell/more-below.ts, marks a scroll box that has more below. Files adopt the parts as the tasks touch them ("later slices adopt it as they touch each screen", tbl-slices row 8). Two small interface additions on the map side: SimMapHandle.setGlowInset (the page's top and right insets for the edge glows, beside SimMap's own bottom one) and the --map-chips-bottom property SimMap publishes for its failure messages. No engine, worker, shader or hashed file changes; no dataset upload.
