Policy rounds plan

Implements the policy rounds spec: the simulator first, policies a few per round, no points.

For agentic workers REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps are checklist items; progress is kept in the workspace ledger, not in the plan.

Overview

Goal
After the opening the page is the simulation for the whole game (map in 3D, timeline, Play and Pause, ruler, chart, cards, Details). Twelve policies arrive three per round, fixed until the player wins with them on. The target is the player's own best goal month. Units, drops, the ring, the tally, the tree, the ladder, side goals and sound are gone.
Architecture
Pure modules first (the round catalogue and its document builders, reach by class of transit, the result rules and the change start), then the reach check on the catalogue. Then the points screens go while the state is still version 2, the header gets its Menu at every width, and the state moves to version 3 with limits read from it. Then the page gets the reset on a change, the result state in the pinned goal, taking a win with its explainers and the round panel, and the New and Edit rows. e2e journeys and the docs sweep close it.
Tech Stack
SvelteKit 2, Svelte 5 runes, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, Vitest (server and client projects), Playwright, Cloudflare D1 via Drizzle (no schema change), the timeline worker and scripts/lib/engine-runner.ts for the reach check.
