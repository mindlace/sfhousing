Funding measures implementation plan

Every funding preset ends, a given measure follows its policy's start, a pool's bar reads its lifetime money, the funding editors end at the window, and a given measure reads as fixed text.

For agentic workers REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps are checklist items; progress is kept in the workspace ledger, not in the plan.

Overview

Goal
Make the level 5 funding presets finite, fixed measures (each preset's money ends, dated from the start the player chooses through dateMeasure, row M1), and show them so: lifetime pool bars, editors bounded by the window's last month, the federal pool edited as a capped pool, a money-only read-only summary, and reworded funding copy.

  closed: Record<string, Day | null>;
  /** Per pool id, by calendar month: true while the pool is closed at the end of that month. */
  shut: Record<string, boolean[]>;
}
  • A pool's last arrival is the last arrival the document in force gives it; a pool with a yearly inflow with no end has none.
  • A pool is spent in a month when the month begins after its last arrival, it has no loan out and holds no guarantee reserve, and that month's serving finds an application short on it.
  • A spent pool is closed from the first day of the next month, and stays closed until it receives a new arrival.
  • A closed pool is treated exactly as a pool switched off from that day: its programmes are in no bundle, and the applications waiting on them are re-made as on a re-pricing day.
  • Money returned to a closed pool stays in it and is never awarded.
  • A pool whose last arrival changes because the policy changed is judged again from the month the change takes effect; a closed pool that receives a new arrival is open again from that arrival's month.
  • The worker's rewind restores each pool's open or closed state with the world.
  • The older scenario engine keeps its behaviour: an application waits on a spent pool.
  • Details lists each closed source by its pool's label with the month it ran out: "{label}: ran out Mar 2030".
  • shut[id][i] is true from the calendar month the pool ran out (the month that found it spent) through the last calendar month before it opens again.
  • A pool's bar on the timeline reads empty in every month shut marks: what a closed pool still holds is never awarded.
