Goal due at the window's end; no rings or glows: plan

Implements the goal-window-no-halos spec in two independent parts: the goal as its own value (Tasks 1 to 4) and the map without rings and edge glows (Tasks 5 to 8), then a final check (Task 9).

For agentic workers REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps are checklist items; progress is kept in the workspace ledger, not in the plan.

Overview

  • Goal: the /sim goal becomes a value built from the dataset date (82,069 homes, due at the window's last month, counted from 30 Jun 2022), and the map stops drawing per-project rings, merged rings and edge glows, keeping only the ◆ policy marks.
  • Architecture: src/lib/goal/goal.ts gains GOAL_BASE (everything but the date) and defaultGoal(asOf); TimelineMode derives the goal from its calibration's asOf and passes it to every reader, which already take a Goal argument. Paint and About read GOAL_BASE and show no window date. On the map, CityLayer and the glow inset go; the halo layer draws marks only, thinned below a fixed zoom 14; CityStore and TimelineUpdate.city stay.
  • Tech stack: SvelteKit 2, Svelte 5 runes, deck.gl custom layers (GLSL), Vitest (server and client projects), Playwright.
  • Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-goal-window-no-halos-design.html
Deviation from the spec's literal date The spec sets the deadline to monthLastDay(asOf, WINDOW_MONTHS) (the Playhead's windowEnd). That day lies inside a calendar month the view does not hold: scoreStaticsOf keeps only whole calendar months up to it (src/lib/sim/world/score/statics.ts:185-189), so for fixture-0 (asOf 20736, 10 Oct 2026) windowEnd is 9 Oct 2066 while view.lastIndex is Sep 2066. goalStep then returns null (goal.ts:121, deadline index 480 > lastIndex 479), which removes the chart's step and the ruler's Goal flag (TimelineMode.svelte:573-575), and goalOutcome can never say "missed" because the playhead stops at the last whole month (playhead.ts:23-29, outcome.ts:56). This plan therefore uses the last day of the view's last calendar month (windowHorizon, Task 1), and lets the outcome judge a goal at the window's last day (Task 2). The goal then reads "by Sep 2066" for fixture-0, not "Nov 2066" as the spec's example says. See Open questions.
