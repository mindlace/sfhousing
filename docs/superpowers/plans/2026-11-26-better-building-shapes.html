Better building shapes, part 1: implementation plan

A LiDAR check on mapped parts, terraces for tall rises, parts drawn at z12–13, then one live rebuild shared with the scenario-engine removal.

For agentic workers REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps are checklist items; progress is kept in the workspace ledger, not in the plan.

Overview

Goal
Salesforce reads as a tapering tower, parts the LiDAR plainly contradicts stop overriding it, tall rises render as stacked terraces, and Sutro (and every parts building) has the same shape at z13 as at z14.
Architecture
Three changes inside the existing boxes → artifact → build flow (docs/dev/building-boxes.md). (1) A new pure module pipeline/lib/lidar/dispute.ts checks each counted part against the cluster's raster window; buildingBoxes() (pipeline/steps/boxes.ts:272-327) takes the no-parts path when the disputed share is too high. (2) deriveBoxes (pipeline/lib/lidar/derive.ts:642-936) gains a sliced branch that replaces the roof and median-split step stages for buildings whose raised heights spread at least taperMinRiseM. (3) 76_render_boxes.sql writes a low_export table that writeBuildingsTiles reads for z12–13 when the artifact is present. The artifact schema becomes boxes/2 (five new roofs.parquet columns) and V37's step cap becomes per building. Code, fixture and tooling tasks come first; the last task runs the one live rebuild after the remove-scenario-engine branch is merged.
Tech stack
TypeScript (tsx), DuckDB spatial SQL, clipper geometry (pipeline/geom.ts), tippecanoe and tile-join; Vitest project pipeline (pipeline/test/**/*.test.ts) and project server for scripts/**/*.spec.ts (vite.config.ts:57-81); the roofer spike's Python scripts for the Salesforce profile.
Spec
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-better-building-shapes-design.html (approved, including the thresholds and drawing parts at z12–13). Sections: §sec-dispute, §sec-terraces, §sec-low, §sec-constraints, §sec-acceptance.

Builder's calls made by this plan

  • V37 reads step_cap (and roofs.parquet also gets sliced): the per-building cap is then a plain n_step > step_cap count with no config in the query; sliced is what the acceptance list and the review read.
  • Ground reference for the parts check is groundOf(w, fp, shift).groundM, and the LiDAR height of a cell is hag + ground − groundM, exactly as deriveBoxes computes h (derive.ts:705-709): the part heights and the derived heights then share one datum.
  • No check without a usable registration: a null window, a null shift, shift.source 'none' or a null ground trusts every part (checked 0), mirroring deriveBoxes's unreliable rule (derive.ts:702).
  • Rejection share uses union areas (area(∪ disputed) / area(∪ counted)): parts overlap in plan, so summed areas could exceed 1.
  • "Taller" for the seen area is by drawn top (partTop, strictly greater); parts with equal tops do not hide each other.
  • A rejected building keeps n_parts and parts_coverage; buildings without counted parts record parts_checked 0, parts_disputed 0, parts_rejected false (not null), so the counts are plain sums.
  • Identical levels merge (new BOXES.taperMergeShare 0.98): a level whose single region keeps at least that share of its parent region's area extends the parent's box instead of starting a new one. Without it a prism tower over a podium becomes 16 identical stacked boxes, all in the downtown z14 tiles that already reach 680 KB of their 1 MB limit (V37). The rendered solid differs by under 2% of area.
  • A region with no child at the next level tops at the median of its cells (the spec's "top level" rule applied per region): a lower tower beside a taller one is not drawn a band too tall.
  • The slicing rough limit is its own value, BOXES.taperRoughM = 1 (equal to treeRoughM, so behaviour is the spec's), and the sampling offset is BOXES.taperSampleFrac = 0.5 (each cell drawn to its nearest level; ruled on open question 1). taperRoughM may be raised in the live round (open question 2).
  • The mini fixture's new paths use bpier (already in buildings, outside parcels, today a new_building fallback with no LiDAR): adding a part and LiDAR to it moves no model input, whereas a new Overture building would change pieces, the chunks and fixture-0.
  • The z12 gate's baseline is main's published buildings.pmtiles on staging: its z12–13 input is buildings_export, which this change does not touch.
  • Tuning and acceptance tooling: boxes --probe (minutes, not the 55-minute full run), building-review --bids and scripts/low-zoom-gate.ts are added because the acceptance list names buildings and a gate the existing tools cannot report.

Review focus

  • A part that leaves the cluster window (Sutro's legs reach past the footprint): no cells under it must read as "LiDAR sees nothing", so the part is trusted and never disputed. Test: Task 1, a part outside the window is trusted.
  • A rejected-parts building whose LiDAR derivation falls back (built after the flight): it must get no part rows and no LiDAR rows, so build draws today's box at every zoom, never parts at z12–13. Test: Task 3, a rejected building whose LiDAR falls back stores no rows.
  • A sliced building over the cap: dropping terraces must never leave a kept box floating over a dropped one. Test: Task 2, the cap keeps the largest terraces and every kept terrace stands on another.
  • A parts building whose uncovered area fell back: z12–13 draw its part rows only, never also today's full-height box. Test: Task 5, in draws no box for a parts building whose uncovered area fell back.
  • A part whose roof_height exceeds its height: its drawn top must be the clamped box top (2.5 m), in the check as in the drawing. Test: Task 1, partTop is the drawn top.

Rules checkParts implements

  1. No usable registration (see builder's calls): every verdict checked: false, rejected: false.
  2. Eligible: area ≥ stepMinAreaM2 and roofShape not in partTierShapes.
  3. Seen area: difference(shape, union(shapes of counted parts with partTop strictly greater)); under stepMinAreaM2 → trusted.
  4. Cells: cellMask(sub, seen, dx, dy) over subWindow(w, bboxOf(seen) moved by the shift, padded 1 m) (parts stay unshifted; the mask samples at part + shift). Raised: finite hag, not veg, hag ≥ raisedHagM. raisedShare = raised / masked cells (0 when no cell); under coverageMin → trusted.
  5. Compared (checked: true): lidarM = median of hag + ground − groundM over the raised cells; disputed when |topM − lidarM| > max(partTolM, partTolFrac × topM).
  6. rejected when at least one part is disputed and area(∪ disputed) ≥ partRejectShare × area(∪ counted).

Open questions

  1. Terrace sampling level (taperSampleFrac). The spec draws each region of cells "with height at least that level" from that level to the next. Since the base is the 5th percentile, about 95% of cells reach level 0, so every sliced tower on a podium has its whole podium raised by one band (rise / 16: 3–15 m downtown). Sampling at L_k + band / 2 (cells drawn to their nearest level) removes the bias and keeps Salesforce's profile unbiased. Ruled: taperSampleFrac 0.5; Task 2's and Task 4's expectations are computed with it.
  2. Rough cells on a smooth taper (taperRoughM). The spec keeps rough cells (treeRoughM 1 m) out of terraces. Seen from above, Salesforce's 130–250 m narrowing is a near-vertical facade: neighbouring 0.25 m cells can differ by several metres, which the rough mask marks, so its terraces could collapse toward the crown and miss the 350 m² bar. Ruled: the live round may raise taperRoughM (sliced buildings only) if the Salesforce profile misses because of it. It ships at 1 m.
