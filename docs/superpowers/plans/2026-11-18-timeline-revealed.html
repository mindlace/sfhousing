Overview

Goal
The pinned block becomes one ruler row that is itself the date slider (transport in its label cell, a date flag riding the playhead, a "Goal" flag at the deadline) over one taller chart that counts from today, with value labels, the ruler's year gridlines, and, while a policy of yours is in force, a current-law line and the shaded gap. Lines, the ruler's track and every outcome figure follow three zones: shown (today to the playhead), seen (to the furthest date reached, dimmed), unseen (nothing; the ruler hatched). An edit pulls the furthest date back to the first month it changes, never before the playhead, and Play then reads "Play from {year}". When the playhead reaches the month the goal is met by its deadline, or passes the deadline short of it, a notice says so once. Rows scrolled under the pinned block are clipped by their own scroll box, so they are never overlapping targets.
Architecture
Two new pure modules: src/lib/timeline/reveal.ts (the furthest date reached, the search for an edit's first changed month by comparing the view the person saw with the newest view on the main thread, and "Play from") and src/lib/timeline/outcome.ts (goal reached or missed, read only over revealed months). A third, src/lib/timeline/chart-model.ts, holds the chart's arithmetic (points from today, the current-law series, running maxima, paths). Playhead.svelte is rewritten as the ruler row (Ruler.svelte goes); ChartRow.svelte is rewritten over chart-model.ts; zones are drawn by two clip rectangles whose widths follow the playhead and the furthest date, so no path is rebuilt per frame. Timeline.svelte splits into the pinned block and a rows scroll box under it. headlineAt takes the playhead's reading and whether a policy is in force, so the header and the chart's readout share one rule. Everything needed is in TimelineView on the main thread (src/lib/sim/world/score/types.ts:160-175): no engine or worker change.

The current-law line

planEffect[i].completedNetNew is the plan world's completedNetNew minus its twin's (src/lib/sim/world/score/chunk.ts:31), cumulative from the dataset date, both on seed 1. While a policy is in force the twin is the default document on current law (docs/dev/ensemble-runner.md, "Edits": "An edit from a document with nothing in force changes the comparison from the pre-plan base to the default on the plan"). So:

current law, month i  =  rows[i].completedNet - planEffect[i].completedNetNew
                       =  twin.completedNetNew[i] + plan.completedNetPipeline[i]
gap, month i          =  planEffect[i].completedNetNew  (exactly the header's "+N from your policy")
at today              =  0 and 0

What it leaves out: the twin's own pipeline completions. The line takes the plan world's completedNetPipeline for both, so a policy's effect on projects already in today's pipeline (permitting timers on in-flight projects, public money awarded to them) is in neither the gap nor the header's number. With nothing in force the twin is the old zoning, not current law, so the line is not drawn at all. No engine or worker change is needed: every term is in TimelineView on the main thread.
