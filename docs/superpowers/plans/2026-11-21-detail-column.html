Overview

Goal
On /sim and /s/<id> the detail column at rest is Activity: the latest stage changes citywide up to the playhead (what, how many homes, where, marked when under the person's policy), the totals so far, cost while a Public money policy is in force, and a Details button; at today it names the goal and, with nothing in force, offers three starter policies. The goal rows read the header's date at today, say when they hold at the deadline, and date the buffer; nothing in Details says "RHNA". Empty kinds say what they add. A pool's bar in the Public money rows shows the share of its money still free, drawn up to the playhead.
