Overview

Goal
The map says what is happening across the city. On /sim: below zoom 14 the per-project rings of slice 5 hand over to one ring per 800 m square of the city, per half-year and per stage tone, sized by the homes in it; a change whose square is off screen shows as a short glow on the map's edge in its direction, at every zoom; progress bars draw from zoom 12 (the phone's default view is 12.2, the desktop's 13) at one size, 32 by 6 px, at every zoom, and where two collide the project under the person's policy wins, then the larger one. The key names the merged ring and the edge glow. /, /p/<id> and /lesscars are unchanged.
Architecture
The worker's map stream already places every project row it hears about, loaded cell or not. A new module beside it (src/lib/sim/world/city-feed.ts) keeps one marker per placed row (the anchor lot's label point, the approved, started and completed days, homes, the two flags) and sends the rows that changed as an optional city delta on the same TimelineUpdate the cells travel in: same revisions, same resync, same rewind, sent once per flush and never per world step. The page keeps the markers in a CityStore inside the TimelineController, groups them into ring records once per applied update, and draws them with one new instanced deck layer, timeline-city (CityLayer), whose vertex shader decides per record and per frame on the GPU whether it is a ring (in view, below the handover zoom), an edge glow (off view) or nothing. HaloLayer gains a zoom gate for its rings. Bars get a second mode ('far') that /sim turns on through a new handle call; /lesscars keeps today's mode. No file under the hashed paths changes.

2. What the feed is

  • One marker per placed row (a project, a phase or a pipeline row with a T11 lot; a master row or a row with no T11 parcel has none, as it has no cell): the anchor lot's label point as float32 lng/lat (0.7 m at San Francisco), the approved day (the first of entitled and permitIssued entered, the halo layer's own rule), the started day (construction), the completed day (a complete end), each NaN when not (yet); the final revision's homes; the SLOT_FLAG bits.
  • Message shape. TimelineUpdate.city?: CityDelta, struct of arrays: rows (the world's row count after this update: markers at or past it are dropped), then per changed row, ascending: row (Int32Array), lngLat (Float32Array, 2), days (Float32Array, 3), homes (Uint16Array), flags (Uint8Array). 27 bytes a row. Revision 0 carries every marker: 30,000 projects are 810 KB, transferred, not copied. A later update carries only the rows an event touched since the last flush. The worker sends nothing per world step: the delta rides the flush (at most every FLUSH_EVERY_MS, 250 ms, runner.ts:103), and a flush with only city rows to say still sends an update.
  • Who computes it. createCityFeed, composed by createMapStream: onEvent touches the row, flush takes the touched rows, rewind diffs every row against what was sent, snapshot sends all. The stream owns revisions, so the feed inherits revision order, resyncMap and the controller's gap check for free.
  • Rewound and replaced on an edit. After a restore the stream's rewind runs (runner.ts:667-679); the feed then drops rows at or past the restored world's n (rows = n) and marks every row whose marker now differs from the one sent. The next flush sends them. The page drops markers past rows, overwrites the rest and rebuilds its records, so no ring of the old numbers survives. 2026-11-20, Task 2 fix round 1: the drop is sent as CityDelta.from, the restored n (the lowest since the last flush), because refill steps may re-create rows before the flush; the page forgets every marker at or past from before applying the delta's rows, which include every re-created row with a marker.
  • The reveal rule. Every marker carries its days and the page hides what the clock has not reached: a record shows only for a day at or before the playhead (the shaders compare with the clock uniform), and the page builds no record for a day at or before today (setHalos({ from: asOf }), slice 5). The worker never sends a day past the world's current month (it reads scheduleAt at the month's last day).

At the desktop default the view spans -122.540 to -122.343 and 37.748 to 37.851, most of the city, so the page pays that today (the 2026-11-14 fill-install bench: 378 installs, whole at 3,257 ms on live data).
