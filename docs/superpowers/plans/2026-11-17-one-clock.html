One clock plan

Slice 2 of the information design review: the /sim header headline follows the playhead and counts from today, the goal is read from one module, every homes figure agrees with the headline, the empty status line goes, and the save state is silent when saved.

For agentic workers REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task by task. Steps are checklist items; progress is kept in the workspace ledger, not in the plan.

Overview

Goal
On /sim and /s/<id> the header reads "47,335 homes by Apr 2040", then "+1,817 from your policy · 68% of the goal", all at the playhead's month, live during Play and scrubbing. Nothing in the header is read at the end of the window. The goal (today the state target) is defined once. Results and both charts stop presenting homes counted from June 2022 as the headline's quantity. The 24 px line under the header is gone, "Updating" sits beside the save state, and "Saved" no longer shows.
Architecture
A new pure module src/lib/goal/goal.ts holds the goal and reads it from a TimelineView. src/lib/timeline/results.ts replaces headerOf with headlineAt (numbers at one month) and headlineText (the strings), which both the visible header and its live region use. TimelineHeaderScore becomes a dumb renderer of those strings. TimelineMode derives the headline from the playhead's month (index) and the view on show (shown.view), never from lastIndex. Everything needed is already in TimelineView on the main thread (rows[i].completedNet, planEffect[i].completedNetNew, baseline.completedNet, months, compare, filledThrough; src/lib/sim/world/score/types.ts:160-175): no engine or worker change.
Tech Stack
SvelteKit 2, Svelte 5 runes, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, Vitest (server project for *.spec.ts, client project for *.svelte.spec.ts), Playwright, axe through expectNoA11yViolations.
Spec
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-information-design-review-design.html, row 2 of Order of work. Governing parts: Main findings 1, Six rules (rule 1), Header, The loop (the goal as one replaceable thing, and progress only), Decided, One vocabulary, and the inventory rows for the headline, the date, progress to the state target, "Work is saved", "The forecast is being recomputed" and "How far the forecast is computed".
Assumes
Slice 1 (2026-11-17-quick-repairs.html) has landed: no "Fewer cars" header link, an "About" entry after the account entry on every route, "FZP" copy reads "current law" (fixed rows "Current law", "Current timelines", "Current funding"; scoreCopy keeps compare: 'fzp' | 'preFzp'), no footer on full-height routes, fromPolicyText(n) in src/lib/fzp/score-view.ts ("+1,234 from your policy", U+2212 for negatives, "+0"), the header delta under test id score-delta already reading that, e2e/helpers/chrome.ts (expectNoFooter, expectAboutReachable) and clientNavigate. /**
 * The homes chart's step: { deadlineIndex, units: amount - valueAt(view, TODAY) }. Null when the
 * measure is not 'homesCompleted' or the deadline's month is outside [0, view.lastIndex].
 */
export function goalStep(
  goal: Goal,
  view: TimelineView
): { deadlineIndex: number; units: number } | null;

The deadline month is the raw month offset of goal.deadline from view.months[0] (do not use monthIndex, which clamps): below 0 means the goal ended before today, above lastIndex means it ends after the window, read as lastIndex.

Builder's call The goal is reached only by its deadline. After the deadline the reading holds the deadline month's value, so a goal first met later (current law passes 82,069 in the late 2050s) keeps showing its percentage, not "Goal reached". The spec's "follows the playhead to the deadline, then holds" and "Goal reached, {date}" only agree this way; slice 3's "goal missed" message covers the rest.
Builder's call The header gets strings, never a Goal. A later goal with another measure adds a GoalMeasure and swaps GOAL; the header component does not change. Nothing in readGoal, goalText or the header assumes the measure is a count of homes; only goalName (through measure.unit) and goalStep (homes chart only) know the unit.
