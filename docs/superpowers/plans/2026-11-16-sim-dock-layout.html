The /sim bottom pane plan

Give the timeline one time axis, pin the play control, ruler and chart, and move the inspector out from beside the map into a detail column on the right of the bottom pane.

For agentic workers REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task by task. Steps are checklist items; progress is kept in the workspace ledger, not in the plan.

Overview

Goal
On /sim and /s/<id>, the ruler, the slider, the chart, every lane and one playhead line share one x scale. The controls row, the ruler and the chart stay in view while the policy rows scroll. From md the map is full width and the bottom pane (about 40% of the mode box) splits into the timeline (about 60%) and a detail column (about 40%, 22rem minimum). The detail column holds the inspector, and Results when nothing is open.
Architecture
Task 1 puts every axis element in the timeline's existing grid. The playhead's root becomes display: contents: its controls span both columns and its track and ruler sit in the lane column. ChartRow maps months with dayPct, the function the lanes and the ruler already use. Task 2 makes the controls, ruler and chart a sticky block inside one scroll box. Task 3 rebuilds TimelineMode's bottom pane as two columns and moves the inspector aside into it. Task 4 changes what the header score does from md, in SimPage. Task 5 re-pins the e2e suite and updates the docs.
Tech Stack
SvelteKit 2, Svelte 5 runes, TypeScript, Tailwind 4, Vitest (client project, vitest-browser-svelte), Playwright, axe through expectNoA11yViolations.
Spec
docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-sim-dock-layout-design.html

Global Constraints

  • One axis. The x of a day d anywhere in the timeline is dayPct(d, asOf, windowEnd) (src/lib/timeline/rows.ts:117-120) of the lane column's width. Ruler ticks, the slider thumb, chart points, bar edges, the hatch and the playhead line agree to within 1 px. There is no second ruler, axis or scale anywhere in the bottom pane (spec anti-goal).
  • The window stays WINDOW_MONTHS = 480 from the dataset date and never moves or zooms (spec non-goal, ruling R9).
  • md is 768 px: (max-width: 767px) in MediaQuery and matchMedia, and the Tailwind md: / max-md: prefixes. From md the bottom pane is a fixed 40% of the mode box (tl-mode). The timeline gets about 60% and the detail column about 40%, never under 22rem. Check both at 1280 and 1024 px wide.
  • Owner rulings, not to be reopened: policy details go in the right column of the bottom pane from md. The project card and the tooltip stay on the map. There is one shared time axis. The controls, ruler and chart are pinned. Results shows by default from md only. From md the header score closes an open editor, add menu or draft and shows Results, or else scrolls Results to the top and focuses it, and it carries no aria-expanded. The pane height is fixed at about 40% from md. The phone keeps today's sheet and toggle, with a sticky pinned block. The 40-year window stays.
  • Unchanged, do not edit: ProjectCardPanel, ProjectTooltip, NoticePills, SourcesDialog, --map-controls-br-* and the zoom lift (TimelineMode.svelte:1202-1215), src/lib/map/**. What the editors and Results contain does not change. Only where they show changes.
  • Keep the test ids tl-inspector (the detail column from md, the sheet below md), tl-dock (the whole bottom pane), tl-map, tl-timeline, tl-results and inspector. The new ids are tl-lanes (the timeline's scroll box), tl-pin (the pinned block), tl-controls, tl-axis and tl-playhead-line.
  • Svelte 5 runes only. Tailwind 4 utilities, with component <style> where the grid needs it (as Timeline.svelte does). Code style: 2-space indent, single quotes, no trailing commas, 100 columns.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA. Targets are at least 24x24 CSS px (44 on a coarse pointer, as today). A focused control is never hidden under the pinned block (2.4.11). The playhead line, a graphic, keeps 3:1 against the surface (1.4.11) in light and dark. Axe goes through expectNoA11yViolations (e2e/helpers/a11y.ts).
  • Vitest runs with expect.requireAssertions, so every test calls expect.
  • Signoff (npm run signoff) is the only check. Do not git push first. chreode fails a run on server stderr, timing crutches (waitForTimeout, fixed sleeps, setTimeout waits) and flakes that pass only on retry.   • Conventional Commits, with messages given as -m flags. No Claude attribution anywhere: no Co-Authored-By trailers, no generated-by notes in code, docs or commits.

Review Focus

  1. A keyboard user tabs to a row that has scrolled under the pinned block. The focused bar must land below the block, not behind it (WCAG 2.4.11), at md (the tl-lanes scroll box) and on a phone (tl-mode scrolls, and the status line sits above the block). Test: t2-s1.
  2. A short desktop viewport (1280x800 or 1024x768). At 40% of the mode box, the pinned block (controls, ruler, chart and legend, about 220 px) could leave almost no room for rows. Expected: at least three rows' worth (90 px) of the lanes stay visible under the block at 1280x800. Test: t3-s1.

  4. A pool draft is open when the header score is pressed from md. Expected: the draft is discarded with today's "An unfinished item was discarded." and Results shows. It must not be dropped silently or left half open. Test: t4-s1.
  5. The read-only view (/s/<id>) from md. Results shows in the detail column at rest, and a selected policy's read-only editor replaces it. The shared banner and the notices still leave the zoom control clear at 768 px. Test: t5-s2 (sim-share).

Builder's call The playhead line is drawn as two segments with the same left: one in the pinned block, one through the rows below it. The line is 2 px wide, pointer-events: none, aria-hidden, in the accent colour (--color-accent, or --color-accent-dark in dark), and is drawn above the bars. The chart's own hover line stays as it is (spec).
