# /sim: the policy simulation page

Developer notes for `src/routes/sim/` and `src/lib/sim-page/`. Since the timeline-ui branch the page is `TimelineMode` (`src/lib/timeline/`) over a schema 4 document that `SimPage` edits: the timeline, the inspector, the session and the worker messages are in [timeline-ui.md](timeline-ui.md). `FzpMode`, the Setup and Run phases, the dock, the run slider, `?seed=`, the lock and the high-water mark are gone. `/fzp` is still a 307 redirect to `/sim` with every query parameter kept except the retired `start` (Call 14), and `/sim` opens on the document the autosave holds, else `DEFAULT_POLICY4` (the Family Zoning Plan as adopted; there is no current law).

Older sections below (plans 3 to 5 and sim-ux) describe the schema 3 recipe and its editors. The editors are still those components, hosted on one span of a schema 4 policy through `lens.ts` (`spanLens`: a span projects to a `SimPolicy` and writes back); the document and the worker's compile are [policy-timeline.md](policy-timeline.md).

## Layout

1. **Header.** The shell's header (unchanged) holds the header score (`TimelineHeaderScore`, pinned: "{n} homes added by {year}", the RHNA meter, "+N from your policy", signed with U+2212 for a minus (`fromPolicyText`; `vsFzpText` stays for the hashed scoreboard); from md pressing it closes an open editor, menu or draft and shows Results in the detail column, focused, with no `aria-expanded`; below md it toggles the sheet), the status slot (the save state, then the fill state; [timeline-ui.md](timeline-ui.md), rule 32) and the actions: View menu, Undo and Redo, Publish. Below lg the nav links and every other action wait in the "Menu" (a panel under the header, Escape closes it); the pinned score stays in the bar. From lg the header is a three-column grid (`minmax(0,1fr) auto minmax(0,1fr)`): name, mode links and status left, the score centred, the actions right. The score button's name is the count, the delta its description. Its text is not a live region; one polite `sr-only` region speaks the playhead's month's count while paused, 1000 ms after the day settles.
2. **The map**, full width from md, then the bottom pane: 40% of the mode box, the timeline (controls, ruler and chart pinned, then the rows) beside a detail column of at least 22 rem. The column holds the selected policy's editor, "+"'s menu or a draft, and Results otherwise (`Inspector`, `SimResults`). Below md the inspector is a bottom sheet. `--map-controls-br-*` still bound the card's area ([map-interfaces.md](map-interfaces.md)).
3. **Tools.** Move, Paint and Erase. A tool that is on always has a control: in the painted policy's editor, else "Stop painting" or "Stop erasing" in the header from md, and below md the palette strip (`PaletteStrip`, `src/lib/paint/`: the tool, the height swatch, the size, Done). Paint allows the stories shown as "Allow N stories"; while Erase is chosen the stories stepper is disabled with the /sim hint.
4. **Undo and Redo** (`HistoryButtons`) name the change ("Undo: Allowed up to 8 stories on arterials") and the announcement names what was undone. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z go through `BrushController`. Beside them a "Shortcuts" button (named "Keyboard shortcuts"; absent on coarse pointers) opens the shortcut table as a native popover, not a disclosure: it is in the top layer, so it takes no room in the header row or the dock and the dock's scroll box does not clip it. `placePopover` puts it under its button, right-aligned and inside the viewport; Escape (marked handled, so the dock and the header Menu stay as they were), a second press, an outside press, a resize or a scroll outside it closes it.

- **The map's navigation control stays uncovered** (WCAG 2.2 2.4.11). `SimMap` puts its zoom and compass control (fine pointers only) and, on coarse pointers, its Reset view button at the map's bottom left. `TimelineMode`'s style lifts both above the attribution's footprint, `max(inset, --map-controls-br-block-size)` (`src/lib/map/**` is `/lesscars`' and is not edited). The phone palette strip is fixed to the viewport's bottom over the timeline, so it never overlaps the map. `e2e/sim.spec.ts` and `e2e/sim-share.spec.ts` check each button's box.
- A selection is a span of a policy (`SpanRef`, `src/lib/timeline/spans.ts`) and stays selected while other rows open and close.

## The recipe and its compile rules

The schema 3 recipe below is what the hosted editors read and write for one span (`lens.ts`). The page's document is `SimPolicy4`; the page itself no longer calls `compileLevers`; the worker's policy compiler (`src/lib/sim/world/policy/`, [policy-timeline.md](policy-timeline.md)) calls it for the transit rules, so those rules keep their meaning. The page edited a `SimPolicy` (`src/lib/sim-page/policy.ts`, schema 3), a recipe of rule and stroke parameters, not lot lists (Call 1). `compileLevers` turns it into the FZP-only `SimLevers` ([sim-levers.md](sim-levers.md)) once every lot query has resolved (`null` until then). The recipe is what plan 5 autosaves and publishes (inside a `SimDoc`, "Autosave, publish and the read-only view" below); `timing` is plan 4's, edited in "Permitting timelines"; `funding` is edited in the Funding tab ([sim-funding.md](sim-funding.md)).

| Field     | Meaning                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `rules`   | New zoning rules: `transit` (stop types, and distance bands each with stories)                                                                                |
| `custom`  | Custom zoning, in stroke order: `paint` (strokes and the stories they allow) and `erase` (strokes)                                                            |
| `protect` | The categories: `rentControl`, `historic`, `newerThanYears`                                                                                                   |
| `timing`  | `current` (preset, preset timers and shares, custom edits and shares, Local Program) and `saved` (plan 4; [sim-timers.md](sim-timers.md))                     |
| `funding` | The funding levers: `baselineScale`, added `pools` and `programmes`, typed `terms` ([sim-funding.md](sim-funding.md), [finance-levers.md](finance-levers.md)) |

A stored `street` rule (from before 2026-11) is dropped on load; publishing one is `bad-field`.

Compile rules (sim runs spec, section 3; "Call n" is plan 3's):

- **Freeway land never builds** on `/sim`: a parcel in the freeway-land catalog is scenario-ineligible in `parcels.bin` ([pipeline.md](pipeline.md), "Freeway land"), so no rule or stroke reaches it. Nothing new is drawn for it.
- **Queries resolve in the worker** (Call 2) to scenario-eligible lots only (Call 3). A stroke covers a lot whose label point is within the radius of the path, as on `/`. `ruleQueries` gives a rule's queries (a transit rule: one per band, outermost first), `customQuery` a custom brush's one freehand query, and `queriesOf` every query the policy needs, keyed by canonical JSON.
- **Rules are additive:** each rule compiles to `SimLevers.rules` in list order, one edit per transit band; the engine gives each lot `max(FZP, N)` over every rule that names it, whatever their order. Rules have no reorder buttons. An edit left with no lots is not emitted.
- **Custom zoning paints in order:** each `paint` brush compiles to `SimLevers.paint` and sets its lots to exactly N (raising or lowering), a later brush winning; an `erase` brush removes its lots from the paint before it, never from the rules or later paint. There is no reordering. A Paint stroke joins the last custom brush when that one paints the same stories, an Erase stroke the last erase brush; otherwise it starts a new brush. `paintedLots` gives lot → painted stories after erasing. `removeCustomBrush` (`stroke-adapter.ts`) drops one brush, and the two brushes now adjacent merge when they are the same kind and (paint) the same stories: the earlier brush's id is kept and its strokes come first, which is `appendStroke`'s join rule and `/`'s derived row grouping. Undo restores the brushes as they were.
- **No modes or density limits:** rules and paint are uncapped by density (the engine keeps FZP's own caps on the baseline).
- **Stories** are the editor's 1 to 80 (Call 6).
- **Built height:** the generator builds at most the lot's physical max stories (`physicalMaxStories`, `src/lib/generator/physical.ts`), so built height is min(allowed N, physical max), then the typology cascade. Recorded projects are not capped.
- **Transit rules:** see "Stop types and bands" below, Calls 8 to 10.
- **Protection** (Call 11) is the categories only. A protected lot is **not redevelopable** (partner ruling 2026-10-29): protection beats every rule and stroke. Projects already in the pipeline on a protected lot carry on (Call 21).
- **Funding** is copied into the levers' `finance` by `compileLevers` (`structuredClone`); it is not reconciled there (that happens on load, `reconcileFunding`), and the editor offers only pools the dataset has. Any scale entry, added pool or programme, or typed term makes the policy non-default.
- **The twin:** `compare` is `'preFzp'` when the policy equals `DEFAULT_POLICY` (`isDefaultPolicy`, ignoring the timeline Save snapshot), else `'fzp'`. Any customisation, a timing change alone included, compares with FZP as written; undoing back to the default compares with pre-FZP again.
- **Map feedback**: the lot dots and their map key are gone. The overlay mux (`src/lib/map/overlay-mux.ts`, shared with `/`) has the slots `feedback`, `limits`, `outline` and `ring` (drawn in that order, bottom to top); `SimPage` creates it per map handle in both modes. The ring, the height limits, the eraser's outlines and the live preview are described under "Map overlays" below.
- **Map lookups** (Call 13): the map gets the worker's posted `lookups.fresh`; each card picks `inFlight` or `fresh` for its project.
- **No local persistence** (Call 15): **superseded by plan 5.** The policy autosaves and publishes; see "Autosave, publish and the read-only view" below.
- **Sends are debounced 250 ms** after the last change once every lot is resolved (Call 18; `PolicySync`); the worker lets the latest `setPolicy` win. The opening send of a given policy (`/s/<id>`) is not debounced: see "Request flow".

`PolicyStore` (`policy-store.svelte.ts`) keeps the recipe with undo and redo: every change is one labelled store change, so the Undo and Redo buttons name it and Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (the `/` editor's keys, through `BrushController`) step through the same history.

## Map overlays

Sim map overlays plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-31-sim-runs-2-map-overlays.md`), Task 4. Everything here shows at all times, in both modes (`/sim` and the read-only `/s/<id>`), and answers for the zoning in force in the model month the playhead is in (the worker answers for the last `viewMonth`, [timeline-ui.md](timeline-ui.md)).

- **View menu.** The header actions start with `ViewMenu`, always (rule 18): rows "Transit stops", "Transit capacity within a 10-minute walk" and "Zoning height limits" (on by default), or only "Zoning height limits" when the manifest the map renders (`TimelineMode`'s `onmanifest`, which follows a Retry) has no `transit`. Its swatches and the limits layer share one `useSystemTheme()`.
- **Prefs.** `viewPrefs` is read once with `readViewPrefs(storage())` and every change is written with `writeViewPrefs` to the same storage (SimPage's `storage` prop, default `localStorage`; the test host passes an in-memory one). It is the `sfhousing:view` record `/` uses, so a viewer's transit choice follows them between modes. A record from the fog's time (`heightFog`) still reads as `heightLimits`. TimelineMode gets `view = viewPrefs`.
- **Height limits.** `city` is the latest `HeightCity` from TimelineMode's `onheights`; `lids` is the latest `heightLids` answer for the view. One effect writes the overlay mux's `limits` slot: `[heightLimitsLayer({ city, lids, mode: limitsMode, theme })]` with `heightLimits` on, else `[]`. See [map-interfaces.md](map-interfaces.md), "Height limits". Contours show city-wide as soon as the city is in; from z14.5 `LidRequest` asks the worker for glass lids on each `moveend` (view bbox clamped 3 km around the view centre) and keeps the last answer, so a theme or tool change asks nothing. The height is the effective table (FZP plus rules plus paint); a painted lot under a category protection shows its FZP height (ruling M5: protection beats every rule and stroke).
- **Mode.** `limitsMode` is `plain` by default; `paint` while the Paint or Erase tool is chosen in `/sim` (paint-set limits are dashed, so the brush's effect can be found); and `rule` while a transit policy is selected, which wins over the tool (`limitsViewOf`, `selection.ts`).
- **Selection and the limits view** (`selection.ts`, `limitsViewOf`). A selected transit policy gives the `rule` view on its id (`ruleId === policy id`); the fixed "Current law" zoning row gives `fzp`; a selected painted policy, or Paint/Erase in edit, gives `paint`; else `plain`. Selecting a transit policy or the fixed zoning row turns the height-limits layer on if it was off. The map dims other lines and labels and shows the policy's lots; see the modes in [map-interfaces.md](map-interfaces.md). The schema 3 dock's `DockSelection` (`liveSelection`) stays in `selection.ts` for `TransitTab` only.
- **Strokes.** A Paint or Erase stroke goes into the selected painted policy's span through `spanLens`; the outlines, the live preview and the lot lookups are as below.
- **Map selection** (rule 19). With the Move tool, a tap on a project that exists on the playhead's day opens its card (`cards` is always on). A tap on a lot with none is TimelineMode's `onmiss`; the page then takes the map's next `click` and asks `api.lotAt([lng, lat])`, and only the latest click's answer counts (a sequence number; an answer after a tool change is dropped). `selectionForLot(owner, parcelId, doc, day)` (`selection.ts`) maps the worker's owner to the span of the policy that sets the lot's limit, covering the playhead's day, else its first; the plan, or a policy the document no longer holds (the answer can be a revision behind), selects the fixed zoning row; no lot clears the selection. Then `openKind(ref.kind)` opens that kind's rows. Focus stays on the map. With Paint or Erase chosen a click is a stroke. The card's "Allowed by" is `ownerName4` and its "Funded by" `fundingName4` (`src/lib/timeline/owner.ts`). Hover tooltips work in every case.

## Transit layers

On `/sim` the list is the Zoning kind's rows and the editor is `TransitLayerRow` in the inspector (stop types per policy, bands per span; `part`); `TransitTab` is not mounted there. What follows describes the editor and its edits.

The Transit tab (`TransitTab.svelte`, `TransitLayerRow.svelte`; edits in `transit-edit.ts`) is `<ol aria-label="Transit layers">`: one row per zoning rule, in policy order (layers combine by taking the tallest, so order means nothing and there is no reordering), then the locked **FZP baseline** row, then **"+ Transit layer"**.

- **A row** (`data-layer-row={rule.id}`) is a select button (`aria-pressed`) holding the neutral ink swatch with the layer's tallest band (`layerTop`), its name ("Near Muni Metro", `layerName`) and its lot count, named "Near Muni Metro · 8 stories · 1,204 lots". The lot count is also a sibling `sr-only` polite status, outside the button (a button's children are presentational). Retry follows when the lookup failed. A selected row has the accent border on a raised surface, as the Paint tab's rows.
- **The FZP baseline row** (`data-layer-row="fzp"`) is a select button named "FZP baseline" with an `aria-hidden` lock glyph: no lot count, no editor.
- **"+ Transit layer"** (`data-brush-add`, named "Add transit layer") adds `newLayer()` (regional rail and Muni Metro at `DEFAULT_BANDS`) as one change named by `ruleChangeLabel`, selects it and focuses its first chip. While the catalog loads, or without transit data, it is `aria-disabled` with `transitUnavailable`'s reason as its description.
- **The editor** follows the selected row's button, in place (no form, no Save): a "Stop types" group of chips (`aria-pressed`; "Muni Metro · 34 stops" from the catalog, the stop label alone without one; the last chip on is `aria-disabled` with the description "A layer needs at least one stop type"); per band, innermost first, a group "Band N" with "Within" (a number input in metres named "Within metres, band N", whose `min`/`max` come from `distanceRange`, widened to include the current value so a saved band past `BAND_MAX_M` renders valid; committed on change and Enter, clamped to `distanceRange`; a non-number reverts) and a stories stepper ("Fewer stories, band N", the value as a polite `output`, "More stories, band N"; `aria-disabled` at `storiesRange`'s bounds, so an outer band stops at the inner band's height), and "Remove band N" (`aria-disabled` with one band); every control's name starts with its visible text and is unique in the row; then "+ Band" (`aria-disabled` when `addBand` is null: three bands, or the outermost at `BAND_MAX_M`) and "Remove layer". The bounded controls are `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so a keyboard run that reaches a bound keeps focus.
- **Edits** are each one store change, `store.apply(p => replaceRule(p, next), 'Edited: …', coalesce)`: a stepper run on one band coalesces (`rule:<id>:stories:<i>`), as do distance commits (`rule:<id>:distance:<i>`), so one Undo takes the run back; chips and band adds and removes are a step each. A selection change (deselecting the row, selecting another) or the tab remounting ends a run: TransitTab suffixes the key with a run number (`@<n>`) it renews on each, so a later press is a new step. An edit that changes nothing is not applied. Every rule the editor hands on passes `bandProblem`, so nothing invalid reaches the store or `compileLevers`.
- **Remove layer** is one change ("Removed: …"); the selection clears and focus moves to the layer now at that index, else "+ Transit layer".
- No building flash on a transit edit: the lids, contours, the row's lot count and the "Updating…" pill show progress.
- **Limits colours.** Light theme: `limit-ink` `#2B2A28` (labels), `limit-edge` `rgba(43,42,40,0.45)` (rims and contours), `limit-glaze` `rgba(72,74,78,0.4)`, `limit-wall` `rgba(72,74,78,0.25)`, `limit-halo` `#F3EFE6`; dark theme: `#E8E4DC`, `rgba(232,228,220,0.4)`, `rgba(214,216,220,0.3)`, `rgba(214,216,220,0.18)`, `#1C1D1F` (`src/lib/map/palette.ts`, with contrast tests in `palette.spec.ts`). Glaze is a neutral grey with no hue; its alpha steps with the room to grow (`GLAZE_ALPHA`). The old `height-fog` token is gone.
- **Outlines** (Task 5; selection added in sim-ux plan 3). The outline request wants, in order: the selected painted area's lots (its `customQuery` result, an erase area's too; while that query is unanswered the last wanted set stands, and a failed one draws nothing), else, while the Erase tool is chosen, the painted lots, else nothing. The rest of this item is the Erase case. While the Erase tool is chosen in `/sim` (`mode === 'edit'`), the custom-painted lots are outlined: one dashed ring per lot (4 m dash, 3 m gap, 2 px, the `painted-outline` token), in the mux's `outline` slot, layer id `sim-painted-outline` (`paintedOutlineLayers`, `src/lib/paint/outline-layers.ts`, shared with `/`'s row outlines). The lots are `paintedLots(store.policy, lots)` (erase strokes already removed), where `lots` reads `LeverSync.result`. While that returns null (a stroke's query is unresolved) the last wanted set stands, so the outlines do not blink after each stroke. The geometry comes from a worker request, `lotOutlines` (`ScenarioClient.lotOutlines`, `SimClientApi.lotOutlines`): rings of the loaded lots as transferred typed arrays (`ringLot`, `starts`, `positions`), plus `missing`, the count of lots whose chunk is not loaded yet. `OutlineRequest` (`outline-request.ts`) sends it for a changed set only, drops an older answer, and asks again on FzpMode's `onchunks` while the last answer had `missing` lots (or failed with `no-session`). `SimPage` keeps the last answer, so a theme change redraws without a new request. Answered only by a session with levers (`no-session` otherwise).
- **Live preview** (sim-ux plan 3, Task 4). While a Paint stroke is drawn, the generated buildings its lots could hold appear under the brush ring, as on `/`: one deck `SolidPolygonLayer` (layer id `sim-feedback-<n>`, `pickable: false`) in the mux's `feedback` slot, at full strength while the stroke lasts. On release (the commit) it fades linearly to nothing over `FEEDBACK_FADE_MS` (1500 ms), because Set Policy's map shows the present and the lids and contours carry the new limit; under reduced motion it clears at once. A cancelled stroke (Escape, a second finger, a tool change, a window blur) clears it at once, since nothing was committed, and a new map handle clears it and drops answers still on their way (`onMapReady` destroys the old controller and calls `strokePreview.cancel()`, a barrier). Only a drag shows live buildings: a keyboard stamp commits straight away with no preview (nothing to fade), and a tap's quick pointer-up supersedes its one preview before any request goes out. A press held past one animation frame does send a preview for its single point, and shows that point's buildings if the worker answers before release, then fades like a drag's. An Erase drag previews nothing (erasing removes paint; the dashed outlines show its reach), and adding or editing a transit layer shows no buildings (spec Goal 2: the lids and contours update, the row's lot count updates and "Updating…" shows progress). `feedback.ts` holds the layer (`PaintFeedback`: `live(pack)`, `release()`, `clear()`, `state` `'none' | 'live' | 'fading'`). `stroke-preview.ts` is `StrokePreview`, which the stroke adapter hands to BrushController as its client's `preview` and `cancelPreview`; the adapter's `commit` and `undo` are its fading barriers. It keeps one rev counter across previews and barriers, so revs keep increasing as `SimClient`'s do, and drops an answer to a preview a barrier followed. Every preview's rev is answered through `BrushController.previewDone(rev)` (in a `finally`, whatever became of it: an Erase, a failed request, a dropped answer), so the controller keeps `/`'s rule of one preview in flight, rate-limited to a frame, and never waits on an answer that will not come. A preview first yields a microtask, so the pointer-up's last preview, which the commit right behind it supersedes, asks the worker for nothing. A Paint preview resolves the stroke alone (`customQuery` of a one-stroke brush, `api.resolveBrush`), keeps its lots in the map's bounds nearest the ring (the path's last point; `lotsInView`, at most `PREVIEW_MAX_LOTS`), and asks for their buildings at the stroke's stories. The geometry comes from a worker request, `previewBuildings` (`ScenarioClient.previewBuildings`, `SimClientApi.previewBuildings`; `no-session` without a lever session): per lot, of the first `PREVIEW_MAX_LOTS` (300, `src/lib/sim/scenario/preview.ts`, shared by the host and the page), a lot protected under the levers in force builds nothing (`LeverWork.protectedMask`, set at `current` from `protectedRows`); a lot whose record is not loaded counts as `missing`; any other builds at the stroke's `stories` (required), clamped by the generator at the lot's physical max. The buildings are `generate(scenarioRecordOf(…), { maxStories }, fnv1a32(id), 'max')` through the host's generator memo under `simKey(id, stories)` (the same call and key as the timeline's simulated buildings, so either warms the other), packed as `packCell('preview', …)` with the plan's existing units and drawn by `packedCellLayer` (`generated-controller.ts`, shared with the controller's own layers). The handler is synchronous: 300 cold lots measured 13 to 26 ms on Node against `SLICE_MS` (`scenario.bench.spec.ts`, "previewBuildings budget"). Before a session's first `current` the mask is not set, so previews apply no protections (the default levers protect none; a restored policy with protections painted in its first seconds can briefly show buildings on a protected lot). The preview does not hide the existing building under it, because the timeline controller's demolish pump owns the `replaced` feature state. In VITE_E2E builds `window.__sfhSim.preview` (`src/lib/sim-page/debug.ts`) reports the layer's state.
- **Header width.** The shell header is inline (mode links, status, centred score, actions) from lg (1024 px) and the Menu layout below it, because the third mode link ("Fewer cars") did not fit the header's status ("Autosave off"), Publish and a five-digit score at 768 px. From lg to xl the site name drops "Simulator" and the mode links tighten (`px-1`, `gap-2`) so the row fits at 1024 px with the score centred (the header's three-column grid). The fill state ("Forecast filled to 2047") is not in the header below 2xl: it is a line under the bar, so the status slot never has to fit it ([timeline-ui.md](timeline-ui.md), rule 32). Checked by the SiteHeader spec's 1024 and 1280 px rows and its 768 px Menu row, and `e2e/sim.spec.ts`.
- **Lot tooltip limits** (Task 7). The existing-building tooltip (and the tap card) of a `/sim` lot gains a third line: `Allowed: N · Buildable here: M`, `Allowed: N · Protected`, or `Allowed: N` when `buildable` is null. The worker's `parcelSummary` sets `ParcelSummary.limits` (`LotLimits` in `map/types.ts`) only in a session with levers, for an eligible lot whose shown plan stories (`envelope.maxStories`) are above 0; `allowed` repeats that value so a `ParcelSummary` stays assignable to `ParcelSummaryLike` (I17), and FzpMode's `requestParcelSummary` passes `limits` through. `buildable` is the stories of the building generated at `allowed` (the host memo under `simKey(id, allowed)`, the same call as the preview and the timeline; the generator clamps to the lot's physical max), else `lotBuildableStories(polygon, allowed, existingUnits)` (the generator's null means "no gain over what stands", while the line is about what the lot's shape allows). A lot protected under the levers in force reads `Protected` with its FZP stories as Allowed (protection beats every rule and stroke). The editor host never sets `limits`, so the `/` tooltip is unchanged.

## Autosave, publish and the read-only view

Plan 5 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-30-sim-publish.md`; "Call n" in this section is that plan's), moved to schema 4 by timeline-ui: the document is `SimDoc4` (`src/lib/timeline/sim-doc4.ts`); the server side, limits and redirects are in [plan-sharing.md](plan-sharing.md), "Policies (`/sim`)". `src/routes/sim/+page.svelte` and `src/routes/s/[id]/+page.svelte` both render `SimRoute` (`src/lib/sim-page/SimRoute.svelte`: the `{#key}` remounts and the dropped `?seed=`), which renders `SimPage` with a `mode` and a `persist` setting.

- **Autosave** (`persist: 'restore'`, plain `/sim`). Every change (undo and redo included, and the title) is written under `sfhousing:sim:v2`, debounced 500 ms; a `sfhousing:sim:v1` record is carried over first. On mount `restoreSim4` (`src/lib/timeline/session4.ts`) reads it through the schema 4 codec, reconciles it with the calibration (`reconcilePolicy4`) and builds the notices: N1 to N3 in the policy wording, the timing refit, the funding notices ([sim-funding.md](sim-funding.md)), "dates moved", "upgraded", and the Remix toast. `store.reset(policy)` is not an undoable change. The header's status slot shows the save state ("Saving…", "Saved", "Autosave off").
- **Over-limit policies are not autosaved** (ruling R4). A document that fails `simDocProblems` (over `OPS_MAX` strokes or `MAX_PLAN_BYTES`) is never written, because it would load back as corrupt. The last valid write stays, and the header shows "Autosave off" until a valid write succeeds. Its popover still gives the storage reason (N4), not the size (known gap).
- **Autosave always restores** (`persist: 'restore'` in edit, `'off'` in view). The retired `start=fzp` deferral, its `fzp-kept` notice and `persist: 'deferred'` are gone: a visit restores the saved policy, and every change is saved. A corrupt or future record is kept under `sfhousing:sim:corrupt:<ISO>` before it is replaced.
- **Publish** sits in the header with Undo and Redo. It is disabled on the default policy ("Change the policy first.", Call 14) and posts the schema 4 document to `/api/policies`; the link is `/s/<id>`.
- **The read-only view** (`/s/<id>`, `mode: 'view'`, `persist: 'off'`). The route's shared banner (Remix, Start your own) sits above the map, then the same screen with `readonly`: nothing can be edited, no tools, no Publish, no autosave, and selecting a policy shows its settings read-only (`PolicySummary` of the span). The shared document is reconciled with the calibration it runs on, and the "dates moved" and timing notices show when anything moved. Viewing never writes the local policy: only Remix does, then goes to `/sim`.
- **Notices** (`SimNotices.svelte`) render in `TimelineMode`'s notice pill stack (`NoticePills`, an overlay at the map's top from md, below the status line; in flow under the header below md), one pill each (`PILL`, `PILL_ACTION`) with Details where there is a list. Toasts dismiss after 8 s. The polite status region mounts empty with the ready-phase notice stack and takes the notices present on load 100 ms later (`LIVE_DELAY_MS`), so a notice present on load is announced: a screen reader announces a change to a live region, not one that mounts already populated. The funding notices come after N1 to N3 and before the timing notice; their text and order are in [sim-funding.md](sim-funding.md) ("Notices on load"). The session's own notices (rules 33 to 36) are in [timeline-ui.md](timeline-ui.md).

## Stop types and bands

Transit stop types (Call 7, `src/lib/sim/levers/brushes.ts`), read from `transit.json` patterns:

| Stop type      | Rule                                                                                                 | Tier                                           |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `regionalRail` | a stop on a `heavy-rail` or `commuter-rail` pattern (BART, Caltrain)                                 | 0                                              |
| `metro`        | a stop on a `light-rail` pattern (Muni Metro; the F streetcar and cable cars are not Metro)          | 0                                              |
| `rapidBus`     | a Muni stop on a bus route whose id matches `RAPID_ROUTE` (`/^\d+R$/`: 5R, 9R, 14R, ...)             | 1                                              |
| `frequentBus`  | a bus stop whose summed peak trips per hour over its bus patterns is at least `FREQUENT_BUS_TPH = 6` | 4 (no SFMTA frequent-network definition cited) |

- **Distance** is straight-line from the lot's label point to the stop. Tier 4: SB 79 measures from the parcel edge, and `parcels.bin` carries no outline.
- **Bands** (`DEFAULT_BANDS`, tier 4): a quarter mile (402 m) at 6 stories and a half mile (805 m) at 4 stories, SB 79's band edges. Bands never get taller with distance (partner ruling): compared by distance, whatever order they were entered in, each band is at most as tall as the band inside it, so the innermost band wins. `bandProblem` checks it: the Transit tab's editor cannot produce such a rule (`transit-edit.ts`), and the parser rejects one at `rules[i].bands`. Two bands at one distance are rejected too (the second could never select a lot).

## Request flow

- **Resolution and sending.** `PolicySync` (`src/lib/timeline/policy-sync.svelte.ts`) asks the worker for each query `queriesOf4(policy)` lists (keyed as `queryKey` keys them) with `resolveBrush`, caches the answers, and settles `lots` 250 ms after the last change; `open` (the opening document) settles at once. A `no-session` answer (no client, a crash, an ended session) is parked, not a failure, until `resume()`; the row then reads "Finding lots…". Other failures read "Couldn't find lots" with Retry. `TimelineSession` then sends the pair to `TimelineClient`; a document whose lots are unresolved at open goes out provisionally ([timeline-ui.md](timeline-ui.md)).
- **Catalog.** The pickers read `brushCatalog` (district families with lot counts, neighborhoods, stop counts, and `has.*` for each optional file). SimPage asks for it once, once a session is up, and again after a session restart if it came back null. Without transit data, or while the catalog loads, "Add transit layer" is `aria-disabled` with its reason. (The catalog still carries districts and neighborhoods for the worker's `area` query, which the policy no longer uses.)
- **Lazy files.** The worker fetches `transit.json` and `neighborhoods.geojson` once per session, on the first request that needs them (the catalog needs both).

## Dataset requirement

`/sim` needs a dataset with `policy.bin`, the first being `MIN_SIM_DATASET = '2026-10-29-0fd62c4d'` (`scripts/dataset-version.spec.ts`). That test fails `test:unit` if the tree's computed dataset version predates it (versions are date-prefixed, so the date orders them). There is no missing-data fallback (Call 16): a dataset without `policy.bin` gets the worker's `scenario-missing` answer, shown as "The simulation data for dataset … is missing or damaged" with Retry.

**Ordering.** Code and dataset ship together: the build computes the dataset version from the tree, and a deploy is refused unless that dataset is published ([deploy.md](deploy.md), "Datasets ship with their code"). A change that needs a newer dataset builds and uploads it before merge ([pipeline.md](pipeline.md), "Finishing a branch that changes the dataset"); there is no separate data bump to order.

## Known gaps

- **Map overlays.** A vacant lot has no tooltip (picking reads the buildings layer, `SimMap.svelte`), so the limits line never shows on one. The glass lids write no depth, so overlapping glaze at a step blends twice. Lids cover the view clamped to 3 km around its centre (from z14.5), so on a steeply pitched or very wide view the far reaches have no lids (and, past the fade, no contours either). The outlines and the live preview cover loaded chunks only (`missing` counts the rest; outlines re-ask on `onchunks`). The live preview shows at most `PREVIEW_MAX_LOTS` (300) lots, the nearest the brush ring.
- **Contours zoomed out can miss a single exception lot.** The contours are traced on a 15 m grid of lot label points, so a single 25 ft exception lot may own no cell and draw no contour below the fade. Zoomed in, the lids always show it as its own region.
- **Neighborhood edges** (Call 8). Neighborhood membership is the label point in the polygon, which can differ from DataSF's `analysis_neighborhood` at polygon edges. A `neighborhood` column in `policy.bin` is the exact fix at the next dataset bump.
- **In-flight `chanceShift` on the map.** The map's bars read the cohort's lookups, but the in-flight `chanceShift` for the stage a project is in at `asOf` under `'all'` timers is not applied on the map; cards apply it (Call 15).
- **No keyboard access to cards** (unchanged from `/fzp`): map picking is pointer-only ([map-interfaces.md](map-interfaces.md)). Freehand strokes have keyboard and non-drag alternatives (the transit layers, edited in the Transit tab, and the keyboard stamp `BrushController.stampAtCenter`).
