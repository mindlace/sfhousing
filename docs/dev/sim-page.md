# /sim: the policy simulation page

Developer notes for `src/routes/sim/` and `src/lib/sim-page/` (plan 3 of 5, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-29-sim-page.md`; the calls below are that plan's "Calls made in this plan"). The page is `FzpMode` (`src/lib/fzp/`, see [fzp-mode.md](fzp-mode.md)) running the lever engine ([sim-levers.md](sim-levers.md)) on a policy the user edits in a panel. `/fzp` is a 307 redirect to `/sim` with every query parameter kept except the retired `start` (Call 14). `/sim` always opens on `DEFAULT_LEVERS`: FZP as written, compared with pre-FZP zoning (the engine is FZP-only; there is no current law).

## The recipe and its compile rules

The page edits a `SimPolicy` (`src/lib/sim-page/policy.ts`, schema 2), a recipe of rule and stroke parameters, not lot lists (Call 1). `compileLevers` turns it into the FZP-only `SimLevers` ([sim-levers.md](sim-levers.md)) once every lot query has resolved (`null` until then). The recipe is what plan 5 autosaves and publishes (inside a `SimDoc`, "Autosave, publish and the read-only view" below); `timing` is plan 4's, edited in "Permitting timelines".

| Field     | Meaning                                                                                                                                   |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `rules`   | New zoning rules: `transit` (stop types, and distance bands each with stories)                                                            |
| `custom`  | Custom zoning, in stroke order: `paint` (strokes and the stories they allow) and `erase` (strokes)                                        |
| `protect` | The categories: `rentControl`, `historic`, `newerThanYears`                                                                               |
| `timing`  | `current` (preset, preset timers and shares, custom edits and shares, Local Program) and `saved` (plan 4; [sim-timers.md](sim-timers.md)) |

A stored `street` rule (from before 2026-11) is dropped on load; publishing one is `bad-field`.

Compile rules (sim runs spec, section 3; "Call n" is plan 3's):

- **Queries resolve in the worker** (Call 2) to scenario-eligible lots only (Call 3). A stroke covers a lot whose label point is within the radius of the path, as on `/`. `ruleQueries` gives a rule's queries (a transit rule: one per band, outermost first), `customQuery` a custom brush's one freehand query, and `queriesOf` every query the policy needs, keyed by canonical JSON.
- **Rules are additive:** each rule compiles to `SimLevers.rules` in list order, one edit per transit band; the engine gives each lot `max(FZP, N)` over every rule that names it, whatever their order. Rules have no reorder buttons. An edit left with no lots is not emitted.
- **Custom zoning paints in order:** each `paint` brush compiles to `SimLevers.paint` and sets its lots to exactly N (raising or lowering), a later brush winning; an `erase` brush removes its lots from the paint before it, never from the rules or later paint. Move up and Move down reorder custom brushes. A Paint stroke joins the last custom brush when that one paints the same stories, an Erase stroke the last erase brush; otherwise it starts a new brush. `paintedLots` gives lot → painted stories after erasing.
- **No modes or density limits:** rules and paint are uncapped by density (the engine keeps FZP's own caps on the baseline).
- **Stories** are the editor's 1 to 80 (Call 6).
- **Built height:** the generator builds at most the lot's physical max stories (`physicalMaxStories`, `src/lib/generator/physical.ts`), so built height is min(allowed N, physical max), then the typology cascade. Recorded projects are not capped.
- **Transit rules:** see "Stop types and bands" below, Calls 8 to 10.
- **Protection** (Call 11) is the categories only. A protected lot is **not redevelopable** (partner ruling 2026-10-29): protection beats every rule and stroke. Projects already in the pipeline on a protected lot carry on (Call 21).
- **The twin:** `compare` is `'preFzp'` when the policy equals `DEFAULT_POLICY` (`isDefaultPolicy`, ignoring the timeline Save snapshot), else `'fzp'`. Any customisation, a timing change alone included, compares with FZP as written; undoing back to the default compares with pre-FZP again.
- **Map feedback**: the lot dots and their map key are gone. The overlay mux (`overlay-mux.ts`) has the slots `feedback`, `limits`, `outline` and `ring` (drawn in that order, bottom to top); `SimPage` creates it per map handle in both modes. The ring, the height limits, the eraser's outlines and the paint feedback are described under "Map overlays" below.
- **Map lookups** (Call 13): the map gets the worker's posted `lookups.fresh`; each card picks `inFlight` or `fresh` for its project.
- **No local persistence** (Call 15): **superseded by plan 5.** The policy autosaves and publishes; see "Autosave, publish and the read-only view" below.
- **Lever sends are debounced 250 ms** after the last change once every lot is resolved (Call 18); the worker lets the latest `setLevers` win.

`PolicyStore` (`policy-store.svelte.ts`) keeps the recipe with undo and redo: every change is one labelled store change, so the Undo and Redo buttons name it and Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (the `/` editor's keys, through `BrushController`) step through the same history.

## Setup and Run

Spec §1 (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-sim-runs-design.md`). `SimPage` owns a `RunPhase` (`run-phase.svelte.ts`, `phase: 'setup' | 'run'`), which starts in `'setup'` and is never persisted: a reload, a restored autosave and `/s/<id>` all open in Setup.

- **Setup.** The policy is editable. FzpMode pins the day at the present (`calibration.asOf`) and the scrubber's button reads "Run"; the range and the markers are disabled. The worker keeps computing as before: `LeverSync` and `setLevers` are unchanged.
- **Run** (the scrubber's button, FzpMode's `onrun`): `startRun()` sets the tool to Move, sets `store.locked` (`PolicyStore.apply`, `undo` and `redo` become no-ops, so the controller's Ctrl+Z changes nothing) and moves to `'run'`. FzpMode plays once the score is current for the latest policy: nothing pending in `LeverSync` (`sync.pending`: the 250 ms debounce armed or a lot query in flight, passed as `leversPending`) and no levers sent unanswered. Until then the button still reads "Run" (`aria-busy`), so a second press plays nothing older, and the score bar shows the pending state. A failed revision leaves the older score standing, and Run plays it, as the score bar does. The tool stays Move during a run (the controller's tool keys are put back) and the phone tool bar shows only in Setup.
- **Read-only pane.** `PolicyPanel` takes `readonly`, `onreset` and `resetLabel`: a button at the top of the pane ("Change policy", or "Back to start" in the view), no history row, and the line "Read-only during a run". The sections read `policyReadonly()` (`policy-readonly.ts`, a context from `PolicyPanel`) and wrap their editing controls in `ReadonlyFieldset` (a `<fieldset disabled class="contents">` described by that line), so every control stays readable and focus skips it. Controls that only show information stay usable: the disclosure toggles, a timeline's stage toggles and their details, and a lot lookup's Retry. A timeline's drag handles hide. The stroke adapter's `readOnly` is `store.locked`, so the controller refuses strokes, undo and redo. After the reset, focus moves to the first section toggle. Scrubbing a run stays allowed.
- **Change policy / Back to start** (`resetRun()`): unlocks the store and returns to Setup; FzpMode pauses and returns the day to the present. Scrubbing back to Today does the same: FzpMode's `onrewind` (the scrubber's `ontoday` during a run: Home, the Today marker, a key or drag landing on `asOf`) is wired to `resetRun`. Replay at the horizon is not a rewind. The policy is intact. The next Run starts over from the present and waits for the latest policy's timeline.
- **e2e.** `gotoSim(page, path, { run: true })` and `gotoFzp(page, search, { run: true })` press Run, wait for playback, press Pause, and wait for `runPhase === 'run'` and `playing === false` (`startRun` in `e2e/helpers/run.ts`). The day is then at or just past `asOf`, and the run stays in Run: Home or the Today marker would return to Setup, so a test needing `asOf` inside a run reads `__sfhFzp.day`. Tests that scrub, play or press a marker pass `run: true`.

## Map overlays

Sim map overlays plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-31-sim-runs-2-map-overlays.md`), Task 4. Everything here shows in Setup only, in both modes (`/sim` and the read-only `/s/<id>`).

- **View menu.** The header actions start with `ViewMenu` while `run.phase === 'setup'`: rows "Transit stops", "Transit capacity within a 10-minute walk" and "Zoning height limits" (on by default; Setup only), or only "Zoning height limits" when the manifest the map renders (FzpMode's `onmanifest`, which follows a Retry) has no `transit`. Its swatches and the limits layer share one `useSystemTheme()`.
- **Prefs.** `viewPrefs` is read once with `readViewPrefs(storage())` and every change is written with `writeViewPrefs` to the same storage (SimPage's `storage` prop, default `localStorage`; the test host passes an in-memory one). It is the `sfhousing:view` record `/` uses, so a viewer's transit choice follows them between modes. A record from the fog's time (`heightFog`) still reads as `heightLimits`. A run keeps the toggles: FzpMode gets `view = run.phase === 'setup' ? viewPrefs : HIDDEN_VIEW`.
- **Height limits.** `city` is the latest `HeightCity` from FzpMode's `onheights`; `lids` is the latest `heightLids` answer for the view. One effect writes the overlay mux's `limits` slot: `[heightLimitsLayer({ city, lids, mode: limitsMode, theme })]` in Setup with `heightLimits` on, else `[]`. See [map-interfaces.md](map-interfaces.md), "Height limits". Contours show city-wide as soon as the city is in; from z14.5 `LidRequest` asks the worker for glass lids on each `moveend` (view bbox clamped 3 km around the view centre) and keeps the last answer, so a theme or tool change asks nothing. The height is the effective table (FZP plus rules plus paint); a painted lot under a category protection shows its FZP height (ruling M5: protection beats every rule and stroke).
- **Mode.** `limitsMode` is `plain` by default; `paint` while the Paint or Erase tool is chosen in `/sim` (paint-set limits are dashed, so the brush's effect can be found); and `rule` while a rule is selected, which wins over the tool.
- **Rule selection.** A zoning rule card has a "Show on map" toggle (`aria-pressed`, `onselect`). Pressing it selects that rule (`pickedRule`) and turns the limits view on if it was off; releasing it clears the selection. The selection clears in the same render when the rule leaves the policy (Remove, Undo, a restore), so no frame dims everything for a rule that is gone, and a Redo does not bring it back. The map dims other lines and labels and shows the rule's lots; see the modes in [map-interfaces.md](map-interfaces.md).
- **Limits colours.** Light theme: `limit-ink` `#2B2A28`, `limit-glaze` `rgba(72,74,78,0.22)`, `limit-wall` `rgba(72,74,78,0.25)`, `limit-halo` `#F3EFE6`; dark theme: `#E8E4DC`, `rgba(214,216,220,0.12)`, `rgba(214,216,220,0.18)`, `#1C1D1F` (`src/lib/map/palette.ts`, with contrast tests in `palette.spec.ts`). Glaze is a neutral grey with no hue; its alpha steps with the room to grow (`GLAZE_ALPHA`). The old `height-fog` token is gone.
- **Outlines while erasing** (Task 5). While the Erase tool is chosen in `/sim` (`mode === 'edit'`), the custom-painted lots are outlined: one dashed ring per lot (4 m dash, 3 m gap, 2 px, the `painted-outline` token), in the mux's `outline` slot, layer id `sim-painted-outline`. The lots are `paintedLots(store.policy, lots)` (erase strokes already removed), where `lots` reads `LeverSync.result`. While that returns null (a stroke's query is unresolved) the last wanted set stands, so the outlines do not blink after each stroke. The geometry comes from a worker request, `lotOutlines` (`ScenarioClient.lotOutlines`, `SimClientApi.lotOutlines`): rings of the loaded lots as transferred typed arrays (`ringLot`, `starts`, `positions`), plus `missing`, the count of lots whose chunk is not loaded yet. `OutlineRequest` (`outline-request.ts`) sends it for a changed set only, drops an older answer, and asks again on FzpMode's `onchunks` while the last answer had `missing` lots (or failed with `no-session`). `SimPage` keeps the last answer, so a theme change redraws without a new request. Answered only by a session with levers (`no-session` otherwise).
- **Paint feedback** (Task 6). In Setup a Paint stroke, and a zoning rule saved from its form once its revision is `current`, briefly show the generated buildings their lots in view could hold: one deck `SolidPolygonLayer` (layer id `sim-feedback-<n>`, `pickable: false`) in the mux's `feedback` slot, full strength for `FEEDBACK_HOLD_MS` (500 ms), then a linear fade over `FEEDBACK_FADE_MS` (1500 ms), then removed; under reduced motion it holds at full strength and is removed at the same time (`feedback.ts`: `feedbackOpacity`, `PaintFeedback`). A new feedback replaces the one showing, and requests are latest-wins (a sequence number). The geometry comes from a worker request, `previewBuildings` (`ScenarioClient.previewBuildings`, `SimClientApi.previewBuildings`; `no-session` without a lever session): per lot, of the first `PREVIEW_MAX_LOTS` (300, `src/lib/sim/scenario/preview.ts`, shared by the host and the page), a lot protected under the levers in force builds nothing (`LeverWork.protectedMask`, set at `current` from `protectedRows`); a lot whose record is not loaded counts as `missing`; with `stories` (a stroke) it builds at that height, clamped by the generator at the lot's physical max; without (a rule) only a lot whose plan stories differ from FZP's stock builds, at the plan stories. The buildings are `generate(scenarioRecordOf(…), { maxStories }, fnv1a32(id), 'max')` through the host's generator memo under `simKey(id, stories)` (the same call and key as the timeline's simulated buildings, so either warms the other), packed as `packCell('preview', …)` with the plan's existing units and drawn by `packedCellLayer` (`generated-controller.ts`, shared with the controller's own layers). A stroke resolves alone (`customQuery` of a one-stroke brush, as it may join an earlier brush), and the page sends its lots in the map's bounds nearest the centre first (`lotsInView`). A rule's lots are its `ruleQueries` results from `LeverSync`. `RuleFeedbackTrigger` arms only when the form's save changed the policy (both save paths: RulesSection's inline form from md through its `onsaved(rule, changed)`, the sheet's form below md through `saveRuleBrush`) (an unchanged edit sends no revision) and fires on the first `current` above the latest lever rev seen at the save. A run clears the feedback and drops answers still on their way; the store is locked then, so no stroke can trigger one. The handler is synchronous: 300 cold lots measured 13 to 26 ms on Node against `SLICE_MS` (`scenario.bench.spec.ts`, "previewBuildings budget"). Before a session's first `current` the mask is not set, so previews apply no protections (the default levers protect none; a restored policy with protections painted in its first seconds can briefly show buildings on a protected lot). The feedback does not hide the existing building under it, because the timeline controller's demolish pump owns the `replaced` feature state.
- **Header at 768 px.** View, Policy and Another future tighten their side padding from md to lg (`md:max-lg:px-1`), so the Setup header (View included, with "Autosave off", "Share link" and a five-digit score) still fits at 768 px with the score centred; removing View in a run does not move the score (the header's three-column grid). Checked by the SiteHeader spec's 768 px row and `e2e/sim.spec.ts` ("at 768 px the header fits…").
- **Lot tooltip limits** (Task 7). The existing-building tooltip (and the tap card) of a `/sim` lot gains a third line: `Allowed: N · Buildable here: M`, `Allowed: N · Protected`, or `Allowed: N` when `buildable` is null. The worker's `parcelSummary` sets `ParcelSummary.limits` (`LotLimits` in `map/types.ts`) only in a session with levers, for an eligible lot whose shown plan stories (`envelope.maxStories`) are above 0; `allowed` repeats that value so a `ParcelSummary` stays assignable to `ParcelSummaryLike` (I17), and FzpMode's `requestParcelSummary` passes `limits` through. `buildable` is the stories of the building generated at `allowed` (the host memo under `simKey(id, allowed)`, the same call as the preview and the timeline; the generator clamps to the lot's physical max), else `lotBuildableStories(polygon, allowed, existingUnits)` (the generator's null means "no gain over what stands", while the line is about what the lot's shape allows). A lot protected under the levers in force reads `Protected` with its FZP stories as Allowed (protection beats every rule and stroke). The editor host never sets `limits`, so the `/` tooltip is unchanged.

## Autosave, publish and the read-only view

Plan 5 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-30-sim-publish.md`; "Call n" in this section is that plan's). The document is `SimDoc` with `SIM_CODEC` (`src/lib/sim-page/sim-doc.ts`); the server side, limits and redirects are in [plan-sharing.md](plan-sharing.md), "Policies (`/sim`)". `src/routes/sim/+page.svelte` and `src/routes/s/[id]/+page.svelte` both render `SimRoute` (`src/lib/sim-page/SimRoute.svelte`: the seed handling and `{#key}` remounts), which renders `SimPage` with a `mode` and a `persist` setting.

- **Autosave** (`persist: 'restore'`, plain `/sim`). Every change (undo and redo included, and the title) is written under `sfhousing:sim:v1`, debounced 500 ms. On mount, `restore()` reads it: `store.reset(policy)` (not an undoable change), `levers = compileLevers(policy, () => [])` until the brushes resolve, then the full levers (Call 12). `restoreSimDoc` (`sim-session.ts`) reconciles the timings with the page's calibration and builds the notices: N1 to N3 in the policy wording, the timing refit, and the Remix toast. The header's status slot shows the save state ("Saving…", "Saved", "Autosave off").
- **Over-limit policies are not autosaved** (ruling R4). A document that fails `simDocProblems` (over `OPS_MAX` strokes or `MAX_PLAN_BYTES`) is never written, because it would load back as corrupt. The last valid write stays, and the header shows "Autosave off" until a valid write succeeds. Its popover still gives the storage reason (N4), not the size (known gap).
- **Autosave always restores** (`persist: 'restore'` in edit, `'off'` in view). The retired `start=fzp` deferral, its `fzp-kept` notice and `persist: 'deferred'` are gone: a visit restores the saved policy, and every change is saved. A corrupt or future record is kept under `sfhousing:sim:corrupt:<ISO>` before it is replaced.
- **Publish** sits in the header beside Policy. It is disabled on the default policy ("Change the policy first.", Call 14) and posts `SIM_CODEC.publishable(doc)` (the Save snapshot stripped) to `/api/policies`. The link is `/s/<id>`.
- **The read-only view** (`/s/<id>`, `mode: 'view'`, `persist: 'off'`). The shared banner sits above the map. The panel shows `PolicySummary` under "Policy (read-only)" (Call 15): the new zoning rules, the custom brushes (each with its lots), the protections, the timing label and the shares and changed stages behind a disclosure. There are no tools, no undo, no Publish and no autosave. The shared policy is reconciled with the calibration it runs on, and the timing notice shows when anything moved. Viewing never writes the local policy: only Remix does, then goes to `/sim`.
- **Notices** (`SimNotices.svelte`) render in FzpMode's notice pill stack (`NoticePills`, an overlay at the map's top from md, in flow under the header below md; see [fzp-mode.md](fzp-mode.md) "Layout variables"), one pill each (`PILL`, `PILL_ACTION`) with Details where there is a list. Toasts dismiss after 8 s. The polite status region mounts empty with the ready-phase notice stack and takes the notices present on load 100 ms later (`LIVE_DELAY_MS`), so a notice present on load (the timing refit on `/s/<id>`, a restore or Remix notice) is announced: a screen reader announces a change to a live region, not one that mounts already populated. `SimNotices.svelte.spec.ts` and `e2e/sim-share.spec.ts` check that the region is in the document empty before the notice is inserted.

## Stop types and bands

Transit stop types (Call 7, `src/lib/sim/levers/brushes.ts`), read from `transit.json` patterns:

| Stop type      | Rule                                                                                                 | Tier                                           |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `regionalRail` | a stop on a `heavy-rail` or `commuter-rail` pattern (BART, Caltrain)                                 | 0                                              |
| `metro`        | a stop on a `light-rail` pattern (Muni Metro; the F streetcar and cable cars are not Metro)          | 0                                              |
| `rapidBus`     | a Muni stop on a bus route whose id matches `RAPID_ROUTE` (`/^\d+R$/`: 5R, 9R, 14R, ...)             | 1                                              |
| `frequentBus`  | a bus stop whose summed peak trips per hour over its bus patterns is at least `FREQUENT_BUS_TPH = 6` | 4 (no SFMTA frequent-network definition cited) |

- **Distance** is straight-line from the lot's label point to the stop. Tier 4: SB 79 measures from the parcel edge, and `parcels.bin` carries no outline.
- **Bands** (`DEFAULT_BANDS`, tier 4): a quarter mile (402 m) at 6 stories and a half mile (805 m) at 4 stories, SB 79's band edges. Bands never get taller with distance (partner ruling): compared by distance, whatever order they were entered in, each band is at most as tall as the band inside it, so the innermost band wins. `bandProblem` checks it: the form shows "An outer band can't be taller than the band inside it" and the parser rejects the rule at `rules[i].bands`. Two bands at one distance are rejected too (the second could never select a lot).

## Request flow

- **Resolution.** `LeverSync` (`policy-store.svelte.ts`) asks the worker for each query the recipe needs (`queriesOf`, keyed by canonical JSON) with `resolveBrush { reqId, query }`, and caches the answers (`brushLots`: lots and their label points). A `no-session` answer (no client yet, a crash, an ended session) is parked, not a failure, until `resume()` on the next session; a card then reads "Finding lots…". Other failures read "Couldn't find lots" with Retry.
- **Catalog.** The pickers read `brushCatalog` (district families with lot counts, neighborhoods, stop counts, and `has.*` for each optional file). SimPage asks for it once, when the panel first opens, and again after a session restart if it came back null. Without transit data, or while the catalog loads, "Add transit rule" is `aria-disabled` with its reason. (The catalog still carries districts and neighborhoods for the worker's `area` query, which the policy no longer uses.)
- **Lazy files.** The worker fetches `transit.json` and `neighborhoods.geojson` once per session, on the first request that needs them (the catalog needs both). `policy.bin` is fetched with the scenario, because `/sim` always initialises with levers. So on md+, where the panel opens by default, the first catalog request fetches them soon after load; on a phone nothing fetches them until the sheet opens.
- **Sending.** Once every query is resolved, `compileLevers` runs and, 250 ms after the last change, the new `SimLevers` goes to FzpMode's `levers`, which sends `setLevers` (it waits while a reroll is in flight). The lever status (`lever-status.ts`) goes `resolving` (with done / total lots) and then `current`; meanwhile the notice row above the scrubber shows "Updating the simulation for your policy… {done} of {total} lots", with a Retry chip when chunks failed, and the score bar is `aria-busy`.
- **A failed revision.** If the worker cannot apply a revision (an exception in a recompute slice ends the session and posts `internal` at its rev, `scenario-host.ts`), the status goes `failed`. The notice stack then shows an alert pill, "Couldn't update the simulation for your policy.", with Retry (`data-testid="fzp-lever-failed"`, a `role="alert"` region rendered even while empty so it stays in the accessibility tree). Retry is FzpMode's `retry()`: in the ready phase it re-sends `initScenario` with the latest levers and the shown seed, and `onclient` resumes the parked brush queries. The score keeps the last run's numbers, not `aria-busy` (a busy score that never settles could be withheld by a screen reader), and the score details say "These numbers are from before your latest policy change." until a revision is current. Later edits while failed get `bad-request` and stay failed until Retry. A `bad-request` above the latest levers' rev answers a refused reroll, not the levers, so it leaves the lever status alone (`lever-status.ts`).

## Dataset requirement

`/sim` needs a dataset with `policy.bin`, the first being `MIN_SIM_DATASET = '2026-10-29-0fd62c4d'` (`scripts/wrangler-config.spec.ts`). That test fails `test:unit` if production's or staging's `PUBLIC_DATASET_VERSION` predates it (versions are date-prefixed, so the date orders them). There is no missing-data fallback (Call 16): a dataset without `policy.bin` gets the worker's `scenario-missing` answer, shown as "The simulation data for dataset … is missing or damaged" with Retry.

**Ordering (Task 0B).** Production deploys only on a release, and a release ships whatever is on `main`. So a data bump that `/sim` needs merges to `main`, and is released, **before** the code that needs it merges. Any release that contains the code then also contains the bump, even when the two land in one release. If a later change needs a newer dataset, promote it the same way first ([deploy.md](deploy.md), "Promoting a datasetVersion").

## The debug hook's `levers` field

`window.__sfhFzp` (VITE_E2E builds only, [fzp-mode.md](fzp-mode.md)) has a `levers` field on `/sim`:

| Field     | Meaning                                                                      |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `status`  | `'off'` (a session without levers), `'resolving'`, `'current'` or `'failed'` |
| `done`    | lots recomputed so far while resolving (0 otherwise)                         |
| `total`   | lots to recompute while resolving (0 otherwise)                              |
| `sentRev` | the revision of the latest levers sent, or null                              |

A test waits for a policy change with `status === 'current'` and a `sentRev` above the one read before the change. The lever notices are in `data-testid="fzp-lever-status"`, the session notices in `data-testid="sim-notices"`. The e2e specs are `e2e/sim.spec.ts` and, for autosave, publish and `/s/<id>`, `e2e/sim-share.spec.ts`.

## Known gaps

- **Map overlays.** A vacant lot has no tooltip (picking reads the buildings layer, `SimMap.svelte`), so the limits line never shows on one. The glass lids write no depth, so overlapping glaze at a step blends twice. Lids cover the view clamped to 3 km around its centre (from z14.5), so on a steeply pitched or very wide view the far reaches have no lids (and, past the fade, no contours either). The outlines and the paint feedback cover loaded chunks only (`missing` counts the rest; outlines re-ask on `onchunks`). Rule feedback shows at most `PREVIEW_MAX_LOTS` (300) lots, the nearest the view centre.
- **Contours zoomed out can miss a single exception lot.** The contours are traced on a 15 m grid of lot label points, so a single 25 ft exception lot may own no cell and draw no contour below the fade. Zoomed in, the lids always show it as its own region.
- **Neighborhood edges** (Call 8). Neighborhood membership is the label point in the polygon, which can differ from DataSF's `analysis_neighborhood` at polygon edges. A `neighborhood` column in `policy.bin` is the exact fix at the next dataset bump.
- **In-flight `chanceShift` on the map.** The map's bars read the cohort's lookups, but the in-flight `chanceShift` for the stage a project is in at `asOf` under `'all'` timers is not applied on the map; cards apply it (Call 15).
- **No keyboard access to cards** (unchanged from `/fzp`): map picking is pointer-only ([fzp-mode.md](fzp-mode.md)). Freehand strokes have keyboard and non-drag alternatives (the rule brushes, and the keyboard stamp `BrushController.stampAtCenter`).
