# Public money on `/sim`: funding in a policy, presets and the Funding tab

The page side of the funding levers. Source: `src/lib/sim-page/` (`policy.ts`, `sim-doc.ts`, `sim-session.ts`, `funding-reconcile.ts`, `funding-sources.ts`, `funding-presets.ts`). Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-finance-levers-design.md` (sections 4.3, 4.5 to 4.7). The engine-facing rules (parse, `buildFinance`, the worker) are in [finance-levers.md](finance-levers.md); the lever document is in [sim-levers.md](sim-levers.md); storage, publish and the schema-3 loader are in [plan-sharing.md](plan-sharing.md). The Funding tab, the Results money and income rows, the card line and the notices (`funding-ui`) are in "The Funding tab" below.

## Funding levers in a policy

`SimPolicy.funding` (schema 3) is a `FinanceLevers` ([finance-levers.md](finance-levers.md)): scale entries on the baseline pools, pools and programmes the user adds, and typed market terms. `DEFAULT_POLICY.funding` is a separate fresh copy of `DEFAULT_LEVERS.finance`, so no caller can mutate one through the other. The parse and limit rules are in [sim-levers.md](sim-levers.md) ("Funding levers") and [finance-levers.md](finance-levers.md); how a stored or shared document loads, what the loader reports and what publish refuses are in [plan-sharing.md](plan-sharing.md) ("Policies (`/sim`)"). `compileLevers` copies the funding into the levers (`structuredClone`) and does not reconcile: reconciliation happens on load and the editor offers only pools the dataset has.

`restoreSimDoc` returns `{ doc, notices, funding: FundingLoadReport }`; the report is what the page's notices are built from (`funding-ui` owns their text). Days in a policy are absolute and are never rewritten when the dataset's `asOf` moves: money dated before `asOf` never arrives, a programme with `from` before `asOf` is active from `asOf`, and one whose `to` is before it is never active. `pastItems(f, asOf)` returns the added pools with no arrival on or after `asOf`, the programmes whose `to` is before it, and the inflows with no arrival on or after it, for the page to mark "not counted".

## Presets

`fundingPresets(cal)` builds the list against the page's calibration, because presets refer to baseline pool ids and to `asOf`. Presets shape only a policy, never published data, so the module is page code outside the dataset-version paths. Sources and their provenance live in `funding-sources.ts`, in the `Sourced`, `Unsourced` and `Evidence` shapes (`src/lib/sim/finance/evidence.ts`).

### Availability

A preset is offered (`available: true`) when all hold, else `available: false` with the first failing reason:

1. every number it presents as a fact is `Sourced`;
2. every market term its instruments read is `Sourced` in `MARKET_TERMS` (`missingTerms(inst, resolveTerms())` is empty), reason `The market terms this loan is valued against are not sourced: <term>`; presets never type a term;
3. an enacted measure it models is certified;
4. the calibration has baseline funding and a market model, reason `This data has no funding model.`

A kind the preset needs and the data lacks gives `This data has no <state|federal> pool.`. Numbers a preset leaves to the user (a bond's date, a loan's share of cost, a federal amount) are **starting values**: each has a stated default and tier and does not block availability. No preset offers a guarantee.

### Ids

A preset carries placeholder ids `u-<presetId>-1`; `addPreset` assigns the real ones. A preset's pool and its programme are both `u-<presetId>-<n>` (separate id spaces), `n` the smallest positive integer for which neither id is in the policy (`nextAddedId`). The longest, `u-federal-credits-off-<n>`, is far inside 40 characters. Hand-made items are `u-pool-<n>` and `u-prog-<n>`.

`addPreset(f, p)` appends the preset's scale entries, pools and programmes, re-sorts `baselineScale`, and a scale entry on the same pool and day as an existing one is replaced. Past a `FINANCE_LIMITS` bound it throws a `RangeError`; the page disables the preset first.

### `POOL_KIND_DEFAULTS` and `valueSource`

`POOL_KIND_DEFAULTS` (`funding-sources.ts`) is one table by `PoolKind` of starting values for a pool or loan added by hand, in the `Evidence` shapes: `debt` (non-null for `goBond` 0.06 over 20 years and `cop` 0.065 over 20 years, both BLA 2026 and tier 2), `leverage` (non-null only for `bank`, 0.67, tier 2) and `loanRate` (`bank` 0.0613, tier 3; every other kind `Unsourced`: "No source gives the rate a public loan programme funded from this kind of money charges in San Francisco; a rate you type is custom, not sourced."). The `housing-bond` and `public-bank` presets take their debt terms, leverage and loan rate from this table, so the two never disagree; `revolving-fund`'s 0.06 is its own starting value.

The page reads `POOL_KIND_DEFAULTS` for a pool or loan it builds by hand: a value that still equals a `Sourced` entry shows its provenance, any other value "custom, not sourced". For an item a preset made it reads `valueSource(item, field, presets)` instead. `valueSource` returns the field's `Provenance` when the item's id is `u-<presetId>-<n>`, the preset is in `presets`, the field has a source for that preset, and the stored value equals what the preset builds now (numbers and days exactly, `tiers` key by key); otherwise `'custom'`. The fields with a source: the bond's `inflows[0].dollars` and `debt`; the general revenue and revolving fund amounts; every `htf-2026` inflow field; the bank's inflow `dollars`, `leverage` and programme `instrument.rate`; every `cityGrant` programme's `instrument.share`; and `minHomes` and `tiers` on `cityGrant` programmes and `public-bank` (the baseline's programme provenance, the last entry of `baselineFunding.provenance`). Everything else is `'custom'` from the start: every default date and every `to: null`, `federal-support`'s amount, the revolving fund's `costShare`, `rate` and `minHomes`, the bank's `costShare` and inflow days, every `requiredReturn: null`. Scale entries carry no id, are the user's policy and have no source line.

### What each preset does under the fill rule

`fundBundle` (`finance-model`, `finance-engine`) fills three tiers in order: `federal` pools, then `state`, then the City's (`bank`, `dedicatedTax`, `generalRevenue`, `goBond`, `cop`). Within a tier, loans and guarantees count first, then grants; each grant pays its full `share × uncoveredPerHome` when the tier's grants fit in what remains, else the same fraction of its share, so the tier covers exactly what remains. The figures use the expected live baseline: grant shares federal 0.51433, state 0.137419, six City pools summing to 0.348251, together 1 of the $861,525 uncovered, so $300,027 a home is the City's part; the capped pools fully fund about 780 homes a year at the City pools and about 1,267 at the state pool; the slope is about 1.75. These are illustrations (tier 2 arithmetic), not run results.

- **`housing-bond`, `general-revenue`.** The City tier's grants then sum to 0.696502 against 0.348251 remaining, so every City grant, the added one's included, pays half its share. The City's cost per home stays $300,027, so homes rise by about the added dollars over that cost: about 1,368 over the bond's life, about 122 a year for general revenue (to about 902 a year, below the state's 1,267, so the City still binds). The bond makes the state's 1,267 a year the bound while both pay. They do not change odds: the default affordable bundle is already certain.
- **`revolving-fund`.** Market projects have no baseline programme, so the bundle is the loan alone. On prototype B its grant-equivalent is about $1,942 a home, coverage about 0.009, an odds shift of about 0.5% more starts on a reached lot; the pool still commits the whole principal at the start. The grant tiers do not touch it.
- **`public-bank`.** A loan to affordable projects counts first at about $2,384 a home, so the City tier covers $297,643 in place of $300,027 and the City pools stretch to about 786 homes a year: a small gain. The bundle was already certain, so odds do not change; when the bank is short it is left out and the grants alone still cover the cost.
- **`state-off`.** From its day the state programme leaves every bundle; City grants then fit and pay full shares, so affordable coverage is 0.862581, funded affordable projects are no longer certain, and start at a log-odds shift of about 1.36. Waiting applications are re-made on that day, lapse and are drawn again. The state pool's balance stays unspent.
- **`state-double`.** Buys no homes on the baseline: the state tier's grant pays its full share and never more, and the City tier must still cover $300,027 from City money, which binds first. Extra state money accumulates. It adds homes only beside money that makes the state the bound (City money past about 1,267 a year, or `federal-support`).
- **`federal-support`.** Federal grants (0.51433 + 0.348251 = 0.862581) fit and pay in full, the state pays its 0.137419, nothing remains for the City tier, so every City grant is scaled to 0 while the added pool has money: about 500 homes a year on top of the City's 780, until the state's 1,267 binds. A smaller amount scales City grants down by the same fraction.
- **`federal-credits-off`.** State and City grants pay full shares; affordable coverage falls to 0.48567, funded affordable projects start only by chance (a shift of about 0.77), and waiting applications lapse as for `state-off`.

### Switching on a certified measure

`htf-2026` is built with its content even while held back (`mk(..., keepContent = true)`), so certifying it is one flag. When the result is certified: re-read the certified result (the Controller's statement and the Proposition C schedule), check the amounts in `PROP_C_SCHEDULE`, change the preset's `reasonOf` argument of its `grantPreset` in `funding-presets.ts` from the held-string function to `needsGrantBase`, as `general-revenue` has it, so that on data with no baseline funding or no affordable programme the preset is still held back with its reason instead of building content from a missing baseline (setting it to `null` would crash there), and reword its `description` ("it is not offered until the result is certified"). `state-bond-2026` additionally needs San Francisco's share published and content written: until then a user who wants a state bond adds a `state` pool by hand in the Funding tab with a grant programme, every amount labelled "custom, not sourced". `regional-bond` needs a measure put to voters and content.

## The Funding tab

`funding-ui` (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-funding-ui-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-11-funding-ui.md`) is the editor for everything above. It is the fifth dock tab, after Protect (`SIM_TABS`, `tabs.ts`; icon `DOCK_ICONS.funding`, a banknote). `simTabs(calibration)` offers it only when the calibration has both `market` and `baselineFunding`, the condition on which `buildSimInput` runs money at all; otherwise `tabOrFirst` falls back to the first tab. A run shows Results only, as for every tab.

The tab is one column at every width (the 320 px panel and the phone sheet render the same component), top to bottom:

1. **The coverage line**: "Affordable homes: fully funded today" when `isCertain(coverage)` at `calibration.asOf`, else "Affordable homes: {p} of the cost is covered today; they start only by chance". It is status of the policy, so it sits above every control.
2. **"Coverage over time"** (a disclosure button, `aria-expanded`): one line per date on which the bundle's coverage or the City's part changes (`coverageSteps`), then the fixed sentences (`COVERAGE_RUN_SENTENCE`, `COVERAGE_RULE_SENTENCE`, `COVERAGE_OFF_SHORT_SENTENCE`, `COVERAGE_SIZE_SENTENCE` when the bundle's programmes have different `minHomes`, `COVERAGE_PRIVATE_SENTENCE`), and last the two engine caveats, muted: R22 (`smallProjectsCaveat(minHomes)`, shown while the baseline's smallest `minHomes` is above 0) and R24 (`MONTH_OFFER_CAVEAT`). See "Known limits" below.
3. **"Today's funding"** (`h3`): one select button (`aria-pressed`) per baseline group, City, State and Federal tax credits (`baselineGroups`); a group with no pool in the dataset is not shown. The button reads "{group} · {amount}" ("$234 million a year", `NO_LIMIT` "no limit" for the uncapped pool, `SWITCHED_OFF` "switched off"), then "pays ${perHome} of each home" (with "; lends … a home, repaid at completion" when its items lend) and, when a dated change touches it, "Changes from {Month Year}". Pressing a row expands its `FundingGroupEditor` in place: the City's sources, dated changes ("+ Change from a date", "Apply change", a Remove per line), what the group pays for (the baseline programmes read-only, then added programmes on the group's pools in `ProgrammeList`, "+ What it pays for") and its `SourceList`.
4. **"Money you add"** (`h3`): one `FundingPoolRow` per added pool (`data-funding-row="pool:<id>"`, its editor `data-funding-editor`), then the open draft pool if there is one, or the muted "Nothing added yet.", then "+ Add money" (`AddMoney`, `data-funding-add`): the available presets in four `h4` groups (City money, The public bank, State money, Federal money), each with its `description` as visible text and `aria-describedby`, then "Your own pool". Presets not offered are listed under "Sources and tiers" with their reasons.
5. **"Market terms you set"** (`h3`, `MarketTerms`): rendered only when some term has no source and an offered instrument needs it, or the policy already holds a typed term (today: the guarantee's rate cut).
6. **"Sources and tiers"**: a closed `details` with the baseline's provenance and its check line, each added preset's provenance, every custom evidence value and typed term, `MARKET_TERMS` (unsourced ones as "No sourced value: {reason}"), the draw assumption of ruling R11, the pricing rule, the presets not offered and the affordable cost line from `AFFORDABLE`.

One row is selected at a time; selecting scrolls it into view (`block: 'nearest'`). A programme's editor is `ProgrammeEditor`, nested under its pool's row (`data-prog-row="<id>"`, a new draft programme `"draft"`; its editor `data-programme-editor`). A programme's pool is the pool it is nested under; only under the City group does a "Pays from" select choose a City pool.

### The one pricing rule

The page never adds shares itself. Every coverage figure and every per-home split it shows is `fundBundle` (`finance-model`) on `bundleOn(day)` (`funding-view.ts`): the affordable programmes active on that day, baseline first in dataset order then the added ones in policy order, whose pool is not switched off that day, priced with `resolveTerms(MARKET_TERMS, policy.funding.terms)`. That is the function the engine prices bundles with, so the tab cannot disagree with a run about what a bundle is worth: federal pools first, then state, then the City's; within a tier, loans and guarantees count first, then grants pay their share when they fit, otherwise the same fraction of it. The City's part is the sum of the City items' `grantEquivalent` (a grant's `granted`, a loan's worth, never the principal lent). A programme whose instrument needs a term that resolves to null (`missingTerms`) is left out of the bundle and its row shows the alert `GUARANTEE_NEEDS_TERM`.

### "Switched off" against "short"

A `baselineScale` entry with scale 0 switches a pool off from its day, capped or uncapped: its programmes leave every bundle, so coverage falls and projects go ahead on the rest at lower odds, and the line shows it. A scale above 0 changes only how much money the pool receives, never what a programme pays per home, so it never changes the coverage line: a pool that is on but runs short keeps projects waiting in a run, and only a run shows that. The coverage disclosure says both in `COVERAGE_OFF_SHORT_SENTENCE`. On the uncapped federal pool a dated change is a two-option radio group ("Switched off" / "Switched back on", scale 0 or 1); on a capped group it is "Amount, % of today's" (0 to 1,000, step 5, default 0). A City-group change writes one entry per City pool as one store change and shows as one line, "City money".

### Evidence and choice fields

Each numeric field is either **evidence** (a claim about the world: a bond's rate and years, a bank's lending per dollar, a loan's rate, an income mix) or a **choice** (the user's proposal: names, kinds, amounts, dates, shares on a hand-made item). Evidence fields carry a muted source line: `sourcedLine` "From {label} (tier {n})" while the value equals its source, else `customLine` "Your value (custom, not sourced). Sourced: {value} from {label}.", or "Custom, not sourced." where there is no source. How the page decides (`fieldSource`, `tiersSource` in `funding-view.ts`):

- **An item a preset made** (`u-<presetId>-<n>`): `valueSource(item, field, presets)` (above). Every field it covers shows a line, choice or evidence, so a preset's tier 4 starting values (the loan presets' 10% of cost, the revolving fund's 6%, the federal amount) read "Custom, not sourced." from the start.
- **A hand-made item** (`u-pool-<n>`, `u-prog-<n>`): the evidence fields are compared with `POOL_KIND_DEFAULTS` for the pool's kind; the source shows while the value equals a `Sourced` entry, and an `Unsourced` entry is always custom. Choice fields have no line. The income mix shows its baseline source while it equals the baseline's tiers, else `YOUR_MIX_LINE`.

A loan on a pool whose `POOL_KIND_DEFAULTS[kind].loanRate` is `Unsourced` (every kind but `bank`) starts with an empty rate: the page never fills in a number that has no source.

### Market terms you set

`FinanceLevers.terms` holds the terms the user typed. A term is offered when it is `Unsourced` in `MARKET_TERMS` and an instrument the editor offers needs it (`termsNeeded`), or when the policy already holds it. Today that is `guaranteeRateCut`: an input in percentage points, 0 to 5, step 0.05, stored as the fraction. Typed, it reads `YOUR_VALUE_LINE` with "Clear"; typed over a sourced term (only from a loaded document), it offers "Use the sourced value"; invalid text stays with `RATE_CUT_ERROR` and nothing reaches the store; while a guarantee in the policy uses it, "Clear" is `aria-disabled` (`TERM_IN_USE_DESC`). While the term is empty the "Loan guarantee" option is `aria-disabled` (`GUARANTEE_DISABLED_DESC`) and a "Go to Market terms you set" button follows the radio group.

### Previews

The page computes before a run only what follows from the policy and the sourced constants: coverage and the City's part (`coverageSteps`), what a programme pays alone and in the bundle (`programmePreview`: `grantEquivalentPerHome`, `fundBundle`), a private programme's range over prototypes A to E and its odds change (`oddsShift` with `calibration.market.slope.perCostShare`), bond repayment (`poolPreview`: the level payment from `finance-model`'s debt-service function, the one nominal figure, and it says so) and a bank's lending capacity. **The previews ignore pool balances**: homes funded, money left, a pool running dry, displacement and legal limits need the queue and appear in Results only, which the coverage disclosure says once (`COVERAGE_RUN_SENTENCE`).

### Fields in short

Every field commits on `change` (and Enter); a value that fails its check is not applied, keeps the typed text, sets `aria-invalid` and fills the field's always-present `role="alert"` line. Committing text that still equals the stored value (`isUnchanged`) is no edit, so an untouched preset value never fails or is rewritten. Number inputs use `step="any"` and show the stored value at full precision (`fieldText`: fractions × 100, dollars in millions to six decimals). The parsers and messages are in `funding-edit.ts` and `funding-copy.ts`.

| Programme   | Loan interest rate                              | 0 to 25%                                                                           | the pool kind's `loanRate` when `Sourced`, else empty (a draft)                                   |
| Programme   | Lender accepts a lower return                   | 1 to 25%                                                                           | unchecked; 5.25% when checked                                                                     |
| Programme   | Reserve held                                    | 0 to 100% of the loan                                                              | 10%                                                                                               |
| Programme   | From / Until                                    | as for a pool                                                                      | the pool's first inflow; no end                                                                   |
| Programme   | Projects of at least, homes                     | whole number 0 to 10,000                                                           | the baseline's `minHomes` (affordable), 10 (private)                                              |
| Programme   | Income mix (affordable)                         | four whole percents summing to 100, rescaled by largest remainder (`rescaleTiers`) | the baseline's tiers                                                                              |
| Market term | Guarantee rate cut                              | 0 to 5 points, step 0.05                                                           | empty                                                                                             |

Every bound is the row's own or the `finance-levers` parser's, so any value a preset or stored document holds passes the field's check. The limits of `FINANCE_LIMITS` (12 added pools, 24 programmes, 24 scale entries, 12 inflows a pool) make the add buttons `aria-disabled` with a description (`limitProblem`); a preset that would pass one is refused with `limitAlert` in the chooser's alert line.

### Drafts

A policy holds only complete pools and programmes (the parser needs 1 to 12 whole-dollar inflows and a finite loan rate), so an unfinished item is a **draft** (`Draft` in `funding-edit.ts`): "Your own pool" (its amount is empty), a new programme that needs a rate with no source, or a stored programme switched to such a loan (the stored one stays until the draft completes). A draft shows `DRAFT_LINE` and a "Discard {name}" button; when its last required field is valid, `completeDraft` adds it in one store change ("Added: your own pool", "Added: {sentence}", or "Changed: {sentence}") with an id from `nextAddedId` at that moment. One at a time: while a draft is open, "+ Add money", the presets and every "+ What it pays for" are `aria-disabled` (`DRAFT_OPEN_DESC`), and it counts toward the limits.

**Drafts live in `SimPage`, not in the store or the tab.** `FundingDrafts` (`funding-drafts.svelte.ts`) holds the one open draft as `$state.raw`. It is outside `PolicyStore`, so autosave, undo and redo, publish and the worker never see it; it is owned by `SimPage` rather than `FundingTab` because the Dock renders only the active tab, so tab state would be lost on a tab switch. `startRun` discards an open draft and announces `DRAFT_DISCARDED`, "An unfinished item was discarded." A reload loses it, which its line says.

### Undo labels and coalesce keys

Every edit is a pure `(p: SimPolicy) => SimPolicy` in `funding-edit.ts` returning a `FundingEdit` (`change`, `label`, optional `coalesce`), applied as one `store.apply(change, label, coalesce)`; an edit that changes nothing is not applied. The label is announced through the page's polite region, followed by `coverageSummaryText` when the coverage text changed. The labels are the `label…` builders of `funding-copy.ts` (spec 4.9 lists them): "Added: {preset}", "Added: your own pool", "Removed: {name} and its {n} programmes", "Renamed: {name}", "Changed amount: {name}, row {k}, ${x} million", "Changed date: …", "Changed: {sentence}", "Changed income mix: {sentence}", "Today's funding: {name} switched off from {Month Year}", "Market term: guarantee rate cut {x} points", "Removed change: {name} from {Month Year}". Coalesce keys name the item and field: `pool:<id>:name`, `pool:<id>:in:<k>:amount`, `<pool|prog>:<id>:<field>` for dates, `pool:<id>:<field>` for rate, years and leverage, `prog:<id>:<field>`, `prog:<id>:tiers`, `term:<key>`; adds, removes, kind, reach and instrument changes do not coalesce. `FundingTab` suffixes each key with a run number (`@<n>`, numbered across mounts) renewed on every selection change and on mount, so a coalesce run ends when the selection changes or the tab remounts, as in the Transit tab.

### Results, card and summary

- **Results** (`MoneyResults.svelte`, rendered twice by `SimResults` with `part="money"` and `part="tiers"` so the face keeps its order). "Public money" (`data-testid="fzp-money"`, only with `score.money`) comes after "What … added": `fzp-money-city` "City money: {perAddedHome(money, i, 'city')} per added home · {spent} spent so far" (or "no homes added yet"), then `fzp-money-all` for all public money. The page never divides: per-home figures are `finance-score`'s `perAddedHome`. "Money details" opens the "By source" table (`fzp-money-sources`; zero columns dropped; "no limit" under Received for an uncapped pool, from `Pool.uncapped` through FzpMode's `uncapped` set, see [fzp-mode.md](fzp-mode.md)), the lost-grants line (`lossOnExit` from the shown levers' `terms`), funded and added homes, displacement, repayment owed and the legal limits ("Over:" / "Within:"). "Who the homes are for" (`fzp-tiers`, only with `score.tiers`) comes after the RHNA rows: "Affordable homes so far: {n}" and "Homes by income level" (`fzp-tiers-table`). Every value is read at the scrubbed month; while a revision is pending both blocks carry `aria-busy` (`pending`), and a failed revision keeps the old numbers under the existing stale line.
- **The card's "Funded by" line** (`data-testid="card-funded-by"`): `fundingName(policy.funding, baselineFunding)` (`funding-view.ts`) names the pools of the programmes that pay a simulated project ("the City", "the state", "federal tax credits", or an added pool's name; "A, B and C", "and {n} more"), then " · fully funded" or " · partly funded"; an affordable start adds "100% affordable homes." An id that no longer resolves is left out ("Funded by: public money" when none do). Pipeline and unfunded projects show no line. `SimPage` passes it to FzpMode as `fundingName`, on `/sim` and on `/s/<id>` (through `SimRoute`; `src/lib/sharing` and `src/routes/s/[id]` were not changed for it).
- **The read-only summary** (`PolicySummary`, "Public money" section): the coverage line, then `summaryRows`: "Today's funding: as today" or one row per dated change, added programmes on baseline pools, each added pool with its money sentence and its programmes, "No money added", typed terms ("Guarantee rate cut: 0.5 points (custom, not sourced)"), and " (custom, not sourced)" after custom evidence values.

| ----------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `funding-added`   | `upgradedFrom === 2`               | "This policy was saved before public money could be added. ### Known limits

- **An application waits for a short pool even when no more money is due to it.** A programme on a pool that is on but has run short keeps projects waiting, even after the pool's last inflow (design, "Out of scope"). The page keeps it visible without modelling it: the "By source" table shows each pool's balance, the coverage disclosure says a short source makes projects wait, and the user ends a programme with its "Until" field.
- **The federal pool is on or off, not scaled.** The uncapped tax-credit pool takes only "Switched off" and "Switched back on"; any scale above 0 leaves it on.
- **Small affordable projects below `minHomes` are never funded by the baseline** (ruling R22): the coverage disclosure shows `smallProjectsCaveat`. See [finance-data.md](finance-data.md).
- **A partly covered application keeps its month's first-day bundle** (ruling R24): the coverage disclosure shows `MONTH_OFFER_CAVEAT`. See [finance-engine.md](finance-engine.md).
