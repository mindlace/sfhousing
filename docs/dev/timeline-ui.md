# The /sim timeline

Developer notes for `src/lib/timeline/` and `src/lib/sim-page/SimPage.svelte`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-timeline-ui-design.md` (rules cited "rule N"); plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-14-timeline-ui.md`. `/sim` and `/s/<id>` show one screen: the map, a timeline of dated policies under it, a detail column beside it in the bottom pane. There is no Setup, no Run, no run slider and no high-water mark. The document is schema 4 ([policy-timeline.md](policy-timeline.md)); the worker is the ensemble runner ([ensemble-runner.md](ensemble-runner.md)); the numbers come from `TimelineView` ([world-score.md](world-score.md)). The page shell is in [sim-page.md](sim-page.md).

## The screen

- **Rows**, top to bottom: the ruler row (the transport in the label column, the ruler in the lane column), the chart, Zoning, Permitting, Public money, "The world". Public money exists only when the calibration has `market` and `baselineFunding` (`rows.ts`, `add.ts`).
- **One axis.** Every row, the ruler (it is the slider) and the playhead line sit on one lane column, placed by `dayPct`; the ruler's years are `yearMarks` (`axis.ts`): the dataset date's year at the left edge, then every fifth year's 1 January. The pinned segment of the playhead line starts at the chart; on the ruler the thumb marks the day.
- **The pinned block** (`tl-pin`: the ruler row and the chart) sits above the rows' own scroll box (`tl-rows`) at every width; it is never sticky, and the rows never pass under it. The two are sibling grids on the same columns: the block pads its end by the rows' scrollbar (`--tl-gutter`, measured by a `ResizeObserver`), so they share one x scale. 4 px of scroll padding on the rows box keep a focused bar's 24 px target clear of the chart. When the block would leave fewer than `MIN_ROWS` (64 px) of rows, `TimelineMode` passes `pinned={false}`: the timeline is one piece that its box scrolls (the bottom pane's lanes from md, the mode box below md). Below md, pinned, the bottom pane is the mode box's height: the mode box scrolls the map away first, then the rows scroll in their box. From md at 820 px tall or less the block is compact (a 64 px plot, 24 px controls, a 52 px ruler row) so the rows keep room. Measured at 1280 px wide: 165 px at 900 tall and 117 compact, the same with a policy in force (the stacked legend's four entries fit the plot: 70 of 96 px, and 62 of 64 compact on 14 px lines), so a first policy never moves the block. Below md the legend wraps under the plot and a policy's four entries take a second 28 px line: 193 to 221 px at 375x812, on a fine pointer and on the Playwright `mobile` project alike (one 24 px target size).
- **Phone rows** (below md, `max-width: 767.98px`). The label column is 92 px, too narrow for a name beside the count and "+", so in the rows and the kind sheet every row's label (`.lab` in `KindRow`, `PolicyRow`, `FixedRow` and the world heading) is a line of its own across both columns, and its lane sits on the next line in the lane column with column 1 empty: the lanes keep the ruler's width and x scale. A closed kind's line reads chevron, name, count, "+", the name whole ("Zoning", "Permitting", "Public money" at 375 px). The name line is `position: relative`, `z-index: 2`, on the surface colour, so the playhead line passes under the words. Lanes that never hold anything are not drawn (the world heading's, and an open kind's own in its sheet), and the lines pack at the top of a tall rows box (`align-content: start`) rather than share out its spare height. From md nothing changes. Measured at 375x812 on the `mobile` project: the pinned block 193 px and the rows box 570 px, unchanged; the rows' content 153 to 258 px with nothing in force and with one policy per kind alike (closed kinds take one line more each, their miniatures in the same lane); the Public money sheet with "Current funding" and a housing bond 115 to 162 px, the Permitting sheet with two policies 145 to 216 px. The block plus `MIN_ROWS` still fits with 506 px to spare.
- **The window** is `WINDOW_MONTHS = 480` months from the dataset date's year (`src/lib/sim/timeline-client.ts`); it never moves or zooms. No row is hatched: what the person has not seen yet shows on the ruler alone.
- **A closed kind** shows one thin bar per policy; with none, and the sim editable, its lane says what "+" adds (`EMPTY_LANE`, `rows.ts`: "Add a zoning change", "Add a permitting change", "Add public money"), a `tl-empty-lane` button that calls `onadd(kind)` as "+" does. It is the pointer's duplicate of "+": `tabindex="-1"` and `aria-hidden="true"`, so "+" stays the one keyboard stop, and a press never focuses it (its mousedown default is prevented): it focuses the "+" and then adds, so focus ends where a press of the "+" leaves it; while the words show, "+" takes them as its accessible name ("Add a zoning change"), so voice control can press what it reads; otherwise it is "Add {label} policy". Read-only (`/s/<id>`) shows no words. Open (in place from md, a sheet below md) it shows a row per policy with a bar per span. The fixed rows ("Current law", "Current timelines", "Current funding", "Market conditions") are selectable and read-only; selecting one of the first three shows its sentence (`FIXED_NOTE`, `rows.ts`) under the heading, which names the formal source ("Current law is the Family Zoning Plan as adopted."). Their refs have `span: -1` (`FIXED_SPAN`, `rows.ts`), an index no policy has, so a document policy named `fixed` cannot alias one.
- **The ruler is the scrubber** (`Playhead.svelte`, one row of the pinned grid). The label cell is the transport on two lines: Play at the cell's width (Pause while playing, Replay at `last`, "Play from {year}" when `playFrom` is given; `playLabel`, `playhead.ts`), then the speed button ("1×" or "3×", named `speedName`; it changes speed only, never starts playback) and Today. From md the cell is 168 px with 10 px padding and Play reads `text-sm` ("Play from 2028" measured 101 px of text in 148 px); below md the cell is 92 px with no padding, line 2 is two 44 px buttons and Play's label may wrap. The row is 68 px (52 compact), the same on a coarse pointer. The lane cell is the ruler, and the ruler is the slider, named "Date in the simulation", `aria-valuetext` the month, described by `rulerDescription` ("Seen up to June 2045. Goal deadline January 2031.", each part only when given; "Seen up to" only while the furthest month reached is after the playhead's month). Inside it, hidden from assistive technology: the years; the track in **three zones**, shown from today to the day (`--color-ink-muted`), seen from the day to the furthest date reached (`--color-control-line`) and unseen past it (hatched); the thumb; the date flag on the year line over the thumb (`dateFlag`: "Today" at the dataset date, else the short month, the header's), kept inside the cell at either end, a year label it overlaps hidden whole (`.covered`, from the ruler's and a label's widths measured on resize; per frame at most the nine marks are compared and a label's class changes only as the flag reaches or leaves it); and the goal flag ("Goal") under the track at the goal's deadline while the chart draws the goal's step. A zone edge at or past `last` is drawn at the track's end, so a fully reached forecast shows no hatched sliver; the thumb stays at the day's own place. The flags are not buttons; there are no jump markers. Nothing on the ruler has a CSS transition, and as the day moves only the zones, the thumb and the flag change (the years are never rewritten).
- **The playhead's keys and presses.** A press or drag anywhere on the ruler, years and flags included, moves the day and pauses. Left/Down and Right/Up move a month, Page Down/Up a year, Home goes to the dataset date, End to the computed edge (`end`: filled to the last month, the last day of the last calendar month row, `lastDay`, up to a month short of the window's end); Alt, Ctrl and Meta keys are ignored. A key, a press or Today pauses playback. **Space** plays and pauses from anywhere but a field or a control Space presses; the date slider is neither, so Space plays from it: `TimelineMode` listens on the window and calls `Playhead.toggle()`, the Play button's own action ("Play from" and Replay included), for `isPlayKey` (`src/lib/timeline/shortcuts.ts`: keydown, no repeat, no modifier, not already handled, no modal dialog or open popover, not in `isEditableTarget` or `SPACE_NATIVE`), and marks it handled so the page never scrolls. A held key toggles once: the press that toggled marks its repeats handled too, without a toggle, until Space's keyup or a window blur, so a held key scrolls nothing either. `spacePlays` (default true) gates it: `/sim` turns it off while the brush can paint (`canPaint`: ready, editable, Paint or Erase chosen), where Space is the brush's temporary Move. About this model's Timeline table lists these keys and a focused bar's (`TIMELINE_SHORTCUTS`). Nothing plays on open; playback waits at the computed edge and goes on when it moves (the status cell says "Updating" meanwhile, rule 32), and stops at `lastDay` (the `Playhead`'s `last`; Replay shows there and it is `aria-valuemax`), so every reading names the last row's month. Replay starts again from today; "Play from" moves the day to its date, then plays.
- **The furthest date reached** (`reveal.ts`) is `TimelineMode`'s `reveal`: `revealOpen(asOf)` on opening (and on a Retry's reload with a new calibration), so everything ahead is unseen; every day change goes through `moved`, so dragging forward moves it and dragging back leaves it, and what lies between is seen.
- **A bar's name** starts with the words it shows, so a voice user can say what they see (WCAG 2.5.3): a span bar is `barLabel` (`rows.ts`), "{setting}{left}, {policy}, {from} to {to}" then ", off" when off ("$410M, 99% left, Housing bond, 10 Dec 2030 to no end"; "6 stories, Transit rule, 10 Dec 2030 to no end"); a fixed bar, the world row's included, is `fixedLabel`, "{text}{left}, {label}, whole window" ("In force, 86% left, Current funding, whole window"; "In force, Current law, whole window"; "{market text}, Market conditions, whole window").
- **A bar** moves by drag or, focused, by arrows: the edge nearer the playhead's month (ties and an open end: the start) by a month, with Shift by a year, with Alt the whole span (`spans.ts`). The month fields in the inspector reach both edges.
- **A pool's bar shows its level** (`LevelFill` over `pool-level.ts`), in an open Public money kind only (a closed kind's mini bars carry none): a fund policy's bar whose policy has an own capped pool, and "Current funding" (the calibration's capped baseline pools, summed). The level is the free share of the money received so far (`poolLevel`, defined in [sim-funding.md](sim-funding.md), "A pool's level"). `TimelineMode` builds a `LevelSet` (`levelSet(shownView, resultsDoc, baselineFunding.pools, asOf)`) with the view on show and the document it was run on (the held ones while stale), never as the playhead moves (the spec counts builds). In the bar, under its text, an `aria-hidden` svg draws the empty part from the bar's top down to the level, each month's level held across that month (so at the playhead it is the month the name reads), and the level's edge as a line in the bar's text colour (`--on-k`): the shape carries the level, the edge 5.3:1 against the bar in light and 6.9:1 in dark. The empty part darkens the bar under light text (`rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35)`, light theme) and lightens it under dark text (`rgb(255 255 255 / 0.35)`, dark), so the text only gains contrast (9.5:1 and 9.6:1 over the shown fill); a bar with a level sets its words on a small backing in the bar's colour, so the edge passes under them and never strikes them through. Zones as the chart's: shown to the playhead's day, seen (half strength, a thinner edge) to the furthest day reached, nothing past it; the paths are built once per set and span, and as the day moves only two clip rectangles change. The bar's text and its name add ", 62% left" (`levelLeft`: "$410M, 99% left", "In force, 86% left") at the playhead's month under the header's rule (`{ index, atToday }`): nothing at today, before the pool has money, where the bar does not cover the playhead, or on an off policy. A bar too short for the words clips them at its end, as any bar's text (no ellipsis); at 375 px the kind's sheet gives the open-ended bars 283 px and "In force, 86% left" takes 97. While stale (`stale`, the held view) both zones take the seen styling and the svg carries `data-stale`, as the chart; the words' backing takes the fill's shade and ", 62% left" dims to `--level-dim` (0.75), keeping 4.5:1 (6.1:1 light, 5.3:1 dark). While a bar's edge is dragged its level is hidden (the box no longer matches the run's months) until the pointer is up. An uncapped pool and a fund policy whose programmes draw on a baseline pool have no level. Nothing animates. Under forced colours (`forced-colors: active`) the svg is `forced-color-adjust: none` and draws the empty part in `GrayText` (at its opacities) and the edge in `ButtonText`, over the browser's forced button colours. Every bar (`SpanBar`, `FixedRow`) draws its extent under forced colours as a 1 px `ButtonText` outline inside its box (`outline-offset: -1px`; a selected or focused bar keeps its own outline).
- **A new policy** starts on the model month the playhead is in, with no end. Model months start near the dataset date's day of the month, not on the 1st, so a span's edges read as dates (`formatEdge`, "10 Dec 2030"), written in one place (`spanEdgeText` in `rows.ts`, used by the When list and the bars' names). A start is its first model month's first day; an end is inclusive, its last model month's last day.
- **The header score** is `TimelineHeaderScore` (`src/lib/scoreboard/HeaderScore.svelte` is hashed and untouched). It reads the playhead's calendar month `i` through `headlineAt` and `headlineText` (`results.ts`), counted from today. `headlineAt` takes `playheadAt`'s reading (`{ index, atToday }`, the one rule for the header, the chart's readout and the ruler's flag) and `{ provisional, inForce }`; `inForce` is `!nothingInForce` of the document the numbers on show are from (`view.compare` is never read). It reads `months[i]`: the page overrides neither the month nor the effect. Line 1, the button and its name: `rows[i].completedNet` and the month ("47,335 homes by Apr 2040"), "0 homes by today" while the playhead is at the dataset date (no filled month needed). Line 2, the button's description: the effect, `planEffect[i].completedNetNew` as "+N from your policy", only while a policy is in force (below); then the goal, read through `src/lib/goal/goal.ts` (`GOAL`, `readGoal`, "68% of the goal" or "Goal reached, Mar 2029"), and its name ("State target: …") on hover and focus, opening under the box. It reads "calculating" while provisional and while month `i` of the view on show is not filled; while stale it shows the held view's numbers, dimmed. It depends on the playhead's calendar month (`index` and `atToday`), never the day within it, so it changes at most once per month the playhead crosses, and `readGoal` bounds its row reads. What drops at narrow widths: below sm line 2 (only line 1 shows); from lg to xl the goal part while the effect shows. The dropped parts stay in the description (an `sr-only` copy of line 2) and the live text; Details carries the goal rows. There is no meter.
- **The chart row** (`ChartRow` over `chart-model.ts`) draws the view on show (`shown.view`, the header's: the held numbers while stale) on the ruler's axis, counted from today like the header. Positions: 0 is today at the dataset date (value 0), and `j = i + 1` is month `i`'s value at the last day of month `i` (`posDay`: `filledEndDay(months, j - 1, asOf)`), since a month's value is the state at its end; only filled months are points (`chartPoints`). The line starts at 0 at today. **Current law**: while a policy is in force (`inForce`, from the document the numbers on show came from, never `view.compare`) a second line is `currentLawHomes(view, i)` = `rows[i].completedNet - planEffect[i].completedNetNew`, which is `twin.completedNetNew[i] + plan.completedNetPipeline[i]`, and the shaded gap between the two is `planEffect[i].completedNetNew`, exactly the header's "+N from your policy". It leaves out the twin's own pipeline completions: a policy's effect on projects already in today's pipeline (permitting timers on in-flight projects, money awarded to them) is in neither the gap nor the header's number. With nothing in force the twin is the old zoning, not current law, so the line and the gap are not drawn. **Zones** by clip: each series (`chart-line`, `chart-law`, `chart-gap`) is one path drawn twice, once in the shown clip rectangle (today to the playhead's day, `--chart-total`, `--chart-law`, 2.5 px) and once in the seen one (the day to the furthest date reached, `--chart-total-seen`, `--chart-law-seen`, 1.25 px, the gap at half strength, faint by design: the two seen lines bound it and its value is in the readout); nothing past `reached`. A clip edge at or past the last row's day is the plot's end, as on the ruler. While stale the figure has `data-stale` and every copy takes the seen styling. **Scale**: 0 (or `-niceCeil` of the lowest value reached) to `yTop`, `niceCeil` of the goal's units (2 without a goal, so the top is never a single home) and the running maximum (`runningMax`, built once per view) at the furthest month reached, so it grows in 1-2-5 steps a handful of times in a window and never shrinks as the playhead goes back. Paths depend only on the view, `inForce` and the scale; as the day moves only the two clip rectangles change, and the chart reads no row. Gridlines: one vertical per `yearMarks` entry (the ruler's years) and horizontals at `yLabels` (half the top, left out when it is not a whole number of homes, and the top, `formatCompactCount`), the labels at the plot's left edge, haloed, in one format (both compact, "50K" and "100K", when the top is 100,000 or more, else both in full) and hidden from assistive technology. **The goal** is a dashed `--chart-ink` step at `goalStep(GOAL, view).units` (`src/lib/goal/goal.ts`: the homes still needed, `amount` minus the homes built before today) to the end of the deadline's month, where the ruler's goal flag sits, labelled in the plot by `goalNote` ("73,346 more by Jan 2031") so no one reads it as the state target's total; the label is hidden from assistive technology and the caption carries the same words; it is an input, drawn whole whatever the zones. With no step (the deadline's month outside the window) the chart, its legend and its caption leave the goal out. **Legend** (`ChartLegend`) names only what is drawn: "Homes completed", or "With your policy", "Current law" and "From your policy" (the gap) while a policy is in force; then "Goal" while the step is drawn. From md it stacks in the label column, its entries fitting the plot's height (14 px lines compact); below md it wraps under the plot. The gap's swatch is outlined in `--chart-plan`. **Readout**: the overlay slider "Month in the chart" runs over positions 0 to the furthest month reached and starts at the playhead's (`posOf(playheadAt(…))`); its `aria-valuetext` and hover readout are `chartReading` (`results.ts`), the header's line 1 for that month, then ", +56 from your policy" while a policy is in force; it reads one row. The plot is 96 px tall (64 from md at 820 px tall or less). **Lenses**: `ChartRow`'s `lens` (`ChartLens`, `'homes'` by default; Details switches it; the figure's `data-lens` is the lens drawn) picks what the two series are, through `lensOf` (`chart-lens.ts`, re-exported from `chart-model.ts`): `'effect'` with nothing in force is `'homes'`. **Homes** is the chart above. **By source** (with or without a policy) draws `rows[i].completedNet` over `rows[i].completedNetPipeline` (the pipeline line on the current-law tokens, `--chart-law`, dashed `PIPELINE_DASH` '2 3', shorter than the goal's '5 3', so colour is not the only cue; its legend swatch has the same dash), the shaded gap (`--chart-gap`) being new projects. It draws no goal step or note and the goal does not set its scale (`yTop(null, …)`), so small totals stay readable; its caption names no goal; legend "All homes", "From today's pipeline", "New projects"; readout "1,234 homes by Apr 2040, 800 from today's pipeline". **From your policy** draws `planEffect[i].completedNetNew` alone, no current-law line, no gap and no goal step or note, and the goal does not set its scale (`yTop(null, …)`); a negative effect stays in the plot (`runningMin`); legend "From your policy"; readout "+56 from your policy by Apr 2040" ("+0 from your policy by today" at today), the header's effect figure for the month. Only Homes draws the goal and lets it size the scale, on purpose. Every lens starts at 0 at today and keeps the zones and the scale rule (the running extremes at the furthest month reached), so nothing not yet reached sizes it. The legend never has more than four entries. A lens change rebuilds the paths once; the day moving rebuilds none. The caption (`chartCaption(inForce, goal, lens)`) names each lens's lines. `LENS_NAME` (`copy.ts`) holds the switch's words: "Homes", "By source", "From your policy". The lens is `TimelineMode`'s state, passed to `ChartRow` and to `Timeline` (the stacked legend from md), and bound in Details; it resets to Homes when Details closes, so the chart at rest always means one thing. **Details** (`SimDetails`, `src/lib/timeline/SimDetails.svelte`, in `data-testid="tl-details"`) is what the header press opens: an h2 "Details" that never changes with the playhead, then the **Chart** switch (a `fieldset` with the legend "Chart" and radios `LENS_NAME`: "Homes", "By source", and "From your policy" only while a policy is in force, read from the same `inForce` the chart gets, the held document's while stale; a choice no longer offered goes back to Homes, so a radio is always checked and a policy added later does not switch the chart unasked). Details draws no chart of its own. It has no headline. It says once, above the goal rows (`fzp-built`, `builtTowardGoalText`), that the homes built from June 2022 count toward the goal; the goal rows count from June 2022. The goal rows are `GoalRow` over `goalRows(goal, view, playhead, reachedMonth)` (`src/lib/goal/goal.ts`; `goal` is `TimelineMode`'s test seam): "State target" and "State target + 15%" (`bufferAmount`, 94,379), no "RHNA". They read under the header's rule: at today (`playhead.atToday`) both read `TODAY`, so the goal row is the header's percentage exactly; after it, the playhead's month up to the deadline's, then held there. The goal row's fraction is `readGoal`'s. Under each meter the remainder is dated (`goalRowNote`): "73,346 to go by Jan 2031" before the deadline's month, then "Held at Jan 2031" on its own muted line; with the deadline before the window's first month the rows read today and carry no date. The preview "At the deadline: 74%" (`atDeadlineText`) shows only while the playhead is before the deadline's month and the furthest month reached (`reachedMonth`) is at or past it. At today (`playhead.atToday`) Details reads today as the header does: `TimelineMode` passes `todayAt(resultsAt(…))` (`results.ts`), so pipeline, new projects, stopped and the plan effect read 0, and the money and tier blocks do not show (nothing is spent or finished yet), as the table does not. The sections under the h2 are h3 (the money limits h4); the wrapper pads its text (`p-3`, as the Inspector). Source labels pass through `plainSources` (`money-view.ts`), so no plain label says "RHNA"; citation links keep the documents' names. Then "What … added" (with the protection line), "From today's pipeline" and "New projects", the stopped line, "Public money" and "Who the homes are for" (`MoneyResults`), the year table, and last one link, "How this is worked out" (`WORKED_OUT`, `/about#about-assumptions`, `data-testid="details-about"`), which opens About over the map ([about.md](about.md)); the forecast note, the market paragraph, the dataset line and the phasing note left with the old Sources disclosure. The in-progress rows are not in Details (the map key carries them). The **year table** ("Show as table", caption "Running total from today, by year": its figures are totals from today, net, so one can fall, `yearRows`/`YEAR_COLUMNS` in `details.ts`) has one row per calendar year whose December is filled and at or before the playhead's month, then the playhead's month itself ("Mar 2031"), from today's year: Homes (`rows[i].completedNet`, the header's count), From today's pipeline, New projects, and From your policy while a policy is in force; its last row is the header's figure at the same month and nothing after the playhead is listed; at today there is no table. It steps from the first December by 12, so a month crossed costs one read a year, not one a month (`details.spec.ts` counts). The timeline chart's figure is named by the lens drawn: "Homes completed", else `LENS_NAME`'s "By source" or "From your policy"; its screen-reader caption (`chartCaption`, `copy.ts`) says it counts from today, names current law and the shaded gap while a policy is in force, names the goal as the homes still needed with its remainder and deadline (`goalNote`'s words) while the step is drawn, and says that the month slider reads out each month.

## The worker messages as the page uses them

`TimelineClient` (`src/lib/sim/timeline-client.ts`) replaces `initScenario`, `reroll`, `setLevers`, `timeline`, `ranks` and `levers` on `/sim`; nothing on `/sim` sends them; the router and worker still route them (removal waits for the `/lesscars` cut-over). `TimelineSession` takes its own client from `createWorker()` (also what a crash restart uses).

- `open(manifest, doc, lots)` and `setPolicy(doc, lots)` return the rev the client used. `viewMonth(m)` goes through `MonthGate` (`playhead.ts`): at most four a second, only when the model month changes. `resyncMap()` is the map's `onResyncNeeded`. `retryChunks()` answers the "failed cells" notice.
- `compiled`, `filled` and `refill` are applied in arrival order into a `ChunkLog`; `rev` is never assumed to rise, and `compiled` and `refill` are never deduped by `rev` (one `refill` per acceptance; an edit taken up again after a later one was rejected gets a second `compiled` and `refill`). A `filled` chunk belongs to the statics of the last `compiled` before it. The view exists once `compiled` has arrived.
- `cells`, `lookups`, `heights` and `chunks` carry no rev and are forwarded as they come. The map's bars read the posted lookups, the card reads its own answer's.
- `error`: `bad-request` is the page's rule 34; any other code is rule 35. A crash is rule 36.

## The session's rules

- **Stale.** From an edit at rev R the page shows the held view (the one before the first unsettled edit), dimmed, with "Updating". An accepted edit ends it when a `refill` at rev >= R has arrived and the filled part reaches the playhead's month (`fill.ts`, `shownAt`). `compiled` never settles it: it precedes the `refill`. A `refill` at an older rev than the newest sent (an edit taken up again after a later one was rejected) puts the page back in stale; pending is left alone.
- **Rejection.** A `bad-request` (rule 34, "That change couldn't be applied.") undoes the store to the newest kept document not answered `bad-request`. The session keeps every document sent since the last rev answered by a `refill`, that rev's included (`#kept`); an older rev's `bad-request` can follow a newer one. After a rejection the page stays stale until `filledThrough` is back to the held view's and passes the playhead (`floor` in `EditState`).
- **Play** is never held back. Any edit pauses playback and the playhead does not move; Play works at once and the playhead waits at the computed edge. While an edit has no refill yet, Play runs on the held numbers, dimmed, with "Updating" in the header.
- **The provisional open (OQ-3).** The worker resolves brushes only after its dataset loads, so an `open` cannot carry the lots of a restored document with painted or transit policies. With `lots` null the session opens the document without those zoning policies (`provisionalOf`), then sends the full pair as its next `setPolicy`, as part of opening, not an edit. Until a `refill` at that rev or later has come and the window is whole, `provisional` is true: the headline stays "calculating" and the results stay stale. With the dataset loaded and lots resolved before the first open, one open carries the full document.
- **The carry-6 restart.** If the full document stands rejected after a provisional open, the stripped document stays what the forecast shows. A crash restart or the rule 35 Retry then re-opens and immediately sends a `setPolicy` of the stripped pair, keeps `provisional`, and posts the full document again only after the user's next edit. Never undo to the stripped document: that would drop the user's brushes.
- **A restart** re-opens with the last pair posted to the client.
- **The card** asks again after an edit once a `filled` covers its day, and whenever the playhead's month changes.
- **A failed brush query.** When PolicySync settles an edit with a failed query (`failed`, lots null), the session drops the unsent edit (`dropUnsent`): no stale, and the forecast stays the last sent document's. A "Couldn't find lots" notice with Retry asks the failed queries again.

## Revealing time

- **Three zones.** Shown, from today to the playhead's day; seen, from the day to the furthest date reached; unseen, past it. The timeline chart's lines and the gap past them, the ruler's track, and Details' year table and "At the deadline" lines follow the zones; never the policy bars, the goal step or its flag. The header, the goal rows, and the money and income rows read at the playhead, which is always inside what has been reached. The goal notice reads the months reached.
- **The rules** are `reveal.ts`, held in `TimelineMode` as `reveal`. Opening (and a Retry's reload with a new calibration) is `revealOpen(asOf)`: the furthest date is today, everything ahead unseen. Every day change goes through `moved`: the furthest date only grows, and an offered "Play from" goes.
- **An edit** (a new `policy`) calls `editedReveal` beside `session.edited()`, with the view on show and whether a policy is in force in the document it came from (`inForce`, never `view.compare`). A search already under way keeps what was seen first, so a second edit before the first is settled compares with the numbers the person saw. A send with no edit that goes out to the worker (`session.send` returns true: a Retry's lots after a failed brush query, a provisional open's full document) holds the document the numbers on show came from (`hold`, as an edit does, so held numbers keep their wording, money and terms until the refill) and starts a search the same way when none is under way; on a first open with nothing shown there is nothing to compare, so none starts.
- **The scan** runs only once a refill has answered the edit (`session.pending` false) and, after a provisional open, once the full document has filled the window (`session.provisional` false); before that the newest view is still the old one. It runs when the view or the pending state changes, never per frame or as the playhead moves, and `scanned` resumes from the month it stopped at, so a fill is compared once. It compares month by month, never past the furthest month reached. A month changed if any figure it draws differs (`monthChanged`: every row field, the current-law homes and approvals, tiers and money). The first changed month pulls the furthest date back to it, never behind the playhead. No change through the furthest month pulls nothing back. Every search ends: months past the filled extent of the view it compares with cannot be compared, so once the new view has filled past them with no change found, the first of them counts as the first changed month (what cannot be shown unchanged is unseen); the one exception is a provisional open whose full document did not go in, where the search waits for the next edit. A rejected edit, or one whose lots fail, settles with the old view, so it pulls nothing back.
- **"Play from {year}"** (`playFromDay`: the first changed month's start, clamped to today..the computed edge as `Playhead` clamps it, so the debug hook names the day Play goes to) shows when the scan finds a change while not playing. Pressed, it moves the playhead there and plays, wherever that month is: before the playhead it plays the change again, after it it skips the seen months the edit left alone. It wins over Replay at the last day and goes when the playhead moves for any reason or another edit starts a search. A change found while playing offers nothing; a change past the furthest date reached is no pull-back, so Play stays plain.
- **The goal notice** (`outcome.ts`, `tl-goal` in the notice stack after the page's notices) is the only notice that reads the goal, and it reads it over the months reached (`goalOutcome(goal, view, reached, asOf)`), so it never speaks of a month not reached. It depends on `reachedMonth` (a primitive: the month index of the furthest date, `TODAY` at or before the dataset date), never on the day, so scrubbing by days inside a month recomputes nothing and rewrites no DOM; it reads no row past the deadline month, at most the deadline month + 1 rows plus two repeat reads. Reached: "Goal reached in {month}, by the {deadline} deadline." Missed, once the furthest date passes the deadline unmet: "Goal missed by {n} homes. Change the policy and play again." with a policy in force, "Add a policy and play again." with nothing in force. It comes the first time the playhead reaches the month (playing or dragging), stays while the person scrubs back, and goes when dismissed (that outcome, keyed by `outcomeKey`, stays away for the visit) or when an edit pulls the furthest date back before its month. While the numbers are stale or an edit's search is under way the last outcome is held, and with it whether a policy was in force in the document it came from (`outcomeInForce`), so the wording and the numbers are always one document's and nothing in the region is rewritten until the new numbers are on show. An edit that changes the outcome then replaces it, announced once; one that leaves it alone leaves it, unannounced, except that an edit between nothing in force and a policy in force (a first policy, or the last one turned off) swaps "Add a policy" and "Change the policy" once, with the same number. The region is a polite `role=status` mounted empty with the session, so the first notice is announced; the notice has no timer. `TimelineMode`'s `goal` prop (default `GOAL`) is the test seam every goal read goes through.
- **The map's halos and marks follow the playhead.** `onMapReady` calls `setHalos({ from: asOf })` once a calibration is in hand, and a Retry's reload with a new calibration calls it again from the new date; nothing else does, and never as the playhead moves (the map reads its own clock). So nothing rings at today: a ring shows only for a stage change after the dataset date and at or before the playhead's day, and a mark only from its project's first stage day; scrubbing back hides those now ahead. An edit replaces the cells it changes, and with them their rings and marks, so no mark of the old numbers survives. Marks appear only on projects whose slot carries a flag, which needs a policy of the person's in force. Paused, a ring holds its strength; with reduced motion it is a still ring. See [map-interfaces.md](map-interfaces.md), "Halos and marks". The city layer (`timeline-city`) follows the same clock and the same rule: below zoom 14 a merged ring per area stands for its projects' rings and one diamond per area for its projects under the person's policy that have begun, and a change off screen glows at the edge toward it; a record shows only from its day, after today and at or before the playhead, and scrubbing back hides it ([map-interfaces.md](map-interfaces.md), "Merged rings and edge glows"). `onMapReady` also calls `setBarMode('far')` once, whatever the calibration, so the bars show from the default zoom at one size, the person's projects first ("Bar modes").
- **The furthest date lasts for the visit.** It is never saved, published or put in the address; a reload starts at today.
- **Known limit.** Seen months past the computed edge (after a refill withdrew them) show no line until the fill reaches them again.

## Commit path and load notices

- **Commit-path reconcile.** Every store change passes `PolicyStore`'s `normalize`, which `SimPage` wires to `reconcilePolicy4(doc, calibration, asOf)`. The reconciled document is what is committed, sent and undone to. A programme or baseline scale an edit leaves without a source is dropped, with today's funding notice (`fundingNotices`, [sim-funding.md](sim-funding.md)). `spans.ts` is pure and keeps only the span structure valid, so the funding reconcile happens here. `TimelineSession` reconciles again before sending and warns once if that changed anything (it is a bug upstream).

- **`?seed=`** is dropped from the address with `replaceState` (`SimRoute.svelte`, `address.ts`), other parameters kept.

## Editing

- **Drafts.** An unfinished funding draft (`FundingDrafts`, in `SimPage`, outside the store) is discarded, with "An unfinished item was discarded.", when another policy is selected, "+" opens, the editor is closed or it unmounts. A change of span inside the same policy keeps it.
- **Funding** is hosted per policy: `FundingTab` with `only` (a baseline, one fund, or the terms), priced on the playhead's day.
- **Permitting** is `PermittingSection snapshot={false}`: no Save or "Revert to saved"; Revert returns the span's set to its preset's. The preset is the policy's: on a policy of several spans the preset select is disabled (`PRESET_PER_POLICY`, behind a tap). Custom timing keeps preset `observed` with the label `CUSTOM_TIMING_LABEL`.
- **Zoning.** A transit policy's stop types belong to the policy, its bands to the span. A stroke goes into the selected painted policy's span (else its selected span); with none selected a new painted policy is added from the playhead month and selected.
- **Edits in place on an offer** (an existing fund policy): the pool name (label); the kind of money; each money-in row (Once / Every year, amount, start, Until); adding and removing money-in rows; leverage (bank); programmes (add, edit, remove; the programme set is policy-wide, so the lens writes it to every span). **Debt terms (rate, term) are read-only**; end the policy and add a new one to change them (`TERMS_HINT`, behind a tap).

## Known limits

- **The early-edit guess.** The worker does not mark the rejection of an edit sent before the dataset loaded, so the page guesses. An early edit's rejection shows rule 35 ("The simulation couldn't load.") briefly, then flips to rule 34 ("That change couldn't be applied." plus the undo) when a post at an older rev arrives. Pressing Retry inside that window can leave the rule 35 notice up.
- **The restart rev.** `TimelineClient.restart` returns no rev, so the session assumes the client's next rev.
- **Debt terms.** A hand-edited /s link can still carry two sets of terms on one pool id (`parse4` checks only that every span names the same pool); world-score then prices the statements on month W's terms.
- **The painted restore.** A restore with painted or transit policies reaches the full document's first results at 3.66 s on the live city, against 1.83 s for an empty document. The worker resolves brushes only after its dataset loads, so the page opens the stripped document and then sends the full one, which costs a recompile. Accepted by main.
- **Calendar playhead, model-month spans.** The playhead counts calendar months while spans snap to model months, so a policy added at "January 2031" starts on 10 Dec 2030 (live data).
- **The engine's limits** (ruling 8), listed with their numbers in [ensemble-runner.md](ensemble-runner.md): worker memory about +394 MB with no copies and +526 MB with the month-0 copy (live city); the longest worker compile step about 52.6 ms; one cold massing of about 636 ms; planning with rich public money over the per-slice bar. The page shows no state for them.

## For the composer

A worker that could take brush queries before its dataset loads, or an `openTimeline` that awaits lots, would remove the recompile (the painted restore above).
