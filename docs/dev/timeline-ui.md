# The /sim timeline

Developer notes for `src/lib/timeline/` and `src/lib/sim-page/SimPage.svelte`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-timeline-ui-design.md` (rules cited "rule N"); plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-14-timeline-ui.md`. `/sim` and `/s/<id>` show one screen: the map, a timeline of dated policies under it, a detail column beside it in the bottom pane. There is no Setup, no Run, no run slider and no high-water mark. The document is schema 4 ([policy-timeline.md](policy-timeline.md)); the worker is the ensemble runner ([ensemble-runner.md](ensemble-runner.md)); the numbers come from `TimelineView` ([world-score.md](world-score.md)). The page shell is in [sim-page.md](sim-page.md).

## The screen

- **Rows**, top to bottom: the ruler row (the transport in the label column, the ruler in the lane column), the chart, Zoning, Permitting, Public money, "The world". Public money exists only when the calibration has `market` and `baselineFunding` (`rows.ts`, `add.ts`).
- **One axis.** Every row, the ruler (it is the slider) and the playhead line sit on one lane column, placed by `dayPct`; the ruler's years are `yearMarks` (`axis.ts`): the dataset date's year at the left edge, then every fifth year's 1 January. The pinned segment of the playhead line starts at the chart; on the ruler the thumb marks the day.
- **The pinned block** (`tl-pin`: the ruler row and the chart) sits above the rows' own scroll box (`tl-rows`) at every width; it is never sticky, and the rows never pass under it. The two are sibling grids on the same columns: the block pads its end by the rows' scrollbar (`--tl-gutter`, measured by a `ResizeObserver`), so they share one x scale. 4 px of scroll padding on the rows box keep a focused bar's 24 px target clear of the chart. When the block would leave fewer than `MIN_ROWS` (64 px) of rows, `TimelineMode` passes `pinned={false}`: the timeline is one piece that its box scrolls (the bottom pane's lanes from md, the mode box below md). Below md, pinned, the bottom pane is the mode box's height: the mode box scrolls the map away first, then the rows scroll in their box. From md at 820 px tall or less the block is compact (a 40 px plot, 24 px controls, a 52 px ruler row) so the rows keep room.
- **The window** is `WINDOW_MONTHS = 480` months from the dataset date's year (`src/lib/sim/timeline-client.ts`); it never moves or zooms. No row is hatched: what the person has not seen yet shows on the ruler alone.
- **A closed kind** shows one thin bar per policy; open (in place from md, a sheet below md) it shows a row per policy with a bar per span. The fixed rows ("Current law", "Current timelines", "Current funding", "Market conditions") are selectable and read-only; selecting one of the first three shows its sentence (`FIXED_NOTE`, `rows.ts`) under the heading, which names the formal source ("Current law is the Family Zoning Plan as adopted."). Their refs have `span: -1` (`FIXED_SPAN`, `rows.ts`), an index no policy has, so a document policy named `fixed` cannot alias one.
- **The ruler is the scrubber** (`Playhead.svelte`, one row of the pinned grid). The label cell is the transport on two lines: Play at the cell's width (Pause while playing, Replay at `last`, "Play from {year}" when `playFrom` is given; `playLabel`, `playhead.ts`), then the speed button ("1×" or "3×", named `speedName`; it changes speed only, never starts playback) and Today. From md the cell is 168 px with 10 px padding and Play reads `text-sm` ("Play from 2028" measured 101 px of text in 148 px); below md the cell is 92 px with no padding, line 2 is two 44 px buttons and Play's label may wrap. The row is 68 px (52 compact, 92 on a coarse pointer). The lane cell is the ruler, and the ruler is the slider, named "Date in the simulation", `aria-valuetext` the month, described by `rulerDescription` ("Seen up to June 2045. Goal deadline January 2031.", each part only when given). Inside it, hidden from assistive technology: the years; the track in **three zones**, shown from today to the day (`--color-ink-muted`), seen from the day to the furthest date reached (`--color-control-line`) and unseen past it (hatched); the thumb; the date flag on the year line over the thumb (`dateFlag`: "Today" at the dataset date, else the short month, the header's), kept inside the cell at either end; and the goal flag ("Goal") under the track at the goal's deadline while the chart draws the goal's step. A zone edge at or past `last` is drawn at the track's end, so a fully reached forecast shows no hatched sliver; the thumb stays at the day's own place. The flags are not buttons; there are no jump markers. Nothing on the ruler has a CSS transition, and as the day moves only the zones, the thumb and the flag change (the years are never rewritten).
- **The playhead's keys and presses.** A press or drag anywhere on the ruler, years and flags included, moves the day and pauses. Left/Down and Right/Up move a month, Page Down/Up a year, Home goes to the dataset date, End to the computed edge (`end`: filled to the last month, the last day of the last calendar month row, `lastDay`, up to a month short of the window's end); Alt, Ctrl and Meta keys are ignored. A key, a press or Today pauses playback. Nothing plays on open; playback waits at the computed edge and goes on when it moves, and stops at `lastDay` (the `Playhead`'s `last`; Replay shows there and it is `aria-valuemax`), so every reading names the last row's month. Replay starts again from today; "Play from" moves the day to its date, then plays.
- **The furthest date reached** (`reveal.ts`) is `TimelineMode`'s `reveal`: `revealOpen(asOf)` on opening (and on a Retry's reload), so everything ahead is unseen; every day change goes through `moved`, so dragging forward moves it and dragging back leaves it, and what lies between is seen.
- **A bar** moves by drag or, focused, by arrows: the edge nearer the playhead's month (ties and an open end: the start) by a month, with Shift by a year, with Alt the whole span (`spans.ts`). The month fields in the inspector reach both edges.
- **A new policy** starts on the model month the playhead is in, with no end. Model months start near the dataset date's day of the month, not on the 1st, so a span's edges read as dates (`formatEdge`, "10 Dec 2030"), written in one place (`spanEdgeText` in `rows.ts`, used by the When list and the bars' names). A start is its first model month's first day; an end is inclusive, its last model month's last day.
- **The header score** is `TimelineHeaderScore` (`src/lib/scoreboard/HeaderScore.svelte` is hashed and untouched). It reads the playhead's calendar month `i` through `headlineAt` and `headlineText` (`results.ts`), counted from today. `headlineAt` takes `playheadAt`'s reading (`{ index, atToday }`, the one rule for the header, the chart's readout and the ruler's flag) and `{ provisional, inForce }`; `inForce` is `!nothingInForce` of the document the numbers on show are from (`view.compare` is never read). It reads `months[i]`: the page overrides neither the month nor the effect. Line 1, the button and its name: `rows[i].completedNet` and the month ("47,335 homes by Apr 2040"), "0 homes by today" while the playhead is at the dataset date (no filled month needed). Line 2, the button's description: the effect, `planEffect[i].completedNetNew` as "+N from your policy", only while a policy is in force (below); then the goal, read through `src/lib/goal/goal.ts` (`GOAL`, `readGoal`, "68% of the goal" or "Goal reached, Mar 2029"), and its name ("State target: …") on hover and focus, opening under the box. It reads "calculating" while provisional and while month `i` of the view on show is not filled; while stale it shows the held view's numbers, dimmed. It depends on the playhead's calendar month (`index` and `atToday`), never the day within it, so it changes at most once per month the playhead crosses, and `readGoal` bounds its row reads. What drops at narrow widths: below sm line 2 (only line 1 shows); from lg to xl the goal part while the effect shows. The dropped parts stay in the description (an `sr-only` copy of line 2) and the live text; Results carries the target rows. There is no meter.
- **The chart row** (`ChartRow`) plots `rows[i].completedNet`, counted from today like the header, and its hover and `aria-valuetext` readout is `homesBy` (the header's wording, "1,234 homes by Apr 2040"). Its y axis starts at 0. The goal is a dashed step at `goalStep(GOAL, view)` (`src/lib/goal/goal.ts`): the homes still needed, `amount` minus the homes built before today, at the deadline's month, so the picture keeps its shape; the legend names the line `GOAL.name` ("State target"). With no step (`goalStep` null: the deadline's month is outside the window), the chart, its legend and its caption leave the goal out. **Results** has no headline. It says once, with the target rows (`fzp-built`, `builtTowardGoalText`), that the homes built from June 2022 count toward the goal; the target rows count from June 2022. The target rows follow the playhead to the goal's deadline month (`goalStep(GOAL, view).deadlineIndex`) and then hold there, as the header's goal part does: the goal's own row (the first) is `readGoal(GOAL, view, i)`, the same figure as the header's percentage, and the buffer row reads the same held month. The line under each row, "At the deadline (Jan 31, 2031): N%", shows only before the deadline month; from it on the row's own figure is the deadline's. The Results chart (`ScoreChart`) also counts from June 2022 and is named "Toward the goal, since Jun 2022", because the published estimates it is compared with count from their own start dates. "A forecast if nothing changes." shows in Sources only with nothing in force. The figure is named "Homes completed" (the visible label); its screen-reader caption says it counts from today, names the goal by `GOAL.name` while the step is drawn, and says that the month slider reads out each month, not the Results table (which counts from June 2022).
- **The comparison wording** (Results' "What … added", its comparison line and "because of" rows, and the header's effect) is decided on the page, once, in `TimelineMode`: `copy = scoreCopy(…)` from `nothingInForce(resultsDoc)`, the document the numbers on show were computed from (the held one while an edit is updating, so the wording never runs ahead of the numbers). Nothing in force: "current law … vs the old zoning", and the header has no effect part (the worker then compares the same document on the old zoning, `comparisonOf`). Otherwise: "your policy … vs current law" and "+N from your policy". The worker's `compare` (in its statics and `TimelineView`) is the plan side's own base, always `'plan'`, and is not used for wording.
- **Rule 32, as amended again (one clock).** "Updating" (the numbers are stale after an edit; its explanation opens under the slot) shows in the header's status slot at every width. It and the save state share one cell (`tl-status-group`) and "Updating" wins while both apply: the save state stays in the cell, hidden (`visibility: hidden`), so the cell is as wide as the wider of the two and nothing in the header, the map or the timeline moves as it comes or goes, with autosave on or off. The page's `status` snippet gets `covered` (true while "Updating" shows); SaveIndicator's `covered` makes the "Autosave off" button inert and closes its popover, and the chip comes back, popover working, when the update ends. SaveIndicator's own live region sets `visibility: visible`, so "Autosave off" and "Autosave on" are still announced, once, while covered (and not again when the cover lifts). Focus on the save state's button when the cover starts moves to "Updating"'s explanation; focus on that explanation when the update ends moves back to the save state's button if it shows, else to the header score. "Updating"'s part of the cell takes no presses while empty. There is no fill text and no line under the bar; the ruler's hatching shows what the person has not seen yet, not how far the forecast has filled. One `role="status"` live region (`tl-status-live`) speaks at every width, at most one change a second (`statusMs`, a test seam). Status is never placed among the buttons.
- **Explanations** (`Explain.svelte`) wait for hover, focus or tap; texts are in `copy.ts`. The `*4` sentences there duplicate `funding-copy.ts` texts that say "a run": `/lesscars` imports the original, so it is not edited.

## The worker messages as the page uses them

`TimelineClient` (`src/lib/sim/timeline-client.ts`) replaces `initScenario`, `reroll`, `setLevers`, `timeline`, `ranks` and `levers` on `/sim`; nothing on `/sim` sends them; the router and worker still route them (removal waits for the `/lesscars` cut-over). `TimelineSession` takes its own client from `createWorker()` (also what a crash restart uses).

- `open(manifest, doc, lots)` and `setPolicy(doc, lots)` return the rev the client used. `viewMonth(m)` goes through `MonthGate` (`playhead.ts`): at most four a second, only when the model month changes. `resyncMap()` is the map's `onResyncNeeded`. `retryChunks()` answers the "failed cells" notice.
- `compiled`, `filled` and `refill` are applied in arrival order into a `ChunkLog`; `rev` is never assumed to rise, and `compiled` and `refill` are never deduped by `rev` (one `refill` per acceptance; an edit taken up again after a later one was rejected gets a second `compiled` and `refill`). A `filled` chunk belongs to the statics of the last `compiled` before it. The view exists once `compiled` has arrived.
- `cells`, `lookups`, `heights` and `chunks` carry no rev and are forwarded as they come. The map's bars read the posted lookups, the card reads its own answer's.
- `error`: `bad-request` is the page's rule 34; any other code is rule 35. A crash is rule 36.

## The session's rules

- **Stale.** From an edit at rev R the page shows the held view (the one before the first unsettled edit), dimmed, with "Updating". An accepted edit ends it when a `refill` at rev >= R has arrived and the filled part reaches the playhead's month (`fill.ts`, `shownAt`). `compiled` never settles it: it precedes the `refill`. A `refill` at an older rev than the newest sent (an edit taken up again after a later one was rejected) puts the page back in stale; pending is left alone.
- **Rejection.** A `bad-request` (rule 34, "That change couldn't be applied.") undoes the store to the newest kept document not answered `bad-request`. The session keeps every document sent since the last rev answered by a `refill`, that rev's included (`#kept`); an older rev's `bad-request` can follow a newer one. After a rejection the page stays stale until `filledThrough` is back to the held view's and passes the playhead (`floor` in `EditState`).
- **Play** is never held back. Any edit pauses playback and the playhead does not move; Play works at once and the playhead waits at the computed edge. While an edit has no refill yet, Play runs on the held numbers, dimmed, with "Updating" in the header.
- **The provisional open (OQ-3).** The worker resolves brushes only after its dataset loads, so an `open` cannot carry the lots of a restored document with painted or transit policies. With `lots` null the session opens the document without those zoning policies (`provisionalOf`), then sends the full pair as its next `setPolicy`, as part of opening, not an edit. Until a `refill` at that rev or later has come and the window is whole, `provisional` is true: the headline stays "calculating" and the results stay stale. With the dataset loaded and lots resolved before the first open, one open carries the full document.
- **The carry-6 restart.** If the full document stands rejected after a provisional open, the stripped document stays what the forecast shows. A crash restart or the rule 35 Retry then re-opens and immediately sends a `setPolicy` of the stripped pair, keeps `provisional`, and posts the full document again only after the user's next edit. Never undo to the stripped document: that would drop the user's brushes.
- **A restart** re-opens with the last pair posted to the client.
- **The card** asks again after an edit once a `filled` covers its day, and whenever the playhead's month changes.
- **A failed brush query.** When PolicySync settles an edit with a failed query (`failed`, lots null), the session drops the unsent edit (`dropUnsent`): no stale, and the forecast stays the last sent document's. A "Couldn't find lots" notice with Retry asks the failed queries again.

## Revealing time

- **Three zones.** Shown, from today to the playhead's day; seen, from the day to the furthest date reached; unseen, past it. They cover the chart's lines and the gap past them, the ruler's track and every outcome figure; never the policy bars, the goal step or its flag.
- **The rules** are `reveal.ts`, held in `TimelineMode` as `reveal`. Opening (and a Retry's reload with a new calibration) is `revealOpen(asOf)`: the furthest date is today, everything ahead unseen. Every day change goes through `moved`: the furthest date only grows, and an offered "Play from" goes.
- **An edit** (a new `policy`) calls `editedReveal` beside `session.edited()`, with the view on show and whether a policy is in force in the document it came from (`inForce`, never `view.compare`). A search already under way keeps what was seen first, so a second edit before the first is settled compares with the numbers the person saw.
- **The scan** runs only once a refill has answered the edit (`session.pending` false); before that the newest view is still the old one. It runs when the view or the pending state changes, never per frame or as the playhead moves, and `scanned` resumes from the month it stopped at, so a fill is compared once. It compares month by month, never past the furthest month reached. A month changed if any figure it draws differs (`monthChanged`: every row field, the current-law homes and approvals, tiers and money). The first changed month pulls the furthest date back to it, never behind the playhead. No change through the furthest month pulls nothing back. A rejected edit, or one whose lots fail, settles with the old view, so it pulls nothing back.
- **"Play from {year}"** (`playFromDay`: the first changed month's start, never before today) shows when the scan finds a change while not playing. Pressed, it moves the playhead there and plays, wherever that month is: before the playhead it plays the change again, after it it skips the seen months the edit left alone. It wins over Replay at the last day and goes when the playhead moves for any reason or another edit starts a search. A change found while playing offers nothing; a change past the furthest date reached is no pull-back, so Play stays plain.
- **The furthest date lasts for the visit.** It is never saved, published or put in the address; a reload starts at today.
- **Known limit.** Seen months past the computed edge (after a refill withdrew them) show no line until the fill reaches them again.

## Commit path and load notices

- **Commit-path reconcile.** Every store change passes `PolicyStore`'s `normalize`, which `SimPage` wires to `reconcilePolicy4(doc, calibration, asOf)`. The reconciled document is what is committed, sent and undone to. A programme or baseline scale an edit leaves without a source is dropped, with today's funding notice (`fundingNotices`, [sim-funding.md](sim-funding.md)). `spans.ts` is pure and keeps only the span structure valid, so the funding reconcile happens here. `TimelineSession` reconciles again before sending and warns once if that changed anything (it is a bug upstream).
- **Load.** `restoreSim4` and `openShared` (`session4.ts`, over `openSimDoc4` in `sim-doc4.ts`) reconcile and turn the report into today's notices in order, then the **"dates moved"** notice ("Some policy dates moved to fit this data."; one line per moved, trimmed or removed span, behind Details, `dates-moved`), then "upgraded" for a schema 2 or 3 document. The autosave is `sfhousing:sim:v2`; a `sfhousing:sim:v1` record is carried over once. Publish, `/s/<id>`, `/api/policies` and Remix are in [plan-sharing.md](plan-sharing.md).
- **`?seed=`** is dropped from the address with `replaceState` (`SimRoute.svelte`, `address.ts`), other parameters kept.

## Editing

- **Drafts.** An unfinished funding draft (`FundingDrafts`, in `SimPage`, outside the store) is discarded, with "An unfinished item was discarded.", when another policy is selected, "+" opens, the editor is closed or it unmounts. A change of span inside the same policy keeps it.
- **Funding** is hosted per policy: `FundingTab` with `only` (a baseline, one fund, or the terms), priced on the playhead's day.
- **Permitting** is `PermittingSection snapshot={false}`: no Save or "Revert to saved"; Revert returns the span's set to its preset's. The preset is the policy's: on a policy of several spans the preset select is disabled (`PRESET_PER_POLICY`, behind a tap). Custom timing keeps preset `observed` with the label `CUSTOM_TIMING_LABEL`.
- **Zoning.** A transit policy's stop types belong to the policy, its bands to the span. A stroke goes into the selected painted policy's span (else its selected span); with none selected a new painted policy is added from the playhead month and selected.
- **Edits in place on an offer** (an existing fund policy): the pool name (label); the kind of money; each money-in row (Once / Every year, amount, start, Until); adding and removing money-in rows; leverage (bank); programmes (add, edit, remove; the programme set is policy-wide, so the lens writes it to every span). **Debt terms (rate, term) are read-only**; end the policy and add a new one to change them (`TERMS_HINT`, behind a tap).

## Known limits

- **The early-edit guess.** The worker does not mark the rejection of an edit sent before the dataset loaded, so the page guesses. An early edit's rejection shows rule 35 ("The simulation couldn't load.") briefly, then flips to rule 34 ("That change couldn't be applied." plus the undo) when a post at an older rev arrives. Pressing Retry inside that window can leave the rule 35 notice up.
- **The restart rev.** `TimelineClient.restart` returns no rev, so the session assumes the client's next rev.
- **Debt terms.** A hand-edited /s link can still carry two sets of terms on one pool id (`parse4` checks only that every span names the same pool); world-score then prices the statements on month W's terms.
- **The painted restore.** A restore with painted or transit policies reaches the full document's first results at 3.66 s on the live city, against 1.83 s for an empty document. The worker resolves brushes only after its dataset loads, so the page opens the stripped document and then sends the full one, which costs a recompile. Accepted by main.
- **Calendar playhead, model-month spans.** The playhead counts calendar months while spans snap to model months, so a policy added at "January 2031" starts on 10 Dec 2030 (live data).
- **The engine's limits** (ruling 8), listed with their numbers in [ensemble-runner.md](ensemble-runner.md): worker memory about +394 MB with no copies and +526 MB with the month-0 copy (live city); the longest worker compile step about 52.6 ms; one cold massing of about 636 ms; planning with rich public money over the per-slice bar. The page shows no state for them.

## For the composer

The places the page works around the worker (`src/lib/timeline/session.svelte.ts`):

- The early-edit classification: `early: !this.#sawCompile` at `:447`, handled at `:546-552` (`#earlyRejected`, `#stop()`), undone by `#fillWentOn` when an older-rev post arrives. A marker on the worker's `bad-request` for an edit sent before the load would remove it.
- The restart rev: `this.#rev += 1` at `:467-469`. A rev returned by `TimelineClient.restart` would remove it.
- Calendar playhead, model-month spans: the playhead counts calendar months while spans snap to model months, so a policy added at "January 2031" starts on 10 Dec 2030 (live data).
- The "await lots" open: `provisionalOf` and the stripped open (`session.svelte.ts`, `open` and `#openWith`), then the full document as the next `setPolicy`. A worker that could take brush queries before its dataset loads, or an `openTimeline` that awaits lots, would remove the recompile (the painted restore above).
